Below is a source-grounded goal model. I use **AND** when subgoals/tasks are jointly required, and **FALLBACK** for low-battery or failure-triggered behavior, because the instructions define fallback for exception/threshold conditions rather than ordinary OR alternatives. Texto colado Texto colado

`R` = number of robots available/assigned, bounded by the number of required item/storage subtasks and the maximum robot availability. The mission explicitly allows either one robot or multiple robots assigned to parallel collect-deliver tasks. Texto colado

## Logical relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G6, G10 [AND][sequential: `G2;G6;G10`]**  
  **Justification:** The mission must first know what to collect and where, then collect resources, then deliver them. Delivery begins only once resources have been retrieved. Texto colado

- **G2 -> G3, G4, G5 [AND][sequential: `G3;G4;G5`]**  
  **Justification:** The robot must know the request and storage places before ordering storage visits, and robot assignment depends on whether multiple items/storages enable parallel tasks. Texto colado

- **G6 -> G7, G8, G9 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK((G7;G8),G9)`]**  
  **Justification:** Normal collection consists of reaching storage and obtaining the resource. If battery reaches 10% in collection, the fallback is returning to recharge and assigning the mission to another robot. Texto colado

- **G10 -> G11, G12 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G11,G12)`]**  
  **Justification:** Normal delivery is attempted first through as many runs as necessary. If battery reaches 30% during delivery, the fallback is checkpoint handover. Texto colado

- **G12 -> G13, G14 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G13,G14)`]**  
  **Justification:** The robot first tries to return the resource to a checkpoint and transfer the remaining task. If returning the resource fails, alert/report behavior is triggered. Texto colado

- **G5 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][parallel-enabled through robot range `[1,R]`]**  
  **Justification:** Multiple robots are not modeled as an OR alternative because the mission says they *can* be assigned to parallel collect-deliver tasks to reduce time, not that they are a separate alternative objective. Texto colado

No true **OR** decomposition was introduced because the mission does not describe mutually sufficient alternative ways to satisfy the same goal. The sterile equipment and clean linen cases are examples of goods/equipment, not separate alternative mission branches. Texto colado
