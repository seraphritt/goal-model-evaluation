- **G2 → G3, G4 [AND][parallel: `G3#G4`]**  
  **Justification:** Required resources and destination/requester information are both necessary, but the description does not impose an order, so they may be obtained in parallel.

- **G5 → G6, G7 [AND][parallel: `G6#G7`]**
