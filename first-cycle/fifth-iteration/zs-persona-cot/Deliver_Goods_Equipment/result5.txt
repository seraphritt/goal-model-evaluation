Below is a **goal-model decomposition** for the mission. Since no attached/embedded file was available in the conversation, I used the definitions provided in your prompt.

## Modeling rationale

The mission has a clear logistics objective: **collect required resources from storage and deliver them to the requesting agent at a specified location**. Therefore, the root is an **Achieve Goal**, because the system must make a target condition true: the requested resources are delivered.

The mission naturally separates into four reasoning layers:

1. **Understand the request**: identify required resources, requesting agent, and specified location.
2. **Plan and assign robots**: identify storage places, estimate waiting/path cost, order visits, and choose single or multi-robot execution.
3. **Execute collection and delivery**: collection must happen before delivery.
4. **Handle battery/failure contingencies**: low battery causes reassignment; failure to checkpoint causes alert and report.

I model battery-handling as **FALLBACK** because those goals are attempted only when the normal collection/delivery path cannot continue safely.
