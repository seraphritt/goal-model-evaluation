## Modeling assumptions

The mission is modeled per delivery request/order. Even if the robot carries multiple meals, each meal is tracked as a specific order for a specific inpatient.

Locations are restricted to locations or location-like sources mentioned in the description: **kitchen**, **inpatient room**, **room table**, **robot tray**, **room door**, and **inpatient record**.

Human helpers are not counted in “Number of Robots.” For example, “robot + nurse” means **1 robot** in the robot-count field.

Key monitored variables include: `order_id`, `meal_id`, `inpatient_id`, `room_id`, `tray_slot`, `retrieval_actor`, `retrieval_capability`, `companion_presence`, `nurse_availability`, `helper_robot_availability`, `door_opening_capability`, `retrieved_meal_id`, `retrieval_time`, `retrieval_location`, and `wrong_meal_detected`.

# Logical Relationships and Justifications

* **G1 -> G2, G26 [AND][G2#G26]**
  **Justification:** The mission includes two required capabilities: delivering food and retrieving dirty dishes. They are independent service flows and can occur in parallel for different rooms or requests.

* **G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G6, G9, G14, G23 [AND][G3;G4;G5;G6;G9;(G14#G23)]**
  **Justification:** A delivery cannot be completed unless the request is known, the meal is prepared, the robot reaches the room, access is obtained, retrieval feasibility is assessed, the meal is transferred, and the event is tracked. Transfer and accountability monitoring can happen in parallel once delivery begins.

* **G3 -> AT1 [AND]**
  **Justification:** Reading the kitchen request is the concrete operation needed to satisfy the query goal.

* **G4 -> AT2, AT3 [AND][AT2;AT3]**
  **Justification:** The meal must be physically loaded and logically associated with order/patient/tray information. Loading without identity binding is unsafe because the robot may carry multiple meals.

* **G5 -> AT4 [AND]**
  **Justification:** Moving from kitchen to inpatient room is the operational task that realizes the transport goal.

* **G6 -> G7, G8 [OR][FALLBACK(G7,G8)]**
  **Justification:** Door opening can involve either a human or another robot. Human opening is attempted first because it may require fewer robotic resources; robot cooperation is the fallback.

* **G7 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][AT5;AT6]**
  **Justification:** The robot must request human assistance and then use the opened doorway.

* **G8 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][AT7;AT8]**
  **Justification:** Robot-assisted door opening requires coordination with the helper robot and execution of the opening/holding action.

* **G9 -> G10, G11, G12, G13 [AND][G10#G11#G12#G13]**
  **Justification:** The feasibility of tray retrieval depends on several uncertain actors: inpatient, companion, nurse, and helper robot. These checks can be performed in parallel.

* **G14 -> G15, G22 [OR][FALLBACK(G15,G22)]**
  **Justification:** The mission provides two delivery modes: tray fetching or table placement. Because tray fetching depends on uncertain human/robot cooperation, table placement is modeled as a fallback requiring manipulation skill.

* **G15 -> G16, G17 [AND][G16;G17]**
  **Justification:** Tray handoff requires first indicating the correct meal and then having the meal retrieved.

* **G17 -> G18, G19, G20, G21 [OR][FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G18,G19),G20),G21)]**
  **Justification:** The meal may be retrieved by the inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot. Fallback reflects uncertainty in availability and capability.

* **G23 -> G24, G25 [AND][G24#G25]**
  **Justification:** Tracking retrieval and alerting on wrong-meal retrieval are both mandatory safety/accountability requirements. They can run in parallel during handoff.

* **G26 -> G27, G28, G31, G35 [AND][G27;G28;G31;G35]**
  **Justification:** Dirty dish retrieval requires a pickup request, room access, collection, and removal from the room.

* **G27 -> AT21, AT22 [AND][AT21#AT22]**
  **Justification:** The robot must receive the pickup call and, when provided, capture the caller’s door-opening signal. These pieces of information are obtained together.

* **G28 -> G29, G30 [OR][FALLBACK(G29,G30)]**
  **Justification:** The door can be opened by a person or by another robot. If the caller says they can open the door, human opening is attempted first.

* **G31 -> G32, G33, G34 [OR][FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G32,G33),G34)]**
  **Justification:** Dish retrieval can occur unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human. The fallback order starts with the lowest robot-team complexity and escalates if needed.

* **G35 -> AT31 [AND]**
  **Justification:** The dirty dishes are only retrieved from the room when they are physically removed from it.
