## Design interpretation

The mission has two main service obligations: food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are modeled as parallel service capabilities because a kitchen delivery request and a dish pickup call can occur independently. Inside a single food-delivery order, however, the main flow is sequential: receive/request context, transport food, open the door, hand over the food, then track/alert. The uncertain human availability and capability conditions are represented as query goals and as fallback structures.
