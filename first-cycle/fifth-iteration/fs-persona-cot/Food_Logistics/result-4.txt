## Modeling rationale

I modeled the mission as two required service capabilities: **meal delivery** and **dirty-dish retrieval**. They are both required by the description, so the root goal uses an **AND decomposition**. They can occur independently across different requests or rooms, so the runtime at the root is **parallel**.

I used **FALLBACK** where the description mentions uncertainty, unavailability, or alternative cooperation modes. Per your definition, children involved in `FALLBACK(...)` are marked as **AND**, even when they are semantically alternatives, because fallback means “try one; if it fails, try the next.”

I used **OR** only where either option is sufficient without needing a strict fallback order: companion retrieval or nurse retrieval.

Robot counts count only robots. Humans such as inpatients, companions, visitors, nurses, or someone in the room are cooperating external agents, not included in the robot number.

# Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G20 [AND][parallel: `G2#G20`]**
  **Justification:** The system must support both meal delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are separate required capabilities and can happen independently across service requests.

* **G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G9, G12, G19 [AND][sequential/parallel: `G3;(G4#G5);G9;(G12#G19)`]**
  **Justification:** A delivery requires the kitchen request first. Meal loading and support-context analysis can occur in parallel. Room access must precede transfer. Tracking and validation must run during the transfer.

* **G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2`]**
  **Justification:** The robot must receive the kitchen request before extracting the operational order details.

* **G4 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][sequential: `AT3;AT4`]**
  **Justification:** Meals must be collected before being loaded onto the robot tray.

* **G5 -> G6, G7, G8 [AND][parallel: `(G6#G7)#G8`]**
  **Justification:** Patient ability, helper availability, and uncertainty all influence whether tray retrieval is safe or feasible.

* **G9 -> G10, G11 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G10,G11)`]**
  **Justification:** Human door opening can be attempted first; if it is unavailable or fails, another robot can cooperate.

* **G12 -> G13, G14, G15, G18 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G13,FALLBACK(G14,FALLBACK(G15,G18)))`]**
  **Justification:** The meal can be placed on the table or fetched from the tray. Because the description emphasizes patient inability, helper unavailability, and uncertainty, fallback is more appropriate than a simple OR.

* **G15 -> G16, G17 [OR][parallel: `G16#G17`]**
  **Justification:** A companion or a nurse is sufficient as a human helper; both do not need to retrieve the same meal.

* **G19 -> AT25, AT26 [AND][parallel: `AT25#AT26`]**
  **Justification:** Logging retrieval and detecting wrong-meal retrieval should occur together during delivery completion.

* **G20 -> G21, G22, G23, G24, G27, G31 [AND][sequential/parallel: `G21;(G22#G23);G24;G27;G31`]**
  **Justification:** Dish pickup starts with a call, then support conditions are assessed, access is obtained, dishes are retrieved, and completion is confirmed.

* **G24 -> G25, G26 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G25,G26)`]**
  **Justification:** If the person in the room can open the door, human-assisted access is used; otherwise another robot can assist.

* **G27 -> G28, G29, G30 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G28,FALLBACK(G29,G30))`]**
  **Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval can be unassisted, two-robot assisted, or robot-human assisted. Fallback captures the progression from least coordination to more cooperative modes.
