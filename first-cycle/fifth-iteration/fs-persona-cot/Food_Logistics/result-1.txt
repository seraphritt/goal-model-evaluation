Below is a goal-model decomposition for the multi-robot inpatient food-delivery mission. I treat **food delivery** and **dirty-dish retrieval** as two top-level service capabilities that may run in parallel for different rooms, while each individual request is handled in a structured sequence.

I use **FALLBACK** when the description implies uncertainty or possible unavailability, such as patient capability, nurse/companion availability, manipulation skill availability, door-opening support, or cooperation mode.

## Step-by-step modeling rationale

1. The mission has two main objectives: **deliver meals** and **retrieve dirty dishes**.
2. Meal delivery is **order-by-order**, so each delivery request follows a sequential flow: receive request, prepare tray, assess assistance, access room, transfer meal.
3. Meal transfer has two alternatives: **robot places food on the room table** or **someone/another robot retrieves it from the tray**.
4. Tray retrieval requires cooperation and is uncertain, so inpatient, companion, nurse, and another robot are modeled as fallback options.
5. Because the robot may carry multiple meals, the model includes meal identification, retrieval tracking, and wrong-meal alerting.
6. Dish retrieval has three modes: unassisted, two-robot cooperation, or robot-human cooperation.
7. Door opening is modeled separately because both delivery and dish retrieval may require cooperation with either a human or another robot.
