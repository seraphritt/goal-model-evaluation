## 1. Modeling basis and assumptions

The model represents a **single hospital food-service lifecycle**: a kitchen delivery request is handled, the food is delivered to an inpatient room, and dirty dishes are later retrieved when needed. The mission explicitly states that the system delivers food from the **kitchen** to an **inpatient room**, that delivery is triggered by **kitchen delivery requests**, and that dirty dishes should be retrieved from the room. 

I use **AND** when all child elements are required to satisfy the parent, and **OR/FALLBACK** when alternative ways exist to satisfy the parent. This follows the provided definition of AND/OR decomposition.  Runtime annotations use `;` for sequence, `#` for parallelism, and `FALLBACK(...)` for ordered alternatives. 

Robot counts refer only to robots. Human collaborators, such as an inpatient, companion, or nurse, are described in the task text but are not counted as robots.

## 5. Logical relationships with justifications

**G1 -> G2, G19 [AND][sequential: `G2;G19`]**

* **Justification:** The mission requires both delivery of food and retrieval of dirty dishes. For one meal lifecycle, food delivery must occur before dishes become dirty and ready for pickup. 

**G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G8 [AND][sequential: `G3;G4;G5;G8`]**

* **Justification:** A delivery request must first be understood, meals must then be prepared and transported, room access must be obtained, and finally the handoff must be completed.

**G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2`]**

* **Justification:** The robot must receive the kitchen request before checking the relevant inpatient and room-context information.

**G4 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][sequential: `AT3;AT4`]**

* **Justification:** Meals must be loaded before they can be transported from the kitchen to the inpatient room.

**G5 -> G6, G7 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G6,G7)`]**

* **Justification:** Door opening can involve either a human or another robot. Fallback is appropriate because one helper may be unavailable.

**G6 -> AT5 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Human-assisted door opening is operationalized by requesting or coordinating a human to open the door.

**G7 -> AT6 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Robot-assisted door opening requires another robot, so the task uses two robots.

**G8 -> G10, G9 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G10,G9)`]**

* **Justification:** The food can be fetched from the robot tray or placed on the room table. Tray retrieval depends on cooperation and is uncertain; table placement requires a manipulation-capable robot.

**G9 -> AT7 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Table delivery requires the concrete manipulation task of placing food on the room table.

**G10 -> G11, G16, G17, G18 [AND][sequential + parallel: `G11;(G16#G17#G18)`]**

* **Justification:** A cooperating retriever must be secured first. During retrieval, the robot can simultaneously indicate the correct meal, track the retrieval, and monitor for wrong-meal events.

**G11 -> G12, G13, G14, G15 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G12,G13),G14),G15)`]**

* **Justification:** The possible retrievers are inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot. Fallback captures uncertainty about patient ability and human availability.

**G12 -> AT8 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If the inpatient can cooperate, the operational task is to enable the inpatient to retrieve the meal.

**G13 -> AT9 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If the companion is present and able, the operational task is to enable companion retrieval.

**G14 -> AT10 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If a nurse is available, the operational task is to request nurse retrieval.

**G15 -> AT11 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If human retrieval is not feasible, another robot can cooperate in tray retrieval.

**G16 -> AT12 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Because the robot can carry multiple meals, it must indicate which one belongs to the given inpatient.

**G17 -> AT13 [AND]**

* **Justification:** The mission requires tracking when and where each meal was retrieved.

**G18 -> AT14 [AND]**

* **Justification:** The mission requires an alert when a wrong meal is retrieved.

**G19 -> G20, G21, G24 [AND][sequential: `G20;G21;G24`]**

* **Justification:** The robot must first receive or understand the pickup request, then obtain room access, then retrieve the dishes.

**G20 -> AT15 [AND]**

* **Justification:** A person in the room can call the robot and indicate whether they can open the door.

**G21 -> G22, G23 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G22,G23)`]**

* **Justification:** Door access for dish retrieval can be human-assisted or robot-assisted.

**G22 -> AT16 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Human door opening for dish pickup is operationalized by requesting the person to open the door.

**G23 -> AT17 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Robot door opening for dish pickup requires cooperation with a second robot.

**G24 -> G25, G26, G27 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G25,G26),G27)`]**

* **Justification:** Dish retrieval has three possible modes: unassisted, two-robot cooperation, or robot-human cooperation.

**G26 -> AT19 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Two-robot dish pickup requires exactly two robots.

**G27 -> AT20 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Robot-human dish pickup requires one robot cooperating with a human.
