## 4. Logical relationships and justifications

**G1 → G2, G17 [AND] [sequential: `G2;G17`]**
**Justification:** The system must support both meal delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. Delivery logically occurs before dish retrieval in the meal-service lifecycle because dishes become dirty after food delivery.

**G2 → G3, G4, G5, G8, G9, G16 [AND] [sequential: `G3;G4;G5;G8;G9;G16`]**
**Justification:** A food delivery mission needs request information, physical transportation, room access, delivery-mode assessment, actual delivery, and completion confirmation.

**G3 → AT1 [AND]**
**Justification:** Obtaining the kitchen request is necessary because the mission is triggered by kitchen delivery requests on an order-by-order basis.

**G4 → AT2, AT3 [AND] [sequential: `AT2;AT3`]**
**Justification:** The robot must first load the requested meal or meals, then navigate toward the inpatient room.

**G5 → G6, G7 [AND] [fallback: `FALLBACK(G6,G7)`]**
**Justification:** Door opening may be supported by a human or another robot. Fallback captures the fact that the first helper type may be unavailable.

**G6 → AT4 [AND]**
**Justification:** A human-opening-door strategy is operationalized by requesting human cooperation at the room door.

**G7 → AT5 [AND]**
**Justification:** A robot-opening-door strategy is operationalized by coordinating with another robot.

**G8 → AT6, AT7, AT8 [AND] [parallel: `AT6#AT7#AT8`]**
**Justification:** Patient ability, human availability, and manipulation capability are independent information checks and can be performed in parallel.

**G9 → G10, G11 [AND] [fallback: `FALLBACK(G10,G11)`]**
**Justification:** The mission allows either table placement or tray retrieval. Fallback is appropriate because table placement depends on a special manipulation skill, while tray retrieval depends on cooperation.
