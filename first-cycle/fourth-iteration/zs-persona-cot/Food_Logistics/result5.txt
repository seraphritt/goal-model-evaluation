I use the provided definition of a goal model as a hierarchical tree with AND/OR decompositions, where AND requires all children and OR requires only one child; runtime annotations use sequential `;`, parallel `#`, and `FALLBACK(...)`.  

## 1. Main interpretation

The mission has two major service objectives: deliver ordered food from the kitchen to an inpatient room, then retrieve dirty dishes from the room. Delivery is order-by-order, may involve multiple meals, may require door-opening cooperation, and has several handoff alternatives: robot table placement, inpatient tray retrieval, companion/nurse tray retrieval, or another robot’s help. The dish retrieval mission also has alternatives: unassisted retrieval, two-robot cooperation, or robot-human cooperation. 

The key uncertainty is whether the inpatient, companion, or nurse can retrieve the food from the robot tray. That motivates query goals and fallback structures.

# 5. Logical relationships

**G1 -> G2, G13 [AND][sequential: `G2;G13`]**
Justification: The overall service requires meal delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. In a meal-service cycle, delivery must occur before dish pickup. 

**G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G6 [AND][sequential: `G3;G4;G5;G6`]**
Justification: The robot must first know the order/context, then transport the meal, then access the room, then complete the handoff.

**G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2`]**
Justification: The kitchen request identifies the order; the inpatient record/context determines who may be able to retrieve the meal.

**G4 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][sequential: `AT3;AT4`]**
Justification: The robot must load the meal before navigating to the inpatient room.

**G5 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][FALLBACK: `FALLBACK(AT5,AT6)`]**
Justification: The room door may be opened through human cooperation or another robot. Fallback captures the fact that one option may not be available.

**G6 -> G7, G12 [AND][sequential: `G7;G12`]**
Justification: The meal must first be transferred, then the system must ensure the retrieval is recorded and wrong-meal events are handled.

**G7 -> G8, G9, G10, G11 [AND][FALLBACK: `FALLBACK(G8,FALLBACK(G9,FALLBACK(G10,G11)))`]**
Justification: The mission gives several transfer alternatives. Fallback is justified because patient/helper availability and manipulation capability are uncertain.

**G8 -> AT7 [AND][sequential: `AT7`]**
Justification: Table placement is a single concrete robot manipulation task.

**G9 -> AT8, AT9 [AND][sequential: `AT8;AT9`]**
Justification: If the inpatient retrieves from the tray, the robot should first indicate the correct meal, then allow retrieval.

**G10 -> AT10, AT11, AT12 [AND][sequential + fallback: `AT10;FALLBACK(AT11,AT12)`]**
Justification: The robot must indicate the correct meal before a companion or nurse retrieves it. Companion retrieval is tried if available; nurse retrieval is the fallback.

**G11 -> AT13 [AND][sequential: `AT13`]**
Justification: Robot-helper tray retrieval is represented as one cooperative robot task.

**G12 -> AT14, AT15 [AND][parallel: `AT14#AT15`]**
Justification: Tracking retrieval and monitoring for wrong-meal retrieval can happen at the same time during handoff.

**G13 -> G14, G15, G16, G17 [AND][sequential: `G14;G15;G16;G17`]**
Justification: Dish retrieval requires pickup context, navigation to the room, access through the door, and collection of dishes.

**G14 -> AT16, AT17 [AND][sequential: `AT16;AT17`]**
Justification: The room caller initiates pickup and can also provide door-opening capability information.

**G15 -> AT18 [AND][sequential: `AT18`]**
Justification: The robot must go to the room before retrieving dishes.

**G16 -> AT19, AT20 [AND][FALLBACK: `FALLBACK(AT19,AT20)`]**
Justification: Door access may be supported by a human or another robot.

**G17 -> AT21, AT22, AT23, AT24 [AND][fallback + sequential: `FALLBACK(AT21,FALLBACK(AT22,AT23));AT24`]**
Justification: Dish retrieval can be unassisted, done by two robots, or done by robot-human cooperation. After one retrieval mode succeeds, the dishes are removed from the room.
