Below is a goal-model decomposition for the mission. I treat the mission as two main service capabilities: food delivery from the kitchen to an inpatient room, and dirty-dish retrieval from the room. This follows the description that delivery is request-driven by the kitchen, meal handoff may require robot-human or robot-robot cooperation, wrong-meal retrieval must be detected, and dirty dishes must also be retrieved from rooms. 

## 1. Modeling assumptions and interpretation

The model uses **AND** when all children are required to satisfy the parent, **OR** when one child is enough, and **FALLBACK** when one alternative is tried first and another is attempted only if the first fails. This follows the provided definitions of AND/OR decomposition and runtime annotations. 

Because the mission states that patient, companion, or nurse availability is uncertain, the meal handoff is modeled with fallback alternatives rather than a simple OR choice. The robot may first try tray retrieval with an available collaborator, but if that fails, it may fall back to direct table placement using a manipulation-capable robot. 

Locations are limited to locations explicitly mentioned in the mission: **kitchen**, **inpatient room**, **room table**, **robot tray**, and **room door**.

# 5. Logical Relationships

## Top-Level Mission

**G1 -> G2, G20 [AND][parallel: `G2#G20`]**
**Justification:** The system must support both kitchen-requested food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. These services are independent and may be active for different rooms or orders at the same time.

## Food Delivery

**G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G6, G7, G10 [AND][sequential/parallel: `G3;(G4#G5);G6;G7;G10`]**
**Justification:** The robot must first obtain the request. Loading meals and checking tray-retrieval feasibility can occur in parallel after the request is known. Then the robot reaches the room, gains door access, and completes the meal transfer.

**G3 -> AT1 [AND]**
**Justification:** Querying the kitchen delivery request is operationalized by reading or receiving the kitchen order.

**G4 -> AT2 [AND]**
**Justification:** The goal of having meals on the robot tray is fulfilled by loading the requested meals at the kitchen.

**G5 -> AT3 [AND]**
**Justification:** The robot must check patient and companion-related information because tray retrieval depends on uncertain human availability and capability.

**G6 -> AT4 [AND]**
**Justification:** The robot must physically navigate to the inpatient room to deliver the order.

**G7 -> G8, G9 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G8,G9)`]**
**Justification:** Door opening may involve a human or another robot. Since one cooperation mode may fail, the second is modeled as fallback.

**G8 -> AT5 [AND]**
**Justification:** A human-door-opening strategy is operationalized by requesting or coordinating with a human at the room door.

**G9 -> AT6 [AND]**
**Justification:** A robot-door-opening strategy requires cooperation with another robot, so two robots are needed.

**G10 -> G11, G19 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G11,G19)`]**
**Justification:** The meal can be delivered by tray handoff or by direct table placement. Because tray handoff depends on uncertain human or robot cooperation, table placement is modeled as a fallback.

**G11 -> G12, G13, G18 [AND][sequential: `G12;G13;G18`]**
**Justification:** Tray handoff requires first indicating the correct meal, then coordinating retrieval, then verifying and recording the retrieval.

**G12 -> AT7 [AND]**
**Justification:** The robot indicates which meal should be retrieved from the tray.

**G13 -> G14, G15, G16, G17 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G14,G15),G16),G17)`]**
**Justification:** Tray retrieval may be done by the inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot. Because availability and ability are uncertain, these are fallback alternatives rather than simple simultaneous alternatives.

**G14 -> AT8 [AND]**
**Justification:** If the inpatient can fetch the meal, the robot prompts the inpatient to retrieve the correct tray item.

**G15 -> AT9 [AND]**
**Justification:** If the companion is present and able, the robot prompts the companion to retrieve the meal.

**G16 -> AT10 [AND]**
**Justification:** If a nurse is available, the robot prompts the nurse to retrieve the meal.

**G17 -> AT11 [AND]**
**Justification:** If human tray retrieval is not feasible, another robot can cooperate in retrieving the meal from the tray.

**G18 -> AT12, AT13 [AND]**
**Justification:** The system must track when and where each meal is retrieved and alert if the wrong meal is retrieved. Both monitoring/recording and wrong-meal alerting are required safety/accountability functions.

**G19 -> AT14 [AND]**
**Justification:** Direct table delivery is fulfilled by placing the food on the room table using the required manipulation skill.

## Dirty-Dish Retrieval

**G20 -> G21, G22, G23, G26 [AND][sequential: `G21;G22;G23;G26`]**
**Justification:** Dish retrieval starts with a room pickup request, then the robot reaches the room door, gains access, and removes the dirty dishes.

**G21 -> AT15 [AND]**
**Justification:** The robot receives the pickup call and the caller’s door-opening signal.

**G22 -> AT16 [AND]**
**Justification:** The robot must move to the correct room door before pickup.

**G23 -> G24, G25 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G24,G25)`]**
**Justification:** Door opening can be performed with a human or another robot. The human option is tried when the caller signaled ability to open the door; otherwise, robot cooperation is used as fallback.

**G24 -> AT17 [AND]**
**Justification:** Human door opening requires coordination with the person in the room.

**G25 -> AT18 [AND]**
**Justification:** Robot-based door opening requires another robot, so two robots cooperate.

**G26 -> G27, G28, G29 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G27,G28),G29)`]**
**Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval can occur unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human. Fallback reflects escalation from simpler to more cooperative execution.

**G27 -> AT19 [AND]**
**Justification:** If one robot can retrieve the dishes alone, unassisted pickup satisfies the goal.

**G28 -> AT20 [AND]**
**Justification:** If unassisted pickup fails, two-robot cooperation is available.

**G29 -> AT21 [AND]**
**Justification:** If robot-only pickup is insufficient, the mission allows cooperation between robots and a human.
