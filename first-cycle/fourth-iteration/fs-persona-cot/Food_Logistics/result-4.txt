## 4. Logical Relationships and Justifications

**G1 -> G2, G18 [AND][parallel: `G2#G18`]**
Food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval are both required system capabilities. They are modeled in parallel because the mission describes them as service capabilities that may be triggered independently.

**G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G8, G17 [AND][sequential plus parallel monitoring: `G3;G4;G5;(G8#G17)`]**
A delivery first needs request information, then physical transport, then room access. Meal transfer and traceability/safety monitoring are parallel because the robot must monitor identity and possible wrong retrieval while the transfer occurs.

**G3 -> AT1, AT2, AT3 [AND][sequential/parallel: `AT1;(AT2#AT3)`]**
The kitchen request must be received first. Once received, the robot can identify the room and map meals to intended patients/tray slots in parallel.

**G4 -> AT4, AT5 [AND][sequential: `AT4;AT5`]**
The robot must load the meals before it can navigate to the inpatient room.

**G5 -> G6, G7 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G6,G7)`]**
Door opening may be handled by human cooperation or robot cooperation. Fallback is used because availability of a human helper is uncertain.

**G8 -> G9, G10, G11, G16 [AND][query then fallback: `G9;FALLBACK(G10,FALLBACK(G11,G16))`]**
The robot first queries retrieval feasibility. It then attempts table placement if manipulation is available, otherwise person-assisted tray retrieval, otherwise robot-assisted tray retrieval.

**G11 -> G12, G13, G14, G15 [AND][sequential/fallback: `G12;FALLBACK(G13,FALLBACK(G14,G15))`]**
When a person retrieves food, the robot must first indicate the correct meal. The inpatient, companion, and nurse are then modeled as fallback alternatives because capability and availability are uncertain.

**G17 -> AT18, AT19, AT20 [AND][sequential plus parallel: `AT18;(AT19#AT20)`]**
The robot monitors meal identity, records time/location, and triggers an alert if the wrong meal is taken.

**G18 -> G19, G20, G23, G27 [AND][sequential: `G19;G20;G23;G27`]**
Dirty-dish retrieval requires receiving the pickup context, obtaining room access, collecting dishes, and completing removal from the room.

**G20 -> G21, G22 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G21,G22)`]**
For dish pickup, the caller may open the door. If human opening fails or is unavailable, another robot can cooperate.

**G23 -> G24, G25, G26 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G24,FALLBACK(G25,G26))`]**
Dirty dishes may be retrieved unassisted, by two robots, or by robots and a human. Fallback captures escalation from the simplest mode to more cooperative modes.

**G27 -> AT31 [AND][single task]**
The final dish-retrieval condition is that dirty dishes are removed from the inpatient room. The mission does not specify a destination beyond retrieval from the room, so the model does not invent one.
