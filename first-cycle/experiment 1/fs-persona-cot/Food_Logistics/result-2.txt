### Task Model

| **Name** | **Text** | **Relation** | **Location** | **# Robots** | **Justification** |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| AT1: Pick up Meals from Kitchen | Robot collects the ordered meals from the kitchen storage. | **AND** (under G3.1 & G3.2) | kitchen | 1 | Only one robot is needed to gather meals. |
| AT2: Open Door Robot Alone | Robot uses its built‑in mechanism to open the patient room door. | **OR** (under G5) | patient room door | 1 | Robot may be able to open the door without assistance. |
| AT3: Deliver to Table | Robot places the meal onto the patient’s table. | **AND** (under G3.1) | patient room table | 1 | Direct delivery to the table requires the robot’s manipulation skill. |
| AT4: Deliver to Tray | Robot places the meal onto the patient’s tray. | **AND** (under G3.2) | patient room tray | 1 | Tray placement is the alternative to table delivery. |
| AT5: Request Human Retrieval | Robot signals the patient or companion to fetch the meal from the tray. | **AND** (under G3.2) | patient room | 1 | Initiates the cooperation needed for tray delivery. |
| AT6: Alert Wrong Meal | Robot alerts staff if an incorrect meal is retrieved. | **AND** (under G3.2 & G6) | patient room | 1 | Ensures the system corrects mistakes. |
| AT7: Retrieve Dirty Dishes | Robot collects and disposes of dirty dishes. | **AND** (under G4) | patient room | [1,2] | Dish retrieval may be done alone or with another robot. |
| AT8: Open Door with Human | Human opens the patient room door while the robot assists. | **OR** (under G5) | patient room door | 1 (robot) + human | Used if the robot cannot open the door by itself. |
| AT9: Open Door with Robot Cooperation | Second robot cooperates to open the door. | **OR** (under G5) | patient room door | 2 | Two robots can open a heavy door. |
| AT10: Monitor Retrieval | Robot observes the patient or companion picking up the meal. | **AND** (under G6) | patient room | 1 | Monitoring is required for tracking. |
| AT11: Record Meal Retrieval | Robot logs the time, location, and patient for each meal retrieved. | **AND** (under G6) | internal system | 1 | Tracking information is needed for audit and correctness. |
| AT12: Indicate Meal to Patient | Robot visually or verbally indicates which meal the patient should take. | **AND** (under G6) | patient room | 1 | Helps patients identify the correct meal. |

### How the Model Meets the Mission Description

1. **Order‑by‑order delivery** – G1’s sequential runtime ensures each order is processed completely before the next begins.  
2. **Special manipulation vs. tray delivery** – G3.1 models the robot‑only table delivery that uses the required manipulation skill. G3.2 models tray delivery that needs human/robot cooperation.  
3. **Uncertainty in patient capability** – G2 queries patient capability and companion presence; G3’s fallback uses this information to decide delivery mode.  
4. **Cooperative retrieval & monitoring** – G6 handles monitoring, indication, and error alert, while AT5 initiates cooperation with patient/companion.  
5. **Dish retrieval** – G4 (AT7) covers dirty dish removal, with optional cooperation.  
6. **Door opening** – G5 encapsulates all door‑opening possibilities, ensuring the robot can enter regardless of the patient’s assistance level.  
7. **Tracking & correctness** – AT11 (record) and AT6 (alert) maintain the system’s knowledge of correct meal delivery.  

This goal–task decomposition captures all functional requirements, handles uncertainty, and explicitly models multi‑robot cooperation, providing a clear blueprint for implementation.
