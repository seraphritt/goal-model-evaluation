### Logical Relationships (Tree Structure)

| **Parent** | **Children** | **Relation** | **Runtime** | **Justification** |
|------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| G1 | G2 | AND | `;` | The root goal must first spawn the parallel collect‑deliver tasks; the two phases (collection and delivery) are sequentially linked. |
| G2 | G2.1 , G2.2 | AND | `#` | Multiple items are processed in parallel by different robots, as stated in the description. |
| G2.1 | G2.1.1 , G2.1.2 | AND | `FALLBACK(G2.1.1 , G2.1.2)` | The robot may either run the normal sequence or need to handle a battery‑low situation; the fallback captures this binary choice. |
| G2.1.1 | AT1–AT7 | AND | `;` | The normal sequence of planning, navigating, requesting, waiting, picking, navigating to delivery, and delivering must be executed in order. |
| G2.1.2 | AT8–AT11 | AND | `;` | Battery‑recovery actions (checking, recharging, re‑assigning, reporting) are performed sequentially once a low‑battery condition is detected. |
| G2.2 | G2.2.1 , G2.2.2 | AND | `FALLBACK(G2.2.1 , G2.2.2)` | Same as G2.1 but for the second item. |
| G2.2.1 | AT12–AT18 | AND | `;` | Same normal sequence for Item 2. |
| G2.2.2 | AT19–AT22 | AND | `;` | Same battery‑recovery sequence for Item 2. |

**Overall Rationale**

- **Sequential vs. Parallel** – Collection and delivery of a *single* resource must occur in order (sequential). When *multiple* resources are required, the mission can dispatch separate robots concurrently (parallel).  
- **Fallback** – Battery thresholds create two distinct failure modes; each is handled by a dedicated subgoal, and a fallback structure ensures the mission proceeds to the appropriate recovery behavior.  
- **AND / OR** – All subgoals listed under a parent must be satisfied for the parent to succeed (AND). Fallback is a binary AND with a runtime operator that selects one of two alternatives.  

This goal model faithfully reflects the mission description and provides a clear decomposition suitable for multi‑robot task allocation and execution.
