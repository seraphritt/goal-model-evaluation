## 4. Logical relationships

* **G1 → G2, G9 [AND][sequential: `G2;G9`]**

  * **Justification:** completing the overall meal-service mission requires both delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. Dish retrieval is placed after delivery because dishes become relevant after the meal-service phase.  

* **G2 → AT1, AT2, G3, G4 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2;G3;G4`]**

  * **Justification:** an order must first be received, the food transported to the room, room access obtained when required, and then the meal transferred to the intended recipient. This corresponds directly to an order-by-order kitchen-to-room delivery. 

* **G3 → AT3, AT4 [AND][`FALLBACK(AT3,AT4)`]**

  * **Justification:** door opening may use human or robot cooperation. Only one successful mechanism is operationally necessary, but the supplied FALLBACK definition requires the alternatives to be connected through an AND decomposition. 

* **G4 → AT5, G5 [AND][`FALLBACK(AT5,G5)`]**

  * **Justification:** food can either be put on the room table using the special manipulation skill or fetched from the tray through cooperation. The cooperative branch provides an alternative if direct table placement cannot be performed. 

* **G5 → G6, AT6, G7, G8 [AND][sequential: `G6;AT6;G7;G8`]**

  * **Justification:** first establish who may be capable of assisting, then identify the correct meal, conduct the cooperative retrieval, and finally ensure that retrieval is recorded and validated. This sequence addresses the uncertainty and multiple-meal constraints in the description. 

* **G7 → AT7, AT8, AT9, AT10 [AND][nested FALLBACK]**

  * **Runtime:** `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(AT7,AT8),AT9),AT10)`
  * **Justification:** the permitted cooperating actors are explicitly the inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot. Because a patient or human assistant can be unavailable or incapable and the information is uncertain, a fallback structure provides robustness. 

* **G8 → AT11, AT12 [AND][parallel: `AT11#AT12`]**

  * **Justification:** retrieval tracking and wrong-meal monitoring are both required and can operate concurrently during the same handoff. 

* **G9 → G10, G11 [AND][sequential: `G10;G11`]**

  * **Justification:** when access is needed, the robot must first obtain access to the room and then retrieve its dirty dishes. 

* **G10 → AT13, AT14 [AND][`FALLBACK(AT13,AT14)`]**

  * **Justification:** door opening can be achieved through a human or another robot. If someone in the room called for pickup, that person can signal whether human door assistance is available. 

* **G11 → AT15, AT16, AT17 [AND][nested FALLBACK]**

  * **Runtime:** `FALLBACK(FALLBACK(AT15,AT16),AT17)`
  * **Justification:** dish retrieval may occur unassisted, with two robots, or through robot-human cooperation. The nested fallback represents changing retrieval method if the preceding method cannot accomplish the pickup.

A significant feature of this model is the uncertainty around human tray-retrieval capability: the inpatient record and companion-presence information can inform the decision, but the description explicitly says that information is uncertain. Therefore, **G6 provides knowledge for selection while G7 retains runtime fallback mechanisms** rather than assuming that the recorded information will always be correct.
