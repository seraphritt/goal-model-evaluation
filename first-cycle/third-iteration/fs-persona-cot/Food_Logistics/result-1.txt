Below is a goal-model decomposition grounded in the pasted mission. I treat the top-level mission as an **Achieve** goal because the system is expected to reach concrete mission states: meals delivered, meal retrieval tracked, wrong-meal retrieval alerted, and dirty dishes retrieved. The source defines a goal model as a hierarchical tree of goals/tasks with AND/OR decompositions, and requires runtime annotations for goals.  

## Modeling assumptions

The mission gives alternatives, but not always a strict preference order. I therefore use **FALLBACK** where the text describes uncertainty, inability, or unavailability, such as patients being unable to fetch food and companions or nurses not being available. 

For delivery, I model this fallback order:

`table placement by manipulation robot → human tray retrieval → robot tray retrieval`

For human tray retrieval, I model:

`inpatient → companion → nurse`

This is a design choice: the fallback order reflects increasing need for external assistance, not an explicitly stated priority in the source.

# 4. Logical relationships

**G1 → G2, G5, G17, G22 [AND] [`G2;(G5#G17);G22`]**
**Justification:** The full mission needs context acquisition, food delivery, correctness/traceability, and dirty-dish retrieval. Delivery and correctness monitoring should overlap because tracking and wrong-meal detection occur during retrieval. 

**G2 → G3, G4 [AND] [`G3;G4`]**
**Justification:** The kitchen request identifies the order and destination; retrieval capability depends on the inpatient and room context, so it follows the request. 

**G5 → G6, G7, G10 [AND] [`G6;G7;G10`]**
**Justification:** The robot must first carry meals, then gain access to the room, then transfer the meal to the table or through tray retrieval. 

**G7 → G8, G9 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G8,G9)`]**
**Justification:** Door opening may be achieved with human cooperation or another robot. Fallback is used because one actor may be unavailable. 

**G10 → G11, G12, G16 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G11,FALLBACK(G12,G16))`]**
**Justification:** The meal may be placed on the table by a manipulation-capable robot, fetched by a person from the tray, or fetched with another robot’s cooperation. 

**G12 → G13, G14, G15 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G13,FALLBACK(G14,G15))`]**
**Justification:** Tray retrieval may be done by the inpatient, companion, or nurse, but the description notes uncertainty and possible unavailability. 

**G17 → G18, G19, G20, G21 [AND] [`(G18;G19)#(G20;G21)`]**
**Justification:** When multiple meals are carried, the robot must indicate which meal belongs to which inpatient, track retrieval time/location, and alert if a wrong meal is retrieved. 

**G22 → G23, G26, G29 [AND] [`G23;G26;G29`]**
**Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval starts from a pickup context, then requires room access, then dish collection. 

**G23 → G24, G25 [AND] [`G24;G25`]**
**Justification:** Someone in the room can call the robot and signal whether they can open the door. Both pieces of information are useful before pickup. 

**G26 → G27, G28 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G27,G28)`]**
**Justification:** Pickup room access can be achieved through a human opening the door or another robot helping open it. 

**G29 → G30, G31, G32 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G30,FALLBACK(G31,G32))`]**
**Justification:** Dirty dishes can be retrieved unassisted, by two robots, or through robot-human cooperation. 

**G3 → AT1 [AND]**
**Justification:** Reading the kitchen request operationalizes the query for the delivery request.

**G4 → AT2, AT3, AT4 [AND]**
**Justification:** Retrieval capability depends on patient information, companion presence, and possible nurse availability.

**G6 → AT5, AT6 [AND]**
**Justification:** Carrying meals requires loading them and transporting them from kitchen to inpatient room.

**G17 → AT14, AT15, AT16, AT17 [AND]**
**Justification:** Correctness and traceability require indication, event recording, identity verification, and alerting.

**G29 → AT22, AT23, AT24 [AND with fallback]**
**Justification:** These are alternative operational ways to retrieve dirty dishes, selected according to assistance availability.
