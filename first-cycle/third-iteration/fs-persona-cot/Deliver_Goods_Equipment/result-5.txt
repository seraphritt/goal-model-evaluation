# Multi-Robot Goal Model: Hospital Food Delivery and Dirty-Dish Retrieval

## 1. Design rationale

The mission has two major service capabilities: delivering food from the kitchen to inpatient rooms, and retrieving dirty dishes from rooms. The description explicitly states that delivery happens order-by-order in response to kitchen requests, that meals may be placed on a room table or fetched from a robot tray, that tray retrieval depends on uncertain human/robot cooperation, and that dirty dishes must also be retrieved from the room.

I model AND decompositions when all children are required to satisfy the parent, OR-style mission alternatives as fallback structures when the system should try one option and use another if the first is not feasible, and query goals when the robot must obtain information such as patient ability, companion presence, or door-opening availability. This follows the definitions of AND/OR decomposition, runtime sequencing, parallelism, and fallback.
Because the instruction says task locations must come from the mission description, I use only cited mission locations or mission entities treated as information locations: kitchen, inpatient room, room table, robot tray, room door, and inpatient record. The dirty-dish return destination is not specified, so the model stops at “dirty dishes removed from the room” rather than inventing a dishwashing area or kitchen return.

## 5. Logical relationships and justifications

**G1 → G2, G25 [AND][parallel: G2#G25]**

* **Justification:** The overall mission includes both food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are both required service capabilities, but different requests may be handled independently and therefore in parallel.

**G2 → G3, G8, G11, G15, G22 [AND][sequential: G3;G8;G11;G15;G22]**

* **Justification:** A delivery request must first be understood, then meals prepared, the room reached, the handover completed, and the result verified.

**G3 → G4, G5, G6, G7 [AND][parallel: G4#G5#G6#G7]**

* **Justification:** The robot needs all delivery context information. These are information-gathering goals and can be performed in parallel.

**G4 → AT1 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Receiving the kitchen request is the operational task that satisfies acquiring the request.

**G5 → AT2 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Reading meal-room mapping is required because the robot may carry multiple meals.

**G6 → AT3, AT4 [AND][parallel: AT3#AT4]**

* **Justification:** The robot needs both patient-record information and current availability of possible helpers.

**G7 → AT5 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Checking the door-opening context operationalizes the need for possible cooperation at the room door.

**G8 → G9, G10 [AND][sequential: G9;G10]**

* **Justification:** Meals must be loaded before their tray identities can be reliably maintained and indicated.

**G9 → AT6 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Loading meals onto the tray directly achieves meal loading.

**G10 → AT7 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Assigning or displaying meal indicators directly supports correct meal selection.

**G11 → AT8, G12 [AND][sequential: AT8;G12]**

* **Justification:** The robot must first navigate to the room door, then obtain door access.

**G12 → G13, G14 [AND][FALLBACK(G13,G14)]**

* **Justification:** Door opening may be performed with human cooperation or with another robot. Fallback is appropriate because the availability of cooperation may vary.

**G13 → AT9 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Coordinating with a human is the concrete action for human-assisted door opening.

**G14 → AT10 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Coordinating another robot is the concrete action for robot-assisted door opening.

**G15 → G16, G21 [AND][FALLBACK(G16,G21)]**

* **Justification:** The food may be fetched from the robot tray or delivered onto the room table. Since tray retrieval depends on uncertain availability of a capable retriever, fallback to table placement is justified when manipulation skill is available.

**G16 → G17, G18, G19, G20 [AND][FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G17,G18),G19),G20)]**

* **Justification:** Tray retrieval can involve the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot. Fallback captures the uncertainty that any particular retriever may be unable or unavailable.

**G17 → AT11 [AND]**

* **Justification:** The robot indicates the correct meal and waits for the inpatient to retrieve it.

**G18 → AT12 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If the inpatient cannot retrieve the meal, a companion may do so if present.

**G19 → AT13 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If no companion is available, a nurse may cooperate.

**G20 → AT14 [AND]**

* **Justification:** If human retrieval is unavailable, another robot may cooperate.

**G21 → AT15 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Placing food on the room table requires the concrete manipulation task.

**G22 → G23, G24 [AND][parallel: G23#G24]**

* **Justification:** Tracking retrieval and detecting wrong-meal retrieval should occur together during or immediately after handover.

**G23 → AT16 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Recording the retrieval event directly satisfies the tracking goal.

**G24 → AT17 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Issuing an alert is the operational response to wrong-meal retrieval.

**G25 → G26, G29, G33, G37 [AND][sequential: G26;G29;G33;G37]**

* **Justification:** Dish pickup requires receiving context, reaching/accessing the room, retrieving dishes, and confirming completion.

**G26 → G27, G28 [AND][parallel: G27#G28]**

* **Justification:** The robot must know both that pickup was requested and whether the caller can open the door.

**G27 → AT18 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Receiving the pickup call operationalizes the pickup request.

**G28 → AT19 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Storing the door-opening signal operationalizes the caller’s ability information.

**G29 → AT20, G30 [AND][sequential: AT20;G30]**

* **Justification:** The robot first navigates to the room, then obtains door access.

**G30 → G31, G32 [AND][FALLBACK(G31,G32)]**

* **Justification:** The door may be opened by a human or by another robot, so fallback handles unavailable cooperation.

**G31 → AT21 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Coordinating a human to open the door satisfies human-assisted door opening.

**G32 → AT22 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Coordinating another robot satisfies robot-assisted door opening.

**G33 → G34, G35, G36 [AND][FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G34,G35),G36)]**

* **Justification:** Dish retrieval can be unassisted, performed by two robots, or performed with robot-human cooperation. Fallback captures escalating cooperation needs.

**G34 → AT23 [AND]**

* **Justification:** A single robot collecting dishes directly satisfies unassisted retrieval.

**G35 → AT24 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Two robots collecting dishes directly satisfies two-robot cooperative retrieval.

**G36 → AT25 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Robot-human collection directly satisfies human-assisted dish retrieval.

**G37 → AT26 [AND]**

* **Justification:** Marking dishes as removed confirms the required end state without inventing an unspecified destination.
