## Goal Model Design Rationale (high level)

The mission is modeled as an **Achieve-oriented mission** because the main objective is to reach a desired world state: **all required patients have their vital signs checked according to their medical condition and mission constraints**.

The main decomposition follows these observations:

* The robot must **obtain information** (occupied rooms, patient conditions, risk categories) → modeled as **Query Goals**.
* The robot must **change the world state** (patients checked, alerts sent, robots reassigned) → modeled as **Achieve Goals**.
* The robot must execute physical actions (move, enter rooms, measure signs, sterilize) → modeled as **Perform Goals/Tasks**.
* Several activities are mandatory together → **AND decomposition**.
* Some actions are alternatives depending on failure conditions → **FALLBACK runtime execution**.
* Some activities can happen simultaneously → **parallel (#)**.
