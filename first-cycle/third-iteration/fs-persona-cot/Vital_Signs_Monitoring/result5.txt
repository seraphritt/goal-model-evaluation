# Multi-Robot Vital-Signs Goal Model

## 1. Modeling assumptions and rationale

The mission’s main objective is to ensure that every two hours all patients’ vital signs are checked by assigned robots visiting every occupied room. Before room entry, the robot must verify patient availability and battery sufficiency. After entry, it must identify the correct patient, prepare the patient, collect the required vital signs according to the patient’s condition, handle failures, and perform hygiene actions when needed. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

The model uses:

- **AND decomposition** when all child elements are jointly required.
- **OR decomposition** when alternatives exist, such as identifying the patient either by asking or by scanning the barcode.
- **FALLBACK** when the description explicitly says one action is attempted first and a second action is triggered if the first fails, such as retrying after patient unavailability or alerting staff after non-response. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

  - **Justification**: Questions are triggered when necessary vital signs fail to be collected; alerting the nurse or doctor is triggered if the patient does not respond.

- **G27 -> G28, G29, G30 [AND] [`G28;(G29#G30)`]**  
  - **Justification**: The patient must be marked done and the robot must proceed. Sterilization and actuator disinfection are conditionally required hygiene tasks and can be treated as parallel post-room obligations when both apply.

- **G31 -> G32, G33 [AND] [`FALLBACK(G32,G33)`]**  
  - **Justification**: Additional robots are attempted first when the mission cannot be completed in time; if no robot combination can complete the mission, the site manager is alerted.
