## Goal Model Overview

The mission is decomposed around the main objective:

**G1: Complete periodic patient vital signs monitoring mission**

The main objective requires several complementary activities:

* discovering the mission scope (rooms/patients),
* prioritizing high-risk patients,
* executing patient checks,
* handling failures,
* ensuring robot availability,
* escalating impossible situations.

Therefore, the decomposition is mainly **AND**, because all these activities are required to successfully complete the mission.

### G4 -> AT8 [AND]
