# Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4, G5 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** The mission requires identification, execution, failure handling, and safety management. These are complementary activities.

* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** The robot must know occupied rooms before identifying patients.

* **G2.2 -> AT1, AT2 [OR]**

  * **Justification:** Asking or barcode scanning are alternative identification methods.

* **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** The robot must reach rooms, handle constraints, and collect signs.

* **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][FALLBACK(G3.2.1,G3.2.2)]**

  * **Justification:** Normal availability is attempted first; battery recovery is an alternative operational path.

* **G3.3.2 -> AT11, AT12, AT13 [OR]**

  * **Justification:** Vital signs depend on patient condition.

* **G4 -> G4.1, G4.2 [AND]**

  * **Justification:** Failed collection requires both assessment and possible notification.

* **G5 -> G5.1, G5.2, G5.3 [AND][parallel]**

  * **Justification:** Safety actions, prioritization, and resource management can occur concurrently during mission execution.

* **G5.3 -> AT19, AT20 [AND][FALLBACK(AT19,AT20)]**

  * **Justification:** Additional robots are attempted first; management notification occurs only if the mission remains impossible.
