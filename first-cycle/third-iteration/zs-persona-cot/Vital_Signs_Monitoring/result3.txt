# Logical Relationships

### G1 -> G2, G6, G10 [AND][Parallel]

* **Justification:** The overall mission requires patient checking, patient-condition adaptations, and operational constraints simultaneously.

### G2 -> G3, G4, G5 [AND][Sequential]

* **Justification:** The robot must verify entry conditions before performing measurements, and failures are handled after measurement attempts.

### G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][Parallel]

* **Justification:** Patient availability and battery level are independent checks that must both succeed before entering.

### G4 -> AT3, AT4, AT5, AT6 [AND][Sequential]

* **Justification:** Room visitation, identification, interaction, measurement, and completion marking occur as a procedure sequence.

### G5 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][Sequential]

* **Justification:** Questions are attempted first; if the patient does not respond, medical staff are alerted.

### G6 -> G7, G8, G9 [AND][Parallel]

* **Justification:** Different patient categories require different measurement sets, and applicable requirements must be fulfilled.

### G7 -> AT9 [AND]

* **Justification:** Infectious disease checking requires all listed vital signs.

### G8 -> AT10 [AND]

* **Justification:** Post-surgical checking requires both specified measurements.

### G9 -> AT11 [AND]

* **Justification:** Diabetes history requires glucose measurement.

### G10 -> G11, G12, G13, G14, G15 [AND][Parallel]

* **Justification:** Safety, timing, resource allocation, and escalation mechanisms are complementary mission requirements.

### G11 -> AT12 [AND]

* **Justification:** Battery failure handling requires recharge and reassignment.

### G12 -> AT13 [AND]

* **Justification:** Sterilization is the required action after contamination risk.

### G13 -> AT14 [AND]

* **Justification:** High-risk patient priority is fulfilled by performing the early checks.

### G14 -> AT15 [AND]

* **Justification:** Additional robot support is achieved by assigning extra robots.

### G15 -> AT16 [AND]

* **Justification:** Manager notification is achieved through the alert action.
