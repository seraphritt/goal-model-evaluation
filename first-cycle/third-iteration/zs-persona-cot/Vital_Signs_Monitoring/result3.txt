Below is a source-grounded goal model. I use the provided definitions: a goal model is a tree of goals/sub-goals/tasks with AND/OR decomposition, AND means all children are needed, OR means one child is sufficient, and leaf goals use `-` as runtime annotation. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"} :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}  
The mission driver is the two-hour vital-signs round across occupied rooms, with availability, battery, patient identification, condition-specific vital signs, failure handling, hygiene, extra-robot assignment, and escalation constraints. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

**Modeling assumptions used:**  
`N` means the number of robots available at the site, because the mission says additional robots may be assigned but does not give a fixed upper bound. Each room/patient workflow is instantiated for every occupied room.

## 1. Step-by-step decomposition rationale

1. **Mission objective:** complete each two-hour checking cycle for all occupied rooms.  
2. **Preparation:** trigger the periodic cycle and assign the robot.  
3. **Execution attempt:** check high-risk patients first, then process occupied rooms.  
4. **Room entry control:** before entering, verify patient availability and battery.  
5. **Patient identification:** identify the correct patient by asking or barcode scanning.  
6. **In-room procedure:** approach, announce, instruct, then collect the applicable vital signs.  
7. **Failure handling:** if a necessary sign cannot be collected, ask assessment questions; if there is no response, alert the nurse/doctor.  
8. **Completion and hygiene:** mark the patient as done, sterilize/disinfect when required, and proceed to the next room.  
9. **Mission infeasibility handling:** if the assigned robot set cannot complete the mission in time, assign extra robots; if no robot combination works, alert the site manager.
