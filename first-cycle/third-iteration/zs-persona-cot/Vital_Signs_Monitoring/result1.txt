## 1. Modeling basis and assumptions

The goal model is built as a hierarchical tree of goals, sub-goals, and tasks, using AND decompositions when all children are required and OR decompositions when one alternative is sufficient. This follows the provided definition of a goal model and AND/OR decomposition. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

I use **FALLBACK** only when the mission explicitly states what happens after failure, such as patient unavailable → return in 5 minutes, insufficient battery → recharge and reassign, vital-sign collection failure → ask questions, and non-response → alert nurse/doctor. This follows the provided rule that fallback must be used only when the first and second actions are explicitly defined. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

The recurring mission is interpreted as: every two hours, robots must check vital signs for patients in every occupied room, while prioritizing high-risk patients in the first 15 minutes, handling battery and availability failures, applying hygiene procedures, and escalating if no robot combination can complete the mission. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}
