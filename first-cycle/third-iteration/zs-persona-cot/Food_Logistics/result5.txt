Below is an auditable goal-model decomposition based only on the pasted mission description. I use **AND** when all children are required, **OR-like alternatives as FALLBACK** when the mission describes alternative cooperation modes under uncertainty, and `-` only where a node has no sub-goal runtime. The definitions require hierarchical AND/OR decomposition and runtime annotations such as sequential `;`, parallel `#`, and `FALLBACK(...)`.  

## Source basis

The mission concerns food delivery from the **kitchen** to an **inpatient room**, order-by-order, with alternative delivery modes: robot placement on the room table or tray retrieval by an inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot. It also requires meal identification, tracking, wrong-meal alerts, dirty-dish retrieval, and possible human/robot cooperation for room-door opening.

## 4. Logical relationships

**G1 → G2, G16 [AND][parallel `G2#G16`]**
Justification: The system must support both meal delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are modeled in parallel because the description defines them as service capabilities that may be triggered by different requests.

**G2 → G3, G4, G5, G8, G15 [AND][sequential `G3;G4;G5;G8;G15`]**
Justification: A food order must first be understood, then transported, then brought through the door, then transferred, and finally tracked for correct retrieval.

**G3 → AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential `AT1;AT2`]**
Justification: Receiving the kitchen request is insufficient unless the robot also identifies the meal, inpatient, and room.

**G4 → AT3, AT4 [AND][sequential `AT3;AT4`]**
Justification: The robot must load the meal before navigating to the inpatient room.

**G5 → G6, G7 [AND][`FALLBACK(G6,G7)`]**
Justification: Door opening may be achieved through human cooperation or another robot. Fallback is appropriate because one method may be unavailable.

**G8 → G9, G10 [AND][`FALLBACK(G9,G10)`]**
Justification: The robot can either place the food on the table using manipulation or rely on tray retrieval. Since table placement requires a special skill, tray retrieval is modeled as an alternative when that skill or mode is unavailable.

**G10 → G11, G12, G13, G14 [AND][`G11;FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G12,G13),G14)`]**
Justification: The robot should first query who can retrieve the food. Because the inpatient may be unable and a companion or nurse may be unavailable, the retrieval actor is modeled as a fallback chain.

**G15 → AT17, AT18, AT19 [AND][parallel `AT17#AT18#AT19`]**
Justification: Indicating the correct meal, tracking retrieval, and detecting wrong retrieval are monitoring activities that can occur during the same handover window.

**G16 → G17, G18, G21, G25 [AND][sequential `G17;G18;G21;G25`]**
Justification: Dish retrieval begins with a pickup request, then requires access, then a retrieval mode, then completion recording.

**G18 → G19, G20 [AND][`FALLBACK(G19,G20)`]**
Justification: A person may open the door, but if that is unavailable, another robot can cooperate.

**G21 → G22, G23, G24 [AND][`FALLBACK(FALLBACK(G22,G23),G24)`]**
Justification: Dirty-dish retrieval can be unassisted, two-robot, or robot-human cooperative. Fallback reflects that assistance may or may not be available.
