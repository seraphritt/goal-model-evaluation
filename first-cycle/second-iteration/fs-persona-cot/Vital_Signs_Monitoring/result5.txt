## Goal Model Overview

The mission is modeled as an **AND-oriented mission** because all major objectives must be satisfied: planning the checks, visiting patients, collecting vital signs, handling failures, and ensuring robot availability/safety.

# Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4, G5 [AND][(G2;G3)#(G4#G5)]**

  * **Justification:** All mission objectives are required. Patient checking depends on preparation, while safety and feasibility monitoring can occur in parallel.

* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** Occupied rooms must be known before assigning robots.

* **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3, G3.4, G3.5 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** The robot must arrive, validate conditions, identify the patient, perform measurements, and finalize the visit.

* **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][FALLBACK(G3.2.1,G3.2.2)]**

  * **Justification:** Normal availability checking is attempted first; retry handling is executed when the patient is unavailable.

* **G3.3 -> G3.3.1, G3.3.2 [OR]**

  * **Justification:** Patient identification can be achieved either by asking or barcode scanning.

* **G3.4 -> G3.4.1, G3.4.2 [AND][sequential]**

  * **Justification:** Required measurements must first be determined before execution.

* **G4 -> G4.1, G4.2, G4.3 [AND][parallel]**

  * **Justification:** Failure recovery, hygiene, and resource monitoring are independent concerns.

* **G4.1 -> G4.1.1, G4.1.2 [AND][FALLBACK(G4.1.1,G4.1.2)]**

  * **Justification:** The robot attempts recovery through questions before escalating to medical personnel.

* **G4.2 -> AT13, AT14 [AND]**

  * **Justification:** Both sterilization after infectious contact and actuator disinfection after physical contact are required safety actions.

* **G3 -> AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4, AT5, AT6, AT7, AT8, AT9, AT10, AT11, AT12 [AND]**

  * **Justification:** These are the concrete operations required to complete a patient visit.

* **G4.3 -> AT15, AT16 [OR]**

  * **Justification:** Either additional robot allocation solves feasibility problems, or escalation is required if no allocation works.
