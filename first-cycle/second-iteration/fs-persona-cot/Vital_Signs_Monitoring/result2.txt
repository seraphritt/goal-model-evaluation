## Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4 [AND][sequential: (G2;G3;G4)]**

  * **Justification:** The mission requires preparation, execution of patient checking, and operational management. All are required.

* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][parallel: G2.1#G2.2]**

  * **Justification:** Robot assignment and identification of priority patients are independent activities.

* **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3, G3.4, G3.5 [AND][sequential: G3.1;G3.2;G3.3;G3.4;G3.5]**

  * **Justification:** The robot must visit rooms, verify conditions, identify patients, collect measurements, and handle failures.

* **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][parallel: G3.2.1#G3.2.2]**

  * **Justification:** Availability and battery checks are both necessary before entry and can occur independently.

* **G3.3 -> G3.3.1, G3.3.2 [AND][FALLBACK(G3.3.1,G3.3.2)]**

  * **Justification:** Asking the patient is attempted first; barcode scanning is used if interaction cannot identify the patient.

* **G3.4 -> AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4 [AND][parallel]**

  * **Justification:** Different measurements and patient communication activities may be performed as part of the same room procedure.

* **G3.5 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][FALLBACK(AT5,AT6)]**

  * **Justification:** Questioning is the first recovery action; alerting medical staff occurs if the patient does not respond.

* **G4 -> G4.1, G4.2, G4.3 [AND][parallel]**

  * **Justification:** Battery handling, sterilization, and workload management are independent operational concerns.

* **G4.3 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][FALLBACK(AT7,AT8)]**

  * **Justification:** The system first attempts to increase robot capacity; if this cannot solve feasibility, management is alerted.

* **G3.4 -> AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4 [AND]**

  * **Justification:** The required measurements depend on patient condition, but the overall vital sign checking process requires executing the applicable measurement tasks and patient interaction.
