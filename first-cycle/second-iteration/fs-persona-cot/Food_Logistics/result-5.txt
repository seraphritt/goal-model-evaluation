### Logical Relationships

- **G1 → G2, G3** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* The mission is completed only after both food delivery (G2) and dish retrieval (G3) are finished, and these tasks are performed one after the other.

- **G2 → G2.1, G2.2** [OR][fallback]  
  *Justification:* The robot may either deliver directly to the table (G2.1) or hand over to someone (G2.2). If the first fails, the second is attempted.

- **G2.1 → AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* The robot must first pick up the meal, then navigate, open the door, and finally place the food on the table; each step must succeed for delivery.

- **G2.2 → G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3, G2.2.4** [OR][fallback]  
  *Justification:* Patient hand‑over is tried first; if unavailable, the robot falls back to companion, then nurse, and finally robot‑cooperation.

- **G2.2.1 → AT5, AT6, AT7, AT8, AT9** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* The patient hand‑over sequence requires querying capability, requesting retrieval, monitoring, verifying the meal, and alerting on error.

- **G2.2.2 → AT10, AT11, AT12** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* Companion hand‑over follows the same structured steps.

- **G2.2.3 → AT13, AT14, AT15** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* Nurse hand‑over also follows a defined procedure.

- **G2.2.4 → AT16, AT17** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* Two robots must cooperate in a coordinated sequence to hand over the food.

- **G3 → AT18, AT19, AT20, AT21** [AND][sequential]  
  *Justification:* Dish retrieval requires checking for dishes, opening the door, collecting them, and returning them to the kitchen.

- **G2.2.1.1 → AT5** [AND]  
  *Justification:* The query goal is directly satisfied by performing AT5.

- **G2.2.2.1 → AT10** [AND]  
  *Justification:* The companion presence query is performed by AT10.

- **G2.2.3.1 → AT13** [AND]  
  *Justification:* The nurse availability query is performed by AT13.
