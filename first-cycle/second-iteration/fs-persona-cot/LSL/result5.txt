## 1. Scope and modeling rationale

I will model only goals/tasks that are performed by robots, as required. Therefore:

- **Included robot actors**
  - A **mobile delivery robot** transporting samples in secure locked drawers.
  - A **robotic arm** in the laboratory that picks, scans, sorts, logs, and loads samples.

- **Excluded non-robot activities**
  - Nurse collecting the sample.
  - Nurse inserting sample data into the tracking system.
  - Laboratory personnel picking samples.
  - Authorized personnel opening drawers.

These human activities are treated as **external preconditions or environment events**, not as goal-model nodes.

- **No OR decomposition is used**, because the explicit alternatives in the description involve non-robot actors, e.g., “robotic arm or laboratory personnel” and “authorized personnel or specific robots.” Since non-robot alternatives must not appear in the model, the robot-only branch is modeled.
- **No FALLBACK relation is used**, because the mission does not explicitly specify a failure-handling order such as “try robot first, if it fails use personnel.”

---

## 2. Design reasoning summary

| Step | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 1 | The mission starts when a nurse requests delivery and the sample already has barcode/data. These are external triggers/preconditions. |
| 2 | A delivery robot must support secure sample pickup from patient floors using locked drawers. |
| 3 | The sample must be transported from patient floors to the laboratory while maintaining drawer security. |
| 4 | The elapsed time between collection and delivery must be tracked during transport, so this can occur in parallel with movement. |
| 5 | In the laboratory, drawer access must be restricted to a specific authorized robot/location. |
| 6 | The robotic arm performs the laboratory processing sequence: pick sample, scan barcode, log pickup information, sort, and load into the analysis machine entry-module. |

# 6. Logical relationships

## Top-level mission

- **G1 -> G2, G3, G4 [AND][sequential: `G2;G3;G4`]**
  - **Justification**: The robot mission requires secure pickup preparation first, then transport to the laboratory, then laboratory robotic processing. These are all required phases and must occur in that order.

---

## Secure pickup preparation

- **G2 -> AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4, AT5 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2;AT3;AT4;AT5`]**
  - **Justification**: The delivery robot must reach the patient floors, validate drawer authorization, open the drawer, lock it after handoff, and confirm the locked state before transport. All are necessary for secure sample transport.

---

## Secure timed delivery

- **G3 -> AT6, AT7, AT8 [AND][parallel/sequential: `(AT6#AT7);AT8`]**
  - **Justification**: The drawer must be transported to the laboratory while elapsed time is tracked. Transport and timing can occur in parallel. Once the robot reaches the laboratory, delivery timing status is recorded.

---

## Laboratory robotic handling

- **G4 -> G5, G6, G7 [AND][sequential: `G5;G6;G7`]**
  - **Justification**: The lab phase requires drawer access first, then picking/scanning/logging, then sorting/loading. The sequence follows the description of laboratory sample handling.

---

## Authorized drawer access in laboratory

- **G5 -> AT9, AT10 [AND][sequential: `AT9;AT10`]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must first verify that it is authorized in the laboratory location before opening the drawer. Both actions are required by the locked-drawer access-control constraint.

---

## Robotic sample pickup, barcode scan, and logging

- **G6 -> AT11, AT12, AT13 [AND][sequential: `AT11;AT12;AT13`]**
  - **Justification**: The description states that the robotic arm picks up samples, scans the barcode, and that pickup information must be logged securely. The scan follows the physical pickup, and logging follows identification.

---

## Sorting and machine loading

- **G7 -> AT14, AT15 [AND][sequential: `AT14;AT15`]**
  - **Justification**: The robotic arm sorts samples before loading them into the entry-module of the analysis machines. Both are required for completing laboratory preparation.

---

# 7. Notes on excluded alternatives

| Description element | Modeling decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “The sample can be picked by a robotic arm or laboratory personnel.” | Only robotic arm branch is modeled. | Laboratory personnel are non-robot actors and must not appear in the goal model. |
| “Only authorized personnel or specific robots…” | Only specific robot authorization is modeled. | Authorized personnel are non-robot actors and excluded. |
| Nurse requests delivery and inserts data in tracking system. | Treated as external trigger/precondition. | These actions are performed by a nurse, not a robot. |
| Barcode assigned before collection. | Treated as external precondition; barcode scanning is modeled. | Barcode assignment is not described as a robot task, but robotic barcode scanning is explicitly described. |
