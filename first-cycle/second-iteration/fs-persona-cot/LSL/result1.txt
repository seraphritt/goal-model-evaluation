## 1. Modeling interpretation and scope

I model only robot-performed parts of the mission, as required. Therefore:

- The nurse’s collection of samples, barcode preparation, and manual data insertion are **not modeled as tasks**, because they are human-only actions.
- The alternative “laboratory personnel can pick the sample” is **not included**, because it is also human-only. The robot-relevant branch is the **robotic arm pickup**.
- No **FALLBACK** relation is used, because the description does not define a failure-triggered alternative such as “if robot fails, then personnel does it.”
- No **OR decomposition** is used in the robot-only goal model. The only explicit “or” alternatives involve humans, and those must be excluded under the zero-robot constraint.
- The modeled robot team contains at least:
  - one mobile delivery robot with secure locked drawers;
  - one laboratory robotic arm.

# 5. Logical Relationships

## Main mission decomposition

- **G1 → G2, G3, G6, G9 [AND][sequential: `G2;(G3;(G6;G9))`]**
  - **Justification:** The robot must first obtain the delivery and sample information, then secure the sample, then transport it, and finally process it in the laboratory. All are required for the mission to be successful.

---

## Request and sample information

- **G2 → AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2`]**
  - **Justification:** The robot first obtains the delivery request and then retrieves the corresponding sample data. Both are required because the robot needs the request and the associated barcode/sample metadata.

---

## Patient-floor securing

- **G3 → AT3, G4, G5 [AND][sequential: `AT3;(G4;G5)`]**
  - **Justification:** The robot must reach the patient floor before drawer access can occur. After that, authorized drawer opening and drawer locking are both necessary to secure the sample.

- **G4 → AT4, AT5 [AND][sequential: `AT4;AT5`]**
  - **Justification:** Authorization and location verification must occur before the robot opens the locked drawer. Both are required to enforce the secure-access rule.

- **G5 → AT6, AT7 [AND][sequential: `AT6;AT7`]**
  - **Justification:** The robot must confirm that the sample is inside the drawer and then close and lock the drawer. The sample is not ready for secure transport unless both are completed.

---

## Transport and protocol timing

- **G6 → G7, G8 [AND][parallel: `G7#G8`]**
  - **Justification:** The physical delivery and time tracking are both required. They can execute in parallel because the elapsed time can be monitored while the robot travels from patient floors to the laboratory.

- **G7 → AT8, AT9 [AND][sequential: `AT8;AT9`]**
  - **Justification:** The robot must first travel with the locked drawer and then confirm laboratory arrival.

- **G8 → AT10, AT11, AT12 [AND][sequential: `AT10;(AT11;AT12)`]**
  - **Justification:** Timing must be initialized using the collection timestamp, monitored during delivery, and recorded after laboratory arrival to verify protocol compliance.

---

## Laboratory robotic processing

- **G9 → G10, G11, G12, G13 [AND][sequential/parallel: `G10;(G11;(G12#G13))`]**
  - **Justification:** Laboratory processing requires authorized drawer opening first. The robotic arm then picks and scans the sample. After identification, sorting/loading and secure pickup logging can proceed in parallel because both use the identified sample information.

- **G10 → AT13, AT14 [AND][sequential: `AT13;AT14`]**
  - **Justification:** Laboratory drawer opening must follow authorization and location verification. This enforces the rule that only specific robots in specific locations can open locked drawers.

- **G11 → AT15, AT16 [AND][sequential: `AT15;AT16`]**
  - **Justification:** The robotic arm must pick the sample and then scan its barcode. Barcode scanning identifies the physical sample for sorting, loading, and logging.

- **G12 → AT17, AT18 [AND][sequential: `AT17;AT18`]**
  - **Justification:** The sample must be sorted before it is loaded into the correct entry-module of the analysis machines.

- **G13 → AT19, AT20 [AND][sequential: `AT19;AT20`]**
  - **Justification:** A pickup event must first be created with barcode, location, and time, and then securely written to the tracking system.

# 6. Notes on excluded alternatives

The description contains two human-inclusive alternatives:

1. “The sample can be picked by a robotic arm or laboratory personnel.”
2. “Only authorized personnel or specific robots in specific locations should be able to open the locked drawers.”

Because the instructions state that goals and tasks not performed by any robot must not appear in the goal model, the human alternatives are excluded. Therefore, they are not represented as OR decompositions. Only the robot-performed branches are modeled.
