## 1. Modeling scope and key assumptions

The goal model below includes only objectives and tasks that can be performed by robots or robot controllers. Therefore:

- Nurse actions are **not modeled as tasks**: sample collection, initial barcode identification, entering data in the tracking system, and requesting delivery are human/system-side preconditions.
- Laboratory personnel pickup is **not modeled**, even though the description says samples “can be picked by a robotic arm or laboratory personnel,” because personnel-only work is explicitly out of scope.
- The modeled robots are:
  - A **mobile delivery robot** transporting secure locked drawers from patient floors to the laboratory.
  - A **laboratory robotic arm** that picks, scans, sorts, and loads samples into analysis-machine entry modules.
- No FALLBACK relation is used because the description does not define failure-triggered alternatives or exception-handling order.

# 5. Logical Relationships and Justifications

- **G1 -> G2, G3, G6, G7 [AND][`G2;(G3#G6);G7`]**
  - **Justification**: The mission requires the robot system to obtain delivery information, transport samples, track time, and process samples in the laboratory. These are all necessary. Transport and time tracking can occur in parallel because the elapsed-time monitoring happens while the sample is being delivered.

- **G2 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][`AT1;AT2`]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must first query the tracking system, then validate that the required data exists. Both are needed before transport.

- **G3 -> G4, G5 [AND][`G4;G5`]**
  - **Justification**: Secure transport requires securing the drawer before movement and then delivering it to the laboratory. The order is sequential.

- **G4 -> AT3, AT4, AT5 [AND][`AT3;AT4;AT5`]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must verify authorization, open or present the drawer, and lock it for transport. These are all required to satisfy secure locked-drawer transport.

- **G5 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][`AT6;AT7`]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must physically move the locked drawer to the laboratory and confirm arrival. Delivery is not complete without both.

- **G6 -> AT8, AT9, AT10 [AND][`AT8;AT9;AT10`]**
  - **Justification**: To track collection-to-delivery time, the robot/controller needs the collection timestamp, the delivery timestamp, and a protocol-compliance check.

- **G7 -> G8, G9, G10, G11 [AND][`G8;G9;(G10#G11)`]**
  - **Justification**: Laboratory processing requires authorized drawer access, sample pickup and barcode scanning, sorting/loading, and secure logging. After sample identification, sorting/loading and logging can proceed in parallel.

- **G8 -> AT11, AT12 [AND][`AT11;AT12`]**
  - **Justification**: Drawer opening is allowed only after verifying that the robot and location are authorized.

- **G9 -> AT13, AT14 [AND][`AT13;AT14`]**
  - **Justification**: The robotic arm must pick up each sample and then scan its barcode. Scanning depends on the sample being picked or presented.

- **G10 -> AT15, AT16 [AND][`AT15;AT16`]**
  - **Justification**: The sample must be sorted before being loaded into the entry-module of the analysis machines.

- **G11 -> AT17, AT18 [AND][`AT17;AT18`]**
  - **Justification**: Secure logging requires capturing event data and then storing it securely.

# 6. Notes on OR and FALLBACK

- **No OR decomposition is used** because the only explicit alternative in the description is “robotic arm or laboratory personnel.” Since laboratory personnel are not robots, that branch is excluded from the robot goal model.
- **No FALLBACK relation is used** because the mission does not describe a failure condition, exception, threshold-triggered alternative behavior, or ordered recovery strategy.
