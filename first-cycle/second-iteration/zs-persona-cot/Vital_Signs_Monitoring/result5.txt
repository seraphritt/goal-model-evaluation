# Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4, G18 [AND] [(G2;G3;G4#G18)]**

  * **Justification:** The mission cannot be completed without planning, execution, priority handling, and safety/failure management.

* **G2 -> G7, G8 [AND] [G7#G8]**

  * **Justification:** Robot allocation and task scheduling are both necessary planning activities.

* **G4 -> G5, G6, G9, G11, G12 [AND] [G5;G6;G9;G11;G12]**

  * **Justification:** A patient must be identified, preconditions checked, interaction prepared, measurements collected, and failures handled.

* **G5 -> AT4, AT5 [AND] [FALLBACK(AT4,AT5)]**

  * **Justification:** Asking and barcode scanning are alternative identification methods.

* **G6 -> G13, G14 [AND] [G13#G14]**

  * **Justification:** Availability and battery status are independent checks that can occur in parallel.

* **G9 -> G15, G16, G17 [AND] [G15;G16;G17]**

  * **Justification:** The robot must approach first, then communicate and instruct.

* **G12 -> AT11, AT12 [AND] [FALLBACK(AT11,AT12)]**

  * **Justification:** Questioning is attempted first; alerting occurs if the patient does not respond.

* **G18 -> AT17, AT18, AT19 [AND] [AT17#AT18#AT19]**

  * **Justification:** Sterilization, actuator disinfection, and manager escalation are independent safety responses.
