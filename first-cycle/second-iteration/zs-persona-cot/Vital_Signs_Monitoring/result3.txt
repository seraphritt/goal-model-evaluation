## Mission-design rationale (summary)

The mission is decomposed around four main concerns:

1. **Periodic vital-sign monitoring**: the robot(s) must repeatedly visit occupied rooms and complete patient checks.
2. **Patient identification and interaction workflow**: before measurements, the robot must verify availability, identify the correct patient, approach, explain the procedure, and collect required signs.
3. **Exception handling and safety**: failures, unavailable patients, low battery, and non-response require alternative actions or escalation.
4. **Mission-level constraints**: high-risk patients have priority, infectious/post-contact sterilization is required, and additional robots must be assigned when deadlines cannot be met.
