# Mission Goal Model Design Rationale

## General modeling assumptions

* The mission objective is decomposed as an **Achieve goal** because the robot system must reach a desired operational state: *all required patients have their vital signs checked according to their conditions within the required time constraints*.
* Operational activities such as navigating, scanning barcodes, measuring vital signs, sterilizing, and alerting staff are represented as **Perform goals/tasks** because they correspond to actions executed by robots.
* Information acquisition activities (e.g., identifying patients, checking availability, verifying battery status) are represented as **Query goals** because they retrieve information from the environment before decisions are made.
* Sequential execution (`;`) is used when one action depends on completion of another.
* Parallel execution (`#`) is used when independent activities may happen simultaneously (e.g., multiple robots checking different rooms).
* Fallback execution is used when the mission explicitly defines an alternative behavior after failure (e.g., patient unavailable → retry after 5 minutes; insufficient battery → recharge and reassign tasks).

# Logical Relationships

### G1 -> G2, G3, G4, G5 [AND] [parallel]

* **Justification:** The mission requires patient identification, checking, robot availability, and safety handling. These are independent dimensions that all must succeed.

### G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND] [sequential]

* **Justification:** The robot first needs the occupied rooms and then must identify the correct patient in those rooms.

### G2.2 -> G2.2.1, G2.2.2 [OR] [parallel]

* **Justification:** Patient identification can occur by asking or scanning the barcode.

### G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3, G3.4 [AND] [sequential]

* **Justification:** Conditions must be verified before interaction, interaction precedes measurement, and failures are handled afterward.

### G3.3 -> G3.3.1, G3.3.2, G3.3.3 [OR]

* **Justification:** The measurement set depends on patient medical condition.

### G3.4 -> G3.4.1, G3.4.2 [AND] [FALLBACK(G3.4.1,G3.4.2)]

* **Justification:** The robot first attempts recovery through patient questions. If unsuccessful, it escalates by alerting healthcare personnel.

### G4 -> G4.1, G4.2, G4.3 [AND]

* **Justification:** Maintaining availability requires battery handling, task redistribution, and resource expansion when needed.

### G5 -> G5.1, G5.2, G5.3 [AND]

* **Justification:** Safety requires sterilization, actuator disinfection, and communication of critical situations.
