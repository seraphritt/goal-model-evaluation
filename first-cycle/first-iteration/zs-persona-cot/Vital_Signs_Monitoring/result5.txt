Below is a structured **goal model decomposition** for the multi-robot hospital vital-sign mission.

Assumption: repeated nodes such as **G10 Patient Check Instance** and **G25 Required Vital Sign Resolution** are **parameterized templates**. At runtime, they are instantiated once per assigned patient/room and once per required vital sign.

| **G13: Query Immediate Patient Availability**        | Determine whether the patient can currently receive the vital-sign procedure.                                              |                                                                      `AT10` | Query     | Patient availability status.                                                                                                                                                                                              | AND      | This goal enquires patient readiness.                                                                       |

| **G16: Query Battery and Energy Demand**             | Determine whether the robot has enough battery for the room tasks.                                                         |                                                                      `AT14` | Query     | Current battery level, estimated task energy, safety margin.                                                                                                                                                              | AND      | This goal retrieves battery feasibility information.                                                        |
| **G17: Recharge and Reassign Pending Tasks**         | If battery is insufficient, recharge the robot and reassign affected tasks.                                                |                                                            `AT15;AT16;AT17` | Achieve   | Robot is recharged and pending tasks are reassigned.                                                                                                                                                                      | AND      | The goal restores mission feasibility after battery failure.                                                |
| **G18: Access Room and Identify Patient**            | Enter the occupied room and identify the correct patient before performing the procedure.                                  |                                                             `AT18;G19;AT23` | Achieve   | Correct patient is identified and approached.                                                                                                                                                                             | AND      | The target state is safe patient-specific positioning.                                                      |
| **G19: Identify Correct Patient**                    | Identify the patient either by asking or scanning the barcode.                                                             |                                                         `FALLBACK(G20,G21)` | Achieve   | Patient identity is verified against the assigned patient record.                                                                                                                                                         | OR       | Either asking or barcode scanning is sufficient.                                                            |
| **G20: Identify by Asking**                          | Ask the patient to confirm identity and verify it against the mission record.                                              |                                                                 `AT19;AT20` | Perform   | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | AND      | This is an operational interaction sequence.                                                                |
| **G21: Identify by Barcode**                         | Scan the patient barcode and verify it against the mission record.                                                         |                                                                 `AT21;AT22` | Perform   | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | AND      | This is an operational sensing and verification sequence.                                                   |
| **G22: Prepare Patient Interaction**                 | Announce the procedure and provide instructions to the patient.                                                            |                                                                 `AT24;AT25` | Perform   | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | AND      | These are concrete communication actions.                                                                   |
| **G23: Determine and Collect Required Vital Signs**  | Determine which vital signs are required and collect or assess them.                                                       |                         `G24;(G25[s∈RequiredSigns] # G25[s∈RequiredSigns])` | Achieve   | All required vital signs are collected or clinically assessed through fallback questions/alerts.                                                                                                                          | AND      | The target state is that each required sign is resolved.                                                    |
| **G24: Query Required Vital Signs**                  | Determine the required vital signs based on patient condition.                                                             |                                                                      `AT26` | Query     | RequiredSigns = infectious disease → heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, blood oxygenation; post-surgical → heart rate, blood oxygenation; diabetes history → blood glucose. Use union if multiple conditions apply. | AND      | This goal enquires condition-dependent clinical requirements.                                               |
| **G25: Resolve Required Vital Sign**                 | For each required vital sign, collect the measurement or trigger the failure-handling procedure.                           |                                                         `FALLBACK(G26,G27)` | Achieve   | Required vital sign is measured, or fallback assessment/alert is completed.                                                                                                                                               | OR       | Measurement is preferred, but fallback assessment can resolve failed collection.                            |
| **G26: Collect Vital-Sign Measurement**              | Collect and record the required vital-sign measurement.                                                                    |                                                                      `AT27` | Achieve   | The required vital-sign value is recorded.                                                                                                                                                                                | AND      | The target state is a recorded clinical measurement.                                                        |
| **G27: Assess Failed Vital-Sign Collection**         | If measurement fails, assess patient status through questions or alert staff on non-response.                              |                                                         `FALLBACK(G28,G29)` | Achieve   | Patient status is assessed through responses, or clinical staff are alerted.                                                                                                                                              | OR       | The failed measurement can be handled by patient response or escalation.                                    |
| **G28: Ask Substitute Assessment Questions**         | Ask questions related to the failed vital sign and record the responses.                                                   |                                                                 `AT28;AT29` | Perform   | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | AND      | This is a concrete fallback interaction.                                                                    |
| **G29: Alert Nurse or Doctor on Non-Response**       | Notify the responsible nurse or doctor if the patient does not respond.                                                    |                                                                      `AT30` | Achieve   | Responsible nurse/doctor receives the alert.                                                                                                                                                                              | AND      | The target state is successful clinical escalation.                                                         |
| **G30: Mark Patient as Done**                        | Mark the patient check as completed and proceed to the next room.                                                          |                                                                      `AT31` | Achieve   | Patient status is marked done in the mission system.                                                                                                                                                                      | AND      | The desired state is that the patient is no longer pending.                                                 |
| **G31: Apply Required Hygiene Actions**              | Perform sterilization and/or actuator disinfection after leaving the patient room when required.                           |                                                                 `G32 # G33` | Achieve   | Required hygiene actions are completed based on infection/contact conditions.                                                                                                                                             | AND      | The target state is hygiene compliance before continuing.                                                   |
| **G32: Sterilize After Infectious Room**             | Sterilize the robot after leaving a room with a patient subject to infectious disease.                                     |                                                                      `AT32` | Achieve   | Robot sterilized if infectious-room condition is true.                                                                                                                                                                    | AND      | The goal brings about the required sterilized state.                                                        |
| **G33: Disinfect Actuators After Contact**           | Disinfect actuators after duties involving patient contact.                                                                |                                                                      `AT33` | Achieve   | Contact actuators disinfected if patient contact occurred.                                                                                                                                                                | AND      | The goal brings about the required disinfected state.                                                       |
| **G34: Close Cycle Report**                          | Update the mission system after all patients in the cycle have been processed.                                             |                                                                      `AT34` | Perform   | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | AND      | This is an operational reporting action.                                                                    |
| **G35: Alert Site Manager of Mission Impossibility** | Alert the responsible site manager if no combination of robots can complete the mission.                                   |                                                                      `AT35` | Achieve   | Responsible manager receives an infeasibility alert.                                                                                                                                                                      | AND      | The goal targets an escalation state when completion is impossible.                                         |

## Task Nodes

| Name                                       | Text                                                                                                                   | Relation | Location                                               | Number of Robots |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------: |
| **AT1: Retrieve Occupied-Room List**       | Retrieve all occupied rooms, assigned patients, and bed information.                                                   | AND      | Hospital information system / ward                     |                1 |
| **AT2: Estimate Mission Load**             | Estimate travel time, per-room task time, patient condition tasks, battery demand, and deadline feasibility.           | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |                1 |
| **AT3: Assign Current Robots**             | Assign occupied rooms and patients to the currently available robot team.                                              | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT4: Detect Current-Team Infeasibility** | Determine that the current robot team cannot complete the mission within required time.                                | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |                1 |
| **AT5: Assign Helper Robots**              | Allocate additional available robots to help complete the mission.                                                     | AND      | Hospital / robot staging area                          |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT6: Reassign Pending Tasks**            | Redistribute room and patient tasks among the expanded robot team.                                                     | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT7: Retrieve Patient Priority Data**    | Retrieve patient risk level, infectious disease status, post-surgical condition, diabetes history, and room occupancy. | AND      | Hospital information system                            |                1 |
| **AT8: Order High-Risk Patients First**    | Place all high-risk patients in the first 15 minutes of the mission schedule.                                          | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |                1 |
| **AT9: Publish Robot Work Queues**         | Send ordered room/patient queues to assigned robots.                                                                   | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT10: Check Patient Availability**       | Check whether the patient is available for vital-sign checking before room entry.                                      | AND      | Outside assigned patient room                          |                1 |
| **AT11: Schedule Five-Minute Return**      | Requeue the unavailable patient for a return attempt after 5 minutes.                                                  | AND      | Outside assigned patient room / mission-control system |                1 |
| **AT12: Return After Five Minutes**        | Navigate back to the room after the 5-minute delay.                                                                    | AND      | Hospital corridor / assigned room                      |                1 |
| **AT13: Recheck Availability**             | Recheck whether the patient is now available.                                                                          | AND      | Outside assigned patient room                          |                1 |
| **AT14: Check Battery Sufficiency**        | Compare current battery level against estimated energy needed for all tasks inside the room.                           | AND      | Outside assigned patient room                          |                1 |
| **AT15: Navigate to Charger**              | Move to an available charging station when battery is insufficient.                                                    | AND      | Hospital charging station                              |                1 |
| **AT16: Recharge Robot**                   | Recharge the robot to a level sufficient for reassigned duties.                                                        | AND      | Hospital charging station                              |                1 |
| **AT17: Trigger Reassignment**             | Notify the mission system that pending tasks must be reassigned due to battery insufficiency.                          | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |                1 |
| **AT18: Enter Occupied Room**              | Enter the assigned occupied room after availability and battery checks pass.                                           | AND      | Assigned patient room                                  |                1 |
| **AT19: Ask Patient Identity**             | Ask the patient to confirm identity.                                                                                   | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT20: Verify Spoken Identity**           | Match the provided identity against the assigned patient record.                                                       | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT21: Scan Patient Barcode**             | Scan the patient barcode for identification.                                                                           | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT22: Verify Barcode Identity**          | Match the barcode result against the assigned patient record.                                                          | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT23: Approach Correct Patient**         | Move to the correct bed/patient after identification.                                                                  | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT24: Announce Procedure**               | Announce the vital-sign procedure to be performed.                                                                     | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT25: Provide Patient Instructions**     | Give instructions needed for the vital-sign procedure.                                                                 | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT26: Determine Required Sign Set**      | Determine which vital signs are required from the patient’s condition profile.                                         | AND      | Hospital information system / bedside interface        |                1 |
| **AT27: Measure and Record Vital Sign**    | Measure the required vital sign and record the result.                                                                 | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT28: Ask Failure-Assessment Questions** | Ask clinical fallback questions for any vital sign that could not be collected.                                        | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT29: Record Assessment Responses**      | Record the patient’s responses to fallback questions.                                                                  | AND      | Assigned patient room / bedside                        |                1 |
| **AT30: Send Nurse/Doctor Alert**          | Alert the responsible nurse or doctor if the patient does not respond.                                                 | AND      | Hospital communication system                          |                1 |
| **AT31: Mark Patient Done**                | Mark the patient check as completed in the mission system.                                                             | AND      | Hospital information system                            |                1 |
| **AT32: Sterilize Robot**                  | Sterilize the robot after leaving an infectious-disease room.                                                          | AND      | Hospital sterilization station / corridor checkpoint   |                1 |
| **AT33: Disinfect Actuators**              | Disinfect actuators after any patient-contact duty.                                                                    | AND      | Hospital room exit / disinfection station              |                1 |
| **AT34: Submit Cycle Completion Report**   | Submit the final report for the completed two-hour checking cycle.                                                     | AND      | Hospital mission-control system                        |                1 |
| **AT35: Notify Site Manager**              | Notify the responsible site manager that no combination of robots can complete the mission.                            | AND      | Hospital communication system                          |                1 |
