Below is a goal model decomposition for the multi-robot hospital vital-signs mission. I use parameterized variables such as `occupied_rooms`, `prioritized_room_queue`, `current_room`, `current_patient`, `required_signs`, `failed_signs`, `patient_available`, `battery_sufficient`, and `high_risk_patients`.
