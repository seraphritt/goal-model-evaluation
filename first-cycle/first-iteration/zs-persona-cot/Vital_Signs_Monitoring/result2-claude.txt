# Goal Model: Periodic Vital-Signs Round in a Hospital (Multi-Robot System)

## Modeling notes and assumptions

- **Goal IDs** (G1…G42) and **task IDs** (AT1…AT27) follow depth-first order.
- **Leaf goals** use "-" as runtime and are operationalized by tasks.
- **Relation column:** for a goal, it is the decomposition over its children. For a task, it is the decomposition it takes part in under its parent goal.
- **Per-instance goals:** G6 is instantiated for each room in `RoomQueue`, and G16 for each patient/bed in the room.
- **Conditional goals:** goals guarded by a condition (e.g., "if X ∈ RequiredSigns") are trivially satisfied when the condition is false.
- **Availability check:** this happens before entering the room, so it uses the patient record or bedside/door interaction, not the barcode.
- **Retry after unavailability:** the description does not say what happens after repeated unavailability. The model keeps retrying, and the time-feasibility goal G39 covers deadline risk.
- **Fleet size:** up to 3 additional robots (`[1,3]`) is my assumption.

| G1: Complete vital-signs round | Every 2 hours, check the vital signs of all patients in occupied rooms | G2 ; G5 | Achieve — Target: `∀ patient ∈ occupiedRooms: status = Checked` within the 2-hour cycle | AND |

Justification: the mission specifies a world state to reach (all patients checked) in each cycle.
