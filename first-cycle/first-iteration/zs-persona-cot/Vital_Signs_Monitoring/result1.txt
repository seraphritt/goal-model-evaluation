Below is a structured goal model for the multi-robot hospital vital-signs mission. I treat conditional branches such as “infectious disease,” “patient contact,” and “measurement failure” as **activated only when the parent goal monitors that condition as true**.

## Monitored Mission Variables

`occupied_rooms`, `assigned_room`, `assigned_patient`, `patient_condition`, `risk_level`, `required_vital_signs`, `patient_available`, `battery_sufficient`, `available_robots`, `mission_deadline`, `high_risk_deadline`, `collection_failure`, `failed_vital_signs`, `patient_response`, `infectious_room_exit`, `patient_contact`.
