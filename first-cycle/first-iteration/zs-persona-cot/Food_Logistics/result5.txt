## 4. Logical relationships

**G1 -> G2, G15, G21 [AND][(G2#G15)#G21]**  
Justification: The full mission includes food delivery, dirty-dish retrieval, and door-opening support. These are complementary service capabilities. They may occur in parallel across different requests or rooms.

**G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G6 [AND][(((G3;G4);G5);G6)]**  
Justification: A delivery mission starts with a kitchen request, then needs tray-retrieval context, then physical transport, then final handover.

**G5 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][AT1;AT2]**  
Justification: The robot must first load the meal at the kitchen and then carry it to the inpatient room.

**G6 -> G7, G8 [OR][G7#G8]**  
Justification: The food can be delivered either by placing it on the room table or by having it fetched from the robot tray. These are alternative ways to satisfy handover.

**G7 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][AT3;AT4]**  
Justification: Table delivery requires the robot to position near the table and then place the meal using manipulation capability.

**G8 -> G9, G14 [AND][G9;G14]**  
Justification: Tray fetching requires a cooperating retriever and also requires validation, tracking, and wrong-meal alerting.

**G9 -> G10, G11, G12, G13 [OR][(((G10#G11)#G12)#G13)]**  
Justification: The possible tray retrievers are alternatives: inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot.

**G10 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][AT5;AT6]**  
Justification: If the inpatient retrieves the meal, the robot must identify the correct meal and supervise retrieval.

**G11 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][AT7;AT8]**  
Justification: If the companion retrieves the meal, the robot must identify the correct meal and supervise retrieval.

**G12 -> AT9, AT10 [AND][AT9;AT10]**  
Justification: If the nurse retrieves the meal, the robot must identify the correct meal and supervise retrieval.

**G13 -> AT11, AT12 [AND][AT11;AT12]**  
Justification: If another robot retrieves the meal, the delivery robot and cooperating robot must coordinate and complete the tray retrieval.

**G14 -> AT13, AT14, AT15 [AND][FALLBACK(AT13,AT15);AT14]**  
Justification: The normal case is correct-meal verification. If that fails because the wrong meal is retrieved, the robot alerts. The retrieval must still be recorded with time and location.

**G15 -> G16, G17 [AND][G16;G17]**  
Justification: Dirty-dish retrieval needs pickup context/request information and then an actual retrieval mode.

**G17 -> G18, G19, G20 [OR][((G18#G19)#G20)]**  
Justification: Dish retrieval has three alternatives: unassisted, two-robot cooperation, or robot-human cooperation.

**G18 -> AT16, AT17 [AND][AT16;AT17]**  
Justification: Unassisted retrieval requires collecting the dishes and confirming they are removed.

**G19 -> AT18, AT19 [AND][AT18;AT19]**  
Justification: Two-robot retrieval requires coordination and joint retrieval execution.

**G20 -> AT20, AT21 [AND][AT20;AT21]**  
Justification: Robot-human retrieval requires coordination with the human and then physical retrieval or handoff of dishes.

**G21 -> G22, G23 [OR][G22#G23]**  
Justification: Door opening can be supported either by a human or another robot.

**G22 -> AT22, AT23 [AND][AT22;AT23]**  
Justification: Human door opening requires knowing whether the human can open the door and coordinating the opening.

**G23 -> AT24, AT25 [AND][AT24;AT25]**  
Justification: Robot-assisted door opening requires requesting a cooperating robot and executing the opening action.
