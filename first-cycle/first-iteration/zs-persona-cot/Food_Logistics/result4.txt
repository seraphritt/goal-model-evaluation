**Justification:** The system must support both food delivery and dirty dish retrieval. They are both part of the inpatient meal service. The description does not say dish retrieval must wait for a specific delivery order, and someone in the room can independently call a robot to pick up dishes, so these objectives may be pursued in parallel.
