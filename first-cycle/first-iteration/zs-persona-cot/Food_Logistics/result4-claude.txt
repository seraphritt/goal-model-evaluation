# Goal Model: Multi-Robot Inpatient Meal Delivery and Dish Retrieval

**Conventions used**
- IDs follow depth-first order (G1, G2, ...; AT1, AT2, ...).
- For goals, *Relation* is the goal's own decomposition over its children.
- For tasks, *Relation* is the decomposition of the parent goal that the task belongs to.
- Every goal here is refined by sub-goals or tasks, so no goal is a leaf. The "-" runtime is therefore not used.
- Combined operators are parenthesized.

---

## 1. Goals

| Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type / Target Condition or Enquired Information | Justification | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1: Provide Meal Service** | Deliver ordered meals to inpatient rooms and retrieve their dirty dishes. | G2 # G15 | **Achieve.** Target: every requested meal is delivered and every requested set of dirty dishes is retrieved. | The mission defines a desired end state (meals delivered, dishes removed), not just activities. | AND |
| **G2: Deliver Meal Order** | Handle one kitchen delivery request, order by order, from the kitchen to the inpatient. | G3 ; (G4 # G5) ; G9 | **Achieve.** Target: the meal of the order is in the room, on the table or retrieved from the tray by the right person. | Success is a world state, not merely running a plan. | AND |

| **G4: Assess Retrieval Feasibility** | Estimate who can retrieve the meal from the tray. | (AT3 # AT4 # AT5) ; AT6 | **Query.** Enquired information: (a) whether the inpatient can retrieve from the tray, (b) whether a companion is present and able, (c) whether a nurse is available, each with a confidence level. | The description says this information comes from the inpatient record and is uncertain, so the goal only retrieves and estimates information. | AND |
| **G5: Access Inpatient Room** | Reach the room and get inside. | AT7 ; G6 ; AT12 | **Achieve.** Target: robot is inside the inpatient room (`robot_in_room = true`). | The door may be closed, so the state "inside the room" must be brought about. | AND |
| **G6: Open Room Door** | Get the door opened, cooperating with a robot or a human. | FALLBACK(G7, G8) | **Achieve.** Target: `room_door = open`. | Door opening can involve robot or human cooperation, and only the resulting door state matters. | OR |
| **G7: Open Door with Robot** | Open the door with another robot's help. | AT8 ; AT9 | **Achieve.** Target: door opened by the cooperating robot and held for passage. | The cooperating robot is the first, least intrusive alternative. | AND |
| **G8: Open Door with Human** | Ask a human (nurse, companion, inpatient) to open the door. | AT10 ; AT11 | **Achieve.** Target: door opened by a human. | Human help is the fallback when no robot is available. | AND |

| **G10: Deliver Meal to Table** | Place the meal on the room table with a manipulation-capable robot. | AT13 ; AT14 | **Achieve.** Target: meal is on the room table. | The end state is the meal on the table. | AND |

| **G13: Indicate Meal to Retrieve** | Show which meal on the tray belongs to this inpatient. | AT19 ; AT20 | **Perform.** | The outcome depends only on the indication activity being carried out, not on a verifiable state. | AND |
| **G14: Monitor Meal Retrieval** | Track retrieval and alert on wrong meals. | AT21 # AT22 | **Perform.** | Monitoring is a continuous activity with no target state. | AND |
| **G15: Retrieve Dirty Dishes** | Remove dirty dishes from the room after a pick-up call and take them away. | G16 ; G17 ; G22 ; AT36 | **Achieve.** Target: dishes are out of the room and returned to the kitchen. | Dishes must be retrieved, so there is a target state. | AND |
| **G16: Receive Pick-up Request** | Get the pick-up call and its context. | AT23 ; AT24 | **Query.** Enquired information: requesting room, caller identity and contact. | The goal only obtains information about the request. | AND |

| **G18: Open Door for Dishes** | Get the door opened, preferring the caller. | FALLBACK(G19, FALLBACK(G20, G21)) | **Achieve.** Target: `room_door = open`. | The caller may signal they can open it. Robot and human help are fallbacks. | OR |
| **G19: Open Door by Caller** | The caller signals if they can open the door when the robot arrives, and opens it. | AT26 ; AT27 | **Achieve.** Target: door opened by the caller. | The description gives the caller a specific role in door opening. | AND |
| **G20: Open Door with Robot** | Open the door with another robot's help. | AT28 ; AT29 | **Achieve.** Target: door opened by the cooperating robot. | Same rationale as G7. | AND |
| **G21: Open Door with Human** | Ask another human to open the door. | AT30 ; AT31 | **Achieve.** Target: door opened by a human. | Same rationale as G8. | AND |
| **G22: Collect Dirty Dishes** | Take the dishes unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human. | FALLBACK(AT33, FALLBACK(AT34, AT35)) | **Achieve.** Target: dirty dishes are on the robot tray(s). | Dishes must end up on the robot. The three modes are alternatives. | OR |

## 5. Modeling Assumptions

- The door-opening subtree is duplicated for delivery (G6) and retrieval (G18) because a goal tree cannot share nodes. Retrieval adds the caller-signal option (G19), as the description requires.
- The default order of the G12 fallback chain (inpatient, companion, nurse, robot) can be re-ranked at runtime from G4's uncertain estimates.
- AT36 (return dishes to the kitchen) is my addition to complete the retrieval. The description only says dishes must be retrieved from the room.
- Dish retrieval is modeled as triggered by a pick-up call (G16), since that is the only trigger the description gives.
