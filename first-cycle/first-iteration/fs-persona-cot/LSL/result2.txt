## 1. High-level decomposition rationale

I model the mission as a secure hospital sample-delivery workflow involving human actors, a mobile delivery robot with secure locked drawers, a laboratory robotic arm, and the tracking system.

Key interpretation decisions:

1. **Main mission objective**: samples must move from **patient floors** to the **laboratory**, then be prepared for analysis.
2. **Mandatory phases** are modeled with **AND**: identification, registration, secure transport, lab intake, logging, and timing compliance are all required.
3. **Alternatives explicitly stated** are modeled with **OR**:
   - Locked drawers may be opened by **authorized personnel** or by **specific robots in specific locations**.
   - Samples may be picked in the lab by a **robotic arm** or by **laboratory personnel**.
4. **No FALLBACK is used** because the description gives alternatives but does not specify “try A, and if A fails then try B.”
5. **Parallelism** is used for monitoring/audit functions because secure logging and time tracking can happen alongside the physical workflow.
6. Human-only activities use **Number of Robots = 0**. Robot-executed activities use **1** robot. Activities that may be produced either by a robot or a human-triggered system use **[0,1]**.

## 5. Logical relationships and justifications

- **G1 -> G2, G5, G9, G14 [AND][(G2;G5;G9)#G14]**  
  - **Justification:** The overall mission requires sample preparation, secure transport, laboratory intake, and compliance monitoring. Preparation, transport, and lab processing are sequential. Audit/timing monitoring can occur in parallel with the physical workflow.

- **G2 -> G3, G4 [AND][G3;G4]**  
  - **Justification:** A sample must first be barcode-identified/collected, then registered and associated with a delivery request.

- **G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][AT1;AT2]**  
  - **Justification:** The sample must be identified by barcode before collection is completed.

- **G4 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][AT3;AT4]**  
  - **Justification:** The nurse must insert sample data into the track system and request delivery to the laboratory.

- **G5 -> G6, G7, G8 [AND][G6;G7;G8]**  
  - **Justification:** Secure transport requires the sample to be secured in a locked drawer, delivered to the laboratory, and opened only under authorized conditions.

- **G6 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][AT5;AT6]**  
  - **Justification:** The sample must be received into the secure drawer and the drawer must be locked for transport.

- **G7 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][AT7;AT8]**  
  - **Justification:** The delivery robot must transport the locked drawer to the laboratory and confirm delivery.

- **G8 -> AT9, AT10 [OR][AT9#AT10]**  
  - **Justification:** The description explicitly permits two drawer-opening alternatives: authorized personnel or specific robots in specific locations. Either satisfies the goal. No fallback order is described.

- **G9 -> G10, G11, G12, G13 [AND][G10;G11;G12;G13]**  
  - **Justification:** Laboratory intake requires picking the sample, scanning the barcode, sorting the sample, and loading it into the entry-module.

- **G10 -> AT11, AT12 [OR][AT11#AT12]**  
  - **Justification:** The sample can be picked by either the robotic arm or laboratory personnel. This is an explicit OR alternative.

- **G11 -> AT13, AT14 [AND][AT13;AT14]**  
  - **Justification:** Barcode information must be scanned and then associated with the track-system record.

- **G12 -> AT15, AT16 [AND][AT15;AT16]**  
  - **Justification:** Sorting requires the robotic arm to sort the sample and position it for loading.

- **G13 -> AT17, AT18 [AND][AT17;AT18]**  
  - **Justification:** Loading requires moving the sorted sample to the entry-module and placing it into the module.

- **G14 -> G15, G16 [AND][G15#G16]**  
  - **Justification:** Secure pickup logging and collection-to-delivery timing checks are both required. They can be performed in parallel because they monitor different compliance aspects.

- **G15 -> AT19, AT20, AT21 [AND][(AT19#AT20);AT21]**  
  - **Justification:** The system must capture both pickup location and pickup time, then securely write the log.

- **G16 -> AT22, AT23, AT24 [AND][AT22;AT23;AT24]**  
  - **Justification:** To verify laboratory protocol timing, the system needs collection time, delivery time, and elapsed-time comparison against protocols.
