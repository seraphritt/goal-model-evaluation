# Goal Model: Multi-Robot Patient Vital Signs Monitoring Mission

## Goal Decomposition Overview

The mission is decomposed into four major objectives:

1. **Prepare the mission**: obtain information, prioritize critical patients, and allocate robots.
2. **Execute patient checks**: visit rooms, identify patients, and collect required vital signs.
3. **Manage exceptions and safety**: handle failures, sterilization, and contact safety.
4. **Guarantee mission completion**: ensure additional robots or escalation when the deadline cannot be achieved.
