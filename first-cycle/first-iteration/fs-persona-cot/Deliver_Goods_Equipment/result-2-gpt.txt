## 1. Modeling interpretation

The mission’s top objective is: **collect required resources from storage and deliver them to the requesting agent at a specified location**. Texto colado

The mission has three major phases:

1. **Planning and allocation**: identify resources, storage places, route/order, and robot assignment.
2. **Collection phase**: go to storage, request resources, and wait until they are retrieved. Texto colado
3. **Delivery phase**: perform as many runs as necessary to deliver all resources. Texto colado

The mission also includes contingency behavior for low battery and failure cases: collection low battery at **10%**, delivery low battery at **30%**, checkpoint handoff, reassignment, alerting, and reporting. Texto colado

# 5. Logical relationships

## G1 → G2, G6, G10 **[AND] [sequential]**

**Runtime:** `G2;(G6;G10)`

**Justification:** The mission must first be planned, then resources must be collected, and only after retrieval does the delivery phase begin. The description explicitly says delivery begins once resources are retrieved. Texto colado

---

## G2 → G3, G4, G5 **[AND] [sequential]**

**Runtime:** `G3;G4;G5`

**Justification:** The robot must know what resources are required, where they can be found, how to order storage visits, and which robot or robots will execute the mission. The storage order is based on waiting time plus path estimation. Texto colado

---

## G4 → AT1, AT2 **[AND] [sequential]**

**Runtime:** `AT1;AT2`

**Justification:** The visit order depends on first estimating waiting/path cost and then producing the storage order. This follows directly from the description’s ordering rule. Texto colado

## G5 → AT3, AT4 **[OR] [selection]**

**Runtime:** single assignment or parallel assignment.

**Justification:** One robot can execute the basic mission, but when multiple items from different storages are required, multiple robots can be assigned to parallel collect-deliver tasks. Therefore, AT3 and AT4 are alternatives depending on mission conditions. Texto colado

**Justification:** Normal collection is attempted first. If collection cannot continue because the assigned robot reaches 10% battery, the mission switches to the low-battery handoff behavior: return to recharge and assign the mission to another robot. Texto colado
