Here is the goal model extracted from the pasted mission description. I used fallback only where the text explicitly gives an exception/condition: the wrong-meal alert. Other alternatives are modeled as OR choices because no attempt order is specified.

## 1. Modeling Rationale

The mission has two major service objectives: deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room in response to kitchen requests, and retrieve dirty dishes from the room. The delivery can be performed either by placing food on the room table with a manipulation-capable robot, or by having the food fetched from the robot tray through cooperation with a human or another robot.

The model also includes uncertainty about whether the inpatient, companion, or nurse can retrieve the food, because the description says this information comes from inpatient records and room companion presence and is uncertain.

The wrong-meal case is modeled with `FALLBACK(AT15, AT16)` because the mission explicitly says the robot should alert if the wrong meal is retrieved. Dirty-dish retrieval and door opening are modeled with OR alternatives, because the description lists possible cooperation modes without specifying a required execution order.

## 5. Logical Relationships and Justifications

**G1 -> G2, G3, G12 [AND][sequential: `G2;G3;G12`]**  
- **Justification:** The mission starts with a kitchen delivery request, proceeds to food delivery, and also requires dirty-dish retrieval. These are complementary outcomes, not alternatives.

**G2 -> AT1, AT2, AT3 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2;AT3`]**  
- **Justification:** The system must first receive the request, associate the meal with the correct inpatient/order, and then load the food onto the robot tray.

**G3 -> G4, G6 [AND][sequential: `G4;G6`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot must access the inpatient room before the food can be handed over.

**G4 -> AT4, G5 [AND][sequential: `AT4;G5`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot first navigates to the inpatient room and then resolves door access.

**G5 -> AT5, AT6 [OR][parallel/no fixed order: `AT5#AT6`]**  
- **Justification:** Door opening may involve a human or another robot. Since the description gives no priority order, this is OR rather than fallback.

**G6 -> G7, G8 [OR][parallel/no fixed order: `G7#G8`]**  
- **Justification:** Food can be delivered either to the room table or fetched from the robot tray. Only one delivery mode is necessary.

**G7 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][sequential: `AT7;AT8`]**  
- **Justification:** Table placement requires the manipulation capability before the robot can place the meal on the room table.

**G8 -> G9, G10, G11 [AND][sequential: `G9;G10;G11`]**  
- **Justification:** Tray retrieval requires first assessing who can retrieve, then executing the retrieval, then validating and recording the result.

**G9 -> AT9, AT10 [AND][sequential: `AT9;AT10`]**  
- **Justification:** The system must obtain information from records/presence data and interpret whether a retriever is available, while accounting for uncertainty.

**G10 -> AT11, AT12, AT13, AT14 [OR][parallel/no fixed order: `AT11#AT12#AT13#AT14`]**  
- **Justification:** Any one of the listed cooperating actors can retrieve the food from the tray: inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot.

**G11 -> AT15, AT16, AT17, AT18 [AND][fallback + parallel: `FALLBACK(AT15, AT16);(AT17#AT18)`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot first validates whether the correct meal was retrieved. If validation fails because the wrong meal was retrieved, it alerts. It must also record both time and location.

**G12 -> G13, G14, G16 [AND][sequential: `G13;G14;G16`]**  
- **Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval requires knowing the pickup context, accessing the room, and executing one retrieval mode.

**G13 -> AT19, AT20 [AND][sequential: `AT19;AT20`]**  
- **Justification:** When someone in the room calls for pickup, that person can also signal whether they can open the door.

**G14 -> AT21, G15 [AND][sequential: `AT21;G15`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot must come to the room door before door-opening cooperation can occur.

**G15 -> AT22, AT23 [OR][parallel/no fixed order: `AT22#AT23`]**  
- **Justification:** Door opening may be supported by either a human or another robot, with no explicit ordering.

**G16 -> AT24, AT25, AT26 [OR][parallel/no fixed order: `AT24#AT25#AT26`]**  
- **Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval can occur unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human; one successful mode is sufficient.
