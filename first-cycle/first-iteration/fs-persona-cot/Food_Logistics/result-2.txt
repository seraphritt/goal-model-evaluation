## Logical relationships

**G1 -> G2, G8 [AND][parallel `G2#G8`]**  
- **Justification:** The system must provide both meal delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are not mutually exclusive. They may occur independently for different orders or rooms, so parallel runtime is appropriate.

**G2 -> AT1, G3, AT4, G4 [AND][sequential `AT1;G3;AT4;G4`]**  
- **Justification:** A delivery begins with a kitchen request, then the robot determines delivery context, transports meals, and completes the final room delivery.

**G3 -> AT2, AT3 [AND][parallel `AT2#AT3`]**  
- **Justification:** The system needs both record-based information and current availability/presence information. These can be obtained without a mandatory order.

**G4 -> G5, G6 [OR][parallel/no mandatory order `G5#G6`]**  
- **Justification:** The mission explicitly gives two alternative delivery modes: place food on the room table or have it fetched from the tray.

**G5 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][sequential `AT5;AT6`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot must first reach the table position and then place the meal using manipulation.

**G6 -> AT7, G7, AT12, AT13 [AND][sequential/parallel `AT7;G7;(AT12#AT13)`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot must identify the correct meal, obtain cooperation for retrieval, track the retrieval, and alert if a wrong meal is retrieved.

**G7 -> AT8, AT9, AT10, AT11 [OR][parallel/no mandatory order `AT8#AT9#AT10#AT11`]**  
- **Justification:** Only one valid cooperator is sufficient for tray retrieval: inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot.

**G8 -> G9, G10, G11 [AND][sequential `G9;G10;G11`]**  
- **Justification:** Dish retrieval requires processing the call/context, opening or gaining access through the door, and then removing the dirty dishes.

**G9 -> AT14, AT15 [AND][sequential `AT14;AT15`]**  
- **Justification:** The robot receives the pickup call and obtains the caller’s door-opening signal.

**G10 -> AT16, AT17 [OR][parallel/no mandatory order `AT16#AT17`]**  
- **Justification:** The door may be opened with human cooperation or another robot’s cooperation.

**G11 -> AT18, AT19, AT20 [OR][parallel/no mandatory order `AT18#AT19#AT20`]**  
- **Justification:** Dirty dishes can be retrieved unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human.
