# Goal Model: Hospital Meal Delivery and Dish Retrieval

## 1. Step-by-step reasoning

**Step 1: Extract the activities in the description.**
- Two service flows: (a) deliver meals kitchen → inpatient room, per order; (b) retrieve dirty dishes from the room.
- Delivery has two alternative *modes*: place the meal on the room table (needs a manipulation skill), or leave it on the tray for someone to fetch. Tray retrieval needs a cooperating agent: inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot.
- Dish retrieval has three alternative *modes*: unassisted, two robots, or robot(s) + human.
- Opening the door has two alternatives: with a human or with another robot.
- Cross-cutting concerns: uncertainty about who can retrieve the meal, indicating which meal goes to whom, tracking when and where each meal was retrieved, and alerting on a wrong meal.

**Step 2: Identify goal types.**
- "Find out who can retrieve the meal" and "find out which rooms need pickup" only gather information, so they are **Query** goals.
- "Load meal", "track retrieval" and "close request" are defined by the activities performed, so they are **Perform** goals.
- Everything with a clear end state (door open, meal delivered, dishes returned) is an **Achieve** goal.

**Step 3: Choose decompositions.**
- Alternatives (modes, door opening, retriever choice) → **OR**, with **FALLBACK**, because there is a natural preference order and the uncertainty makes failure plausible.
- Steps that all must happen → **AND**, sequential or parallel depending on data dependency and timing.

**Step 4: Handle uncertainty.**
The capability of inpatient, companion and nurse is uncertain, so it is never assumed. It is queried (G4), used to steer the choice, and backed up by fallbacks (G9, G11).

**Step 5: Multi-robot aspects.**
- Number of robots: 1 when a robot works alone, 2 when two robots physically cooperate.
- The robot carries several meals, so each order is a separate instance of G2 with its own tray slot (AT2).

**Step 6: Numbering.**
IDs are assigned in depth-first order (G1, G2, …, AT1, AT2, …).

**Assumptions:**
- "Relation" is the relation (AND/OR) of the node with its parent.
- Tasks are atomic (single-step), so their own decomposition is trivially AND.
- Variables such as `kitchen`, `room_door`, `inpatient_room`, `room_table`, `ward_record` and `dish_return` are monitored by the goal that owns the task.
- Goals whose children are tasks still receive a runtime annotation, since it structures those children.

## 5. Logical relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G15 [AND][parallel #]**
  - **Justification**: Both services are mandatory. Delivery is driven by kitchen requests and pickup by room calls, so neither waits for the other.

- **G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G9, G14 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The meal must be loaded first. Ability to retrieve must be known before choosing how to hand over. The robot must be in the room to hand over, and the request is closed last.

- **G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Registration to a tray slot requires the meal to be picked up first.

- **G4 -> AT3, AT4 [AND][parallel #]**
  - **Justification**: Patient capability and companion/nurse presence are independent record lookups, and both are needed for a reliable estimate under uncertainty.

- **G5 -> AT5, G6, AT10 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must reach the door, get it opened, and then pass through.

- **G6 -> G7, G8 [OR][FALLBACK(G7,G8)]**
  - **Justification**: Door opening can be done with a human or with another robot. One suffices. The human is tried first, guided by G4 (whether someone able is present), and the helper robot is the backup.

- **G7 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The request must precede verification of the outcome.

- **G8 -> AT8, AT9 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The helper robot can only open the door after arriving.

- **G9 -> G10, G13 [OR][FALLBACK(G10,G13)]**
  - **Justification**: Delivery is either via tray retrieval or table placement. The tray is tried first (no special skill needed) and the table is the fallback if nobody can retrieve. Table delivery does not depend on human ability, which makes it robust to the uncertainty.

- **G10 -> G11, G12 [AND][parallel #]**
  - **Justification**: A meal counts as delivered only when the correct meal was retrieved and tracked. Tracking is concurrent with retrieval.

- **G11 -> AT11, AT12, AT13, AT14 [OR][FALLBACK(AT11, FALLBACK(AT12, FALLBACK(AT13, AT14)))]**
  - **Justification**: Any one of the four retrievers suffices. The order follows increasing disturbance and cost: the recipient first, then companion, then nurse (limited time), then another robot.

- **G12 -> AT15, AT16, AT17 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The retrieval must be detected and logged, then compared with the assigned meal, and only then can a wrong-meal alert be raised.

- **G13 -> AT18, AT19, AT20 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Move next to the table, place the meal with the manipulation skill, then log where and when it was delivered.

- **G14 -> AT21, AT22 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Leave the room first, then report the status to the kitchen.

- **G15 -> G16, G17, G21, G25 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The pickup need must be known before going. The robot must be in the room to collect, and dishes are returned last.

- **G16 -> AT23, AT24 [OR][parallel #]**
  - **Justification**: A room call or the delivery log can each trigger a pickup, and both channels are monitored concurrently.

- **G17 -> AT25, G18, AT30 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Approach, open the door, then enter.

- **G18 -> G19, G20 [OR][FALLBACK(G19,G20)]**
  - **Justification**: The caller can signal that they can open the door, which makes the human the first choice. If nobody can, another robot helps.

- **G19 -> AT26, AT27 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Ask or use the signal, then verify.

- **G20 -> AT28, AT29 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The helper arrives, then opens.

- **G21 -> G22, G23, G24 [OR][FALLBACK(G22, FALLBACK(G23,G24))]**
  - **Justification**: The description lists three alternative modes (unassisted, two robots, robots + human). Cost order: unassisted, then a second robot, then a human.

- **G22 -> AT31, AT32 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Move to the dishes, then load.

- **G23 -> AT33, AT34 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The second robot must arrive before the joint transfer.

- **G24 -> AT35, AT36 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: The human is asked, then the loading is confirmed.

- **G25 -> AT37, AT38 [AND][sequential ;]**
  - **Justification**: Travel to the return point, then unload.
