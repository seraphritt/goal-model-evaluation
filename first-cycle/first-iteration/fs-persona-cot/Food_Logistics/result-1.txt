## 4. Logical relationships and justifications

**G1 -> G2, G5, G20 [AND] [sequential: `G2;G5;G20`]**  
**Justification:** The complete service requires processing the request, delivering the meal, and retrieving dishes. Delivery is request-based, and dish retrieval is a required part of the mission. Texto colado

**G2 -> G3, G4 [AND] [sequential: `G3;G4`]**  
**Justification:** The robot must first obtain the delivery request and then identify the meal and destination needed for delivery.

**G5 -> G6, G7, G10 [AND] [sequential: `G6;G7;G10`]**  
**Justification:** A successful delivery requires moving the food to the room, gaining room access, and transferring the food.

**G7 -> G8, G9 [OR] [parallel/unordered alternatives: `G8#G9`]**  
**Justification:** Door opening may involve another robot or a human; either option can satisfy room access. Texto colado

**G10 -> G11, G12 [OR] [parallel/unordered alternatives: `G11#G12`]**  
**Justification:** The food can either be placed on the room table by a robot with special manipulation ability or fetched from the robot tray. Texto colado

**G12 -> G13, G14, G19 [AND] [sequential: `G13;G14;G19`]**  
**Justification:** Tray retrieval requires first determining who can retrieve the food, then executing the retrieval cooperation, and then ensuring correct meal identity and logging. The ability information is uncertain, so it must be queried before relying on a human retriever. Texto colado

**G14 -> G15, G16, G17, G18 [OR] [parallel/unordered alternatives: `G15#G16#G17#G18`]**  
**Justification:** Tray fetching can be done through cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot. Any one sufficient capable actor completes the retrieval. Texto colado

**G19 -> AT12, AT13, AT14, AT15 [AND] [sequential: `AT12;AT13;AT14;AT15`]**  
**Justification:** Because the robot can carry multiple meals, it must indicate the correct meal, monitor the retrieval, alert if the wrong meal is retrieved, and track time/location. Texto colado

**G20 -> G21, G22, G25 [AND] [sequential: `G21;G22;G25`]**  
**Justification:** Dirty-dish retrieval starts from a pickup request, requires access to the room, and then requires the dishes to be removed. Texto colado

**G21 -> AT16, AT17 [AND] [sequential: `AT16;AT17`]**  
**Justification:** Someone in the room can call for pickup and can signal whether they can open the door when the robot arrives. Texto colado

**G22 -> G23, G24 [OR] [parallel/unordered alternatives: `G23#G24`]**  
**Justification:** Door opening can involve either another robot or a human, so either is sufficient for access. Texto colado

**G25 -> G26, G27, G28 [OR] [parallel/unordered alternatives: `G26#G27#G28`]**  
**Justification:** Dirty dishes can be retrieved unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human. Texto colado
