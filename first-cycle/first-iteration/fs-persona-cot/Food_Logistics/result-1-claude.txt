# Goal Model: Food Delivery and Dish Retrieval in a Hospital

## 1. Step-by-step reasoning

**Step 1: Identify the top-level intentions.** The mission has two independent service flows. Food goes to the room (triggered by kitchen delivery requests) and dirty dishes come back (triggered by a call or after a meal). They do not need each other's completion, so they are parallel children of the root.

**Step 2: Decompose delivery in temporal order.** Per order the robot must:
1. Understand the request.
2. Load the meals.
3. Know who, if anyone, can retrieve food from the tray.
4. Get into the room, which may need help with the door.
5. Hand over the meal.
6. Close the order.

These steps depend on each other, so they are sequential.

**Step 3: Find the variation points (OR decompositions).**
- Delivery to the table (needs a manipulation skill) or via the tray (needs cooperation). The tray is tried first because it needs no special skill, and the table is the fallback.
- Tray retrieval by the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot.
- Door opening by a human or another robot.
- Dish collection unassisted, with a human, or with two robots.
- The dish-pickup trigger: a call or a schedule.

Each is an OR, and where I impose a preference it is a FALLBACK.

**Step 4: Handle uncertainty.** The information on who can retrieve the meal is uncertain. Two mechanisms address this:
- A query goal (G5) obtains the information and estimates its confidence.
- Every cooperation-dependent option sits in a FALLBACK chain, so a wrong estimate degrades gracefully instead of failing.

**Step 5: Cover the tracking requirements.** Indicating the meal, logging when and where it was retrieved, and alerting on a wrong meal are in the tray branch. They run in parallel with retrieval, because supervision happens while retrieval is in progress.

**Step 6: Assign tasks, locations and robot counts.** Tasks are operational leaves. Robot counts follow the cooperation described: 1 for solo work, 2 for robot–robot cooperation, and [1,2] where a human helps and a second robot may join.

**Step 7: Reflect on coverage.** The check is in section 6.

### Assumptions
- **Numbering:** IDs follow depth-first order (G1, G2, ...; AT1, AT2, ...).
- **Single-child goals:** Goals with exactly one child get "-" as runtime, since there is no execution structure to specify.
- **Relation column:** For goals, it is the AND/OR decomposition over the goal's own children. For tasks, it is the relation to the parent goal.
- **Fallback order:** The order in each FALLBACK chain (cheapest and least dependent first) is a design choice, not stated in the description. It is easy to reverse.
- **Instances:** G2 and G18 are instantiated per order or request. One robot may serve several orders, which is why the tray branch supports multiple meals.
- **Nurse availability:** I assume it comes from hospital information systems, since the description only says the record covers the patient and companion.

## 5. Logical relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G18 [AND][(parallel) #]**
  - **Justification**: The service needs both flows. They are triggered independently and overlap, so neither waits for the other.

- **G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G8, G10, G17 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: Each step consumes the previous step's output: request, meals, mode decision, room access, handover, and closure.

- **G3 -> AT1 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: Reading the request is the only operational step needed to obtain the order data.

- **G4 -> AT2, AT3 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: The robot must be at the pick-up point before loading and registering the meals.

- **G5 -> G6, G7, AT6 [AND][(G6 # G7) ; AT6]**
  - **Justification**: The two queries are independent and run in parallel. The confidence estimate needs both results. All are needed to decide under uncertainty.

- **G6 -> AT4 [AND]** and **G7 -> AT5 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: Each query is answered by a single record lookup.

- **G8 -> AT7, G9, AT10 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: The robot reaches the door, gets it opened, then enters.

- **G9 -> AT8, AT9 [OR][FALLBACK(AT8, AT9)]**
  - **Justification**: A human or a robot can open the door. Human help is tried first so that no second robot is tied up.

- **G10 -> G11, G16 [OR][FALLBACK(G11, G16)]**
  - **Justification**: Tray and table are alternative ways to deliver. The tray needs no special skill, and the table is the robust fallback.

- **G11 -> AT11, G12, G13 [AND][AT11 ; (G12 # G13)]**
  - **Justification**: The meal is indicated first. Retrieval and supervision then run simultaneously because supervision observes the retrieval. Both are needed for a tracked delivery.

- **G12 -> AT12, AT13, AT14, AT15 [OR][FALLBACK(AT12, FALLBACK(AT13, FALLBACK(AT14, AT15)))]**
  - **Justification**: Any one retriever suffices. The order runs from least to most resource-costly.

- **G13 -> G14, G15 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: The wrong-meal check needs the record of which meal was taken.

- **G14 -> AT16, AT17 [AND][(sequential) ;]** and **G15 -> AT18, AT19 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: Detection precedes recording, and comparison precedes alerting.

- **G16 -> AT20 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: Table delivery is a single manipulation task.

- **G17 -> AT21 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: Reporting is the single closing task.

- **G18 -> G19, G20, G22, G23 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: The need must be known, the room entered, the dishes collected, and then returned.

- **G19 -> AT22, AT23 [OR][(parallel) #]**
  - **Justification**: Either trigger starts a pickup. Both are monitored concurrently.

- **G20 -> AT24, G21, AT28 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: Same access pattern as delivery.

- **G21 -> AT25, AT26, AT27 [OR][FALLBACK(AT25, FALLBACK(AT26, AT27))]**
  - **Justification**: The caller who signaled first, then another human, then a helper robot.

- **G22 -> AT29, AT30, AT31 [OR][FALLBACK(AT29, FALLBACK(AT30, AT31))]**
  - **Justification**: The three collection modes are alternatives, ordered by resource cost.

- **G23 -> AT32, AT33 [AND][(sequential) ;]**
  - **Justification**: The robot travels to the return point, then unloads.
