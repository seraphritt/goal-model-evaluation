As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, I have analyzed the mission description, filtered out human-exclusive actions (adhering to the zero-robot rule), and decomposed the mission into a rigorous, hierarchical goal model. Below is the step-by-step reasoning, followed by the complete goal and task specifications, summary table, and logical relationships.

### Step-by-Step Mission Analysis & Reasoning

1. **Entity and Actor Extraction**:
* **Locations mentioned**: Patient floors, laboratory, track system, entry-module of analysis machines.
* **Robotic Agents**: Mobile transport robots (equipped with secure locked drawers) and a laboratory robotic arm.
* **Filtered Out (Zero-Robot Rule)**: Purely manual nurse collection actions and human-only data entry are excluded as they do not involve robotic execution or direct robot control interfaces.


2. **Top-Level Mission Decomposition**:
* The overarching mission is to transport samples from patient floors to the laboratory, process them via a robotic arm, and securely log/track data. This breaks down into three primary sub-goals:
* **G1.1**: Transporting samples across locations.
* **G1.2**: Handling and processing samples in the laboratory.
* **G1.3**: Logging pickup data and tracking delivery duration.




3. **Runtime & Execution Annotations**:
* **Sequential Execution (`;`)**: Samples must first be secured and transported from patient floors to the lab (`G1.1`) before the robotic arm can pick them up and process them (`G1.2`). Similarly, drawers must be locked before transport (`G1.1.1;G1.1.2`), and drawers must be opened before samples can be picked up (`G1.2.1.1;G1.2.1.2`).
* **Parallel Execution (`#`)**: Logging pickup information (`G1.3.1`) and tracking delivery duration (`G1.3.2`) can occur concurrently as independent monitoring processes.
* **Achieve vs. Perform vs. Query Goals**:
* Achieve goals target a specific world state (e.g., samples arrived, drawers locked).
* Perform goals execute ongoing physical actions (e.g., scanning barcodes, sorting, loading).
* Query goals retrieve information (e.g., tracking delivery time duration).

### Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G1.1, G1.2, G1.3 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The mission requires transporting samples (`G1.1`), processing them in the lab (`G1.2`), and tracking/logging metrics (`G1.3`). Transport must occur sequentially before lab processing, while tracking runs across the lifecycle.


* **G1.1 -> G1.1.1, G1.1.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Drawers must be locked (`G1.1.1`) before the mobile robot initiates transit (`G1.1.2`) to ensure sample security.


* **G1.1 -> AT1, AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Transporting samples requires executing both the locking task (`AT1`) and the navigation/transit task (`AT2`).


* **G1.1.1 -> AT1 [AND]**
* **Justification**: The goal of securing drawers is directly achieved by task `AT1`.


* **G1.1.2 -> AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Navigating and transporting samples to the lab is directly achieved by task `AT2`.


* **G1.2 -> G1.2.1, G1.2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Samples must first be accessed and picked up from the drawers (`G1.2.1`) before they can be scanned, sorted, and loaded into analysis machines (`G1.2.2`).


* **G1.2.1 -> G1.2.1.1, G1.2.1.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The secure drawer must be opened (`G1.2.1.1`) before the robotic arm can pick up the sample (`G1.2.1.2`).


* **G1.2.1 -> AT3, AT4 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Accessing and picking up samples requires unlocking the drawer (`AT3`) and grasping the sample (`AT4`).


* **G1.2.1.1 -> AT3 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Opening the secure locked drawer is directly accomplished by task `AT3`.


* **G1.2.1.2 -> AT4 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Picking up the sample is directly accomplished by task `AT4`.


* **G1.2.2 -> G1.2.2.1, G1.2.2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Barcodes must be scanned and samples sorted (`G1.2.2.1`) before they are loaded into the analysis machines (`G1.2.2.2`).


* **G1.2.2 -> AT5, AT6 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Scanning/sorting (`AT5`) and machine loading (`AT6`) are both required to fulfill laboratory sample processing.


* **G1.2.2.1 -> AT5 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Scanning barcodes and sorting is directly executed by task `AT5`.


* **G1.2.2.2 -> AT6 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Loading samples into analysis machines is directly executed by task `AT6`.


* **G1.3 -> G1.3.1, G1.3.2 [AND][(parallel)]**
