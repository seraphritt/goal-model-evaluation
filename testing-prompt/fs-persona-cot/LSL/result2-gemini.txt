### Step-by-Step Thinking and Reasoning

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, my objective is to translate the natural language mission description into a formal hierarchical goal model.

1. **Filtering Actors & Zero-Robot Constraint**:
The mission description mentions several human actors: nurses collecting samples, requesting delivery, entering track data, and laboratory personnel picking samples. According to the constraint: *"Goals and tasks that are not performed by any robot (zero robot) must not be on the goal model."* Therefore, human-only actions (nurse requests, manual data entry, human laboratory personnel picking samples) are excluded. We focus entirely on the robotic system agents: **Mobile Transport Robots** and **Robotic Arms**.
2. **Identifying Locations**:
All locations referenced in the model are strictly derived from the text:
* `patient floors`
* `laboratory`
* `entry-module of the analysis machines`


3. **Decomposing the Mission into Goals and Tasks (Depth-First Order)**:
* **G1 (Root)**: Overall management of automated sample transport and processing.
* **G2**: Transporting samples from patient floors to the laboratory.
* **G2.1**: Securing locked drawers via authorized robots.
* **AT1**: Unlock drawer at patient floors.


* **G2.2**: Navigating mobile robots between locations.
* **AT2**: Transport mobile robot from patient floors to laboratory.




* **G3**: Processing samples in the laboratory via robotic arm (Pick $\rightarrow$ Scan $\rightarrow$ Sort $\rightarrow$ Load).
* **AT3**: Pick up sample.
* **AT4**: Scan sample barcode.
* **AT5**: Sort samples.
* **AT6**: Load samples into analysis machines.


* **G4**: Logging and tracking sample metadata and delivery time.
* **AT7**: Log pickup location and time.
* **AT8**: Track delivery duration against protocols.




4. **Determining Runtime Annotations & Decompositions**:
* **G1**: Uses parallel execution between transport/processing workflow and logging/tracking: `(G2; G3) # G4`.
* **G2**: Sequential execution (drawer security managed before navigation): `G2.1; G2.2`.
* **G3**: Sequential execution of laboratory tasks: `AT3; AT4; AT5; AT6`.
* **G4**: Parallel execution of logging and tracking: `AT7 # AT8`.

### Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4 [AND][(G2; G3) # G4]**
* **Justification**: The primary mission objective requires transporting samples (G2) and processing them in the laboratory (G3) sequentially, while concurrently logging and tracking data and delivery times (G4) in parallel throughout the execution.


* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][G2.1; G2.2]**
* **Justification**: Securing and unlocking the locked drawers (G2.1) must take place before the mobile robot can successfully navigate and transport the drawers to the laboratory (G2.2).


* **G2.1 -> AT1 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Securing/unlocking drawers is operationalized directly through the drawer unlocking task (AT1) performed by an authorized robot at the patient floor.


* **G2.2 -> AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Navigation and physical movement are operationalized through the transport task (AT2) moving between patient floors and the laboratory.


* **G3 -> AT3, AT4, AT5, AT6 [AND][AT3; AT4; AT5; AT6]**
* **Justification**: Laboratory sample processing follows a strict sequential pipeline mandated by the robotic arm workflow: picking up the sample (AT3), scanning the barcode (AT4), sorting the samples (AT5), and finally loading them into the analysis machine entry-module (AT6).


* **G4 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][AT7 # AT8]**
* **Justification**: Information logging (AT7) and protocol time tracking (AT8) are complementary verification tasks that occur in parallel during sample transit and handling.
