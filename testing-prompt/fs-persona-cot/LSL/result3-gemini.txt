## Mission Analysis & Step-by-Step Thinking

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, analyzing the provided natural language description requires translating high-level operational requirements into a rigorous, hierarchical goal model while filtering out human-only actions (zero-robot entities) per the system constraints.

### 1. Filtering & Entity Identification

* **Human-Only Actions (Excluded):** The description mentions "A nurse responsible for collecting a sample can request a delivery... The nurse inserts data about the sample in a track system" and "the sample can be picked by a robotic arm or laboratory personnel." Since nurses and human laboratory personnel are zero-robot entities, tasks performed solely by humans (like manual data entry by nurses or manual picking by lab staff) are excluded from the robotic goal model.
* **Robot-Assisted / Automated Actions (Included):**
* Mobile delivery robots transporting secure locked drawers from patient floors to the laboratory.
* Specific authorized robots/mechanisms opening the locked drawers.
* A robotic arm picking up samples, scanning barcodes, sorting, and loading them into analysis machine entry-modules.
* Automated logging of pickup location/time and transit duration tracking.



### 2. Hierarchical Decomposition & Depth-First Numbering

The overall mission objective is to transport and process lab samples using a multi-robot system. Following a strict **depth-first traversal order**:

* **`G1`**: Root goal to transport and process lab samples.
* **`G2`**: Transport secure drawers from patient floors to the laboratory (`AT1`, `AT2`).
* **`G3`**: Process samples in the laboratory via robotic arm.
* **`G4`**: Pick up and scan samples (`AT3`, `AT4`).
* **`G5`**: Sort and load samples into analysis machines (`AT5`, `AT6`).


* **`G6`**: Log and track mission operations securely.
* **`G7`**: Log sample pickup information (`AT7`).
* **`G8`**: Track transit time (`AT8`).
