### Mission Analysis and Step-by-Step Reasoning

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, I have analyzed the natural language mission description for the hospital robot delivery and retrieval system. Here is the step-by-step reasoning behind the goal model decomposition:

1. **Top-Level Decomposition (G1)**:
The overarching mission requires the system to handle two major operational pillars: delivering food from the kitchen to inpatient rooms and retrieving dirty dishes. Since these are distinct operational phases, G1 is decomposed into **G2 (Food Delivery)** and **G3 (Dish Retrieval)** with a sequential execution annotation (`G2;G3`), as food delivery precedes dish retrieval for a given meal cycle.
2. **Food Delivery Decomposition (G2)**:
Delivering food cannot happen blindly. First, the system must acquire information regarding kitchen orders, patient retrieval capabilities, and companion/nurse presence (**G2.1**, a Query Goal). Once information is acquired, the physical delivery and tracking are executed (**G2.2**, an Achieve Goal) in parallel (`G2.2.1#G2.2.2`), where transport/delivery happens alongside tracking and alerting.
3. **Delivery Methods and Fallback/Alternative Logic (G2.2.1)**:
The description outlines two ways to deliver food: table placement (requiring special manipulation skills) or tray fetching (requiring cooperation). Because table delivery is attempted first (normal behavior) and tray fetching serves as an alternative when table manipulation is unfeasible or conditioned by patient capability/availability, this relationship is modeled using a **FALLBACK** operator: `FALLBACK(G2.2.1.1, G2.2.1.2)` under an OR decomposition.
4. **Dish Retrieval Decomposition (G3)**:
Dish retrieval is initiated by a room pickup call (**G3.1**), followed by executing the door opening and dish collection (**G3.2**) with potential multi-robot or human cooperation.
5. **Task Allocation & Locations**:
All locations (`kitchen`, `inpatient room`) are strictly derived from the text. Robot counts are set to `1` for single-agent tasks and `[1, 2]` for tasks explicitly mentioning cooperation with humans or another robot.

* **Justification**: The mission requires both food delivery and subsequent dirty dish retrieval, executed sequentially in operational cycles.


* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Patient records and kitchen orders must be queried first before physical delivery and tracking can take place.

* **G2.2.1.2 -> AT6, AT7 [AND]**
