## Step-by-Step Thinking and Reasoning

1. **Mission Scope and Objectives Analysis**:
* The mission requires all patient vital signs to be checked every two hours across all occupied hospital rooms.
* It involves a multi-robot system where robots navigate rooms, perform pre-entry checks (availability and battery), identify patients (by asking or barcode), interact and collect vital signs based on patient medical conditions (infectious, post-surgical, diabetes, high-risk), handle failures with assessment questions and nurse/doctor alerts, perform post-room sanitization (sterilization and actuator disinfection), and manage overall mission time constraints, robot reassignments, and manager escalations.


2. **Goal Hierarchy & Depth-First Traversal Numbering**:
* **G1**: Execute Patient Monitoring Mission (`G2#G3`)
* **G2**: Perform Room Visit and Patient Vital Signs Monitoring (`G2.1;G2.2;G2.3;G2.4`)
* **G2.1**: Perform Pre-Entry Room Checks (`G2.1.1#G2.1.2`)
* **G2.1.1**: Check Patient Availability (`-`) [Query Goal] -> Tasks: AT1, AT2
* **G2.1.2**: Check Battery Level (`-`) [Query Goal] -> Tasks: AT3, AT4


* **G2.2**: Identify Patient in Room (`G2.2.1#G2.2.2`)
* **G2.2.1**: Identify Patient by Asking (`-`) [Perform Goal] -> Task: AT5
* **G2.2.2**: Identify Patient by Barcode (`-`) [Perform Goal] -> Task: AT6


* **G2.3**: Conduct Patient Interaction and Vital Signs Collection (`G2.3.1;G2.3.2;G2.3.3`)
* **G2.3.1**: Approach and Instruct Patient (`-`) [Perform Goal] -> Tasks: AT7, AT8
* **G2.3.2**: Collect Patient Vital Signs Based on Condition (`-`) [Achieve Goal] -> Tasks: AT9, AT10, AT11, AT12
* **G2.3.3**: Handle Collection Failures and Escalations (`-`) [Achieve Goal] -> Tasks: AT13, AT14


* **G2.4**: Post-Room Procedures and Room Transition (`-`) [Achieve Goal] -> Tasks: AT15, AT16, AT17


* **G3**: Manage Mission Resources, Time constraints, and System Exceptions (`G3.1#G3.2`)
* **G3.1**: Ensure High-Risk Patient Time Constraints and Robot Allocation (`-`) [Achieve Goal] -> Tasks: AT18, AT19
* **G3.2**: Handle Uncompletable Mission Exception (`-`) [Achieve Goal] -> Task: AT20






3. **Runtime Annotations & Decompositions Justifications**:
* **G1**: Uses parallel execution (`G2#G3`) because room monitoring and resource/exception management operate concurrently throughout the mission.
* **G2**: Uses sequential execution (`G2.1;G2.2;G2.3;G2.4`) because a robot must complete pre-entry checks before identifying the patient, interact/collect vital signs after identification, and finish post-room procedures before leaving the room.
* **G2.1**: Uses parallel execution (`G2.1.1#G2.1.2`) as patient availability and battery checks can be assessed together before entry.
* **G2.2**: Uses parallel/either execution (`G2.2.1#G2.2.2`) representing alternative identification methods (asking or barcode).
* **G2.3**: Uses sequential execution (`G2.3.1;G2.3.2;G2.3.3`) because approaching/instructing precedes vital sign collection, which precedes failure handling and escalation.
* **G3**: Uses parallel execution (`G3.1#G3.2`) because time constraint monitoring and exception management run concurrently with robot operations.
