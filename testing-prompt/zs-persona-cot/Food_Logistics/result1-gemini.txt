### Mission Analysis & Reasoning

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems (MRS), analyzing this hospital delivery and retrieval scenario requires translating natural language operational workflows into a formal goal model.

#### Key System Characteristics & Design Decisions:

1. **Dual Mission Services**: The system handles two primary asynchronous responsibilities: **Food Delivery** (prompted by kitchen requests) and **Dirty Dish Retrieval** (prompted by room calls). These operate as parallel or interleaved services (`G1 -> G2 # G7`).
2. **Uncertainty & Querying**: Because patient mobility and companion/nurse availability are uncertain, the system cannot blindly assume a delivery method. It must first execute a **Query Goal** (`G3`) against inpatient records.
3. **Fallback Execution**: The delivery method features a strict fallback hierarchy (`FALLBACK(G5, G6)`). The system first attempts tray fetching with human/robot cooperation (`G5`). If the patient is unable and no companion or nurse is available, it falls back to direct table delivery via specialized robot manipulation (`G6`).
4. **Cooperation & Multi-Robot/Human Interaction**: Several tasks involve variable robot counts (`[1, 2]`), such as tray retrieval cooperation, door opening, and dish collection, reflecting realistic multi-robot and human-robot interaction (HRI) dynamics.
5. **Valid Locations**: All locations referenced (`kitchen`, `inpatient room`) are directly extracted from the mission text. Zero-robot tasks are strictly excluded.

### Goal Model Tables

#### Goal 1: Execute Hospital Food Delivery and Dish Retrieval Mission

* **Name:** `G1` Execute Hospital Mission
* **Text:** The system must successfully deliver meals from the kitchen to inpatient rooms and subsequently retrieve dirty dishes from the rooms.
* **Runtime:** `G2#G7`
* **Goal Type:** Achieve
* **Target Condition:** Food is successfully delivered from the kitchen to inpatient rooms, and dirty dishes are retrieved and returned to the kitchen.
* **Relation:** AND
* **Justification:** The mission description explicitly outlines two core concurrent services—delivering food on an order basis and retrieving dirty dishes—both of which must be fulfilled to complete the mission.

### Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G7 [AND][(parallel)]**
* **Justification**: The hospital mission encompasses both food delivery and dirty dish retrieval as concurrent, ongoing services managed in parallel based on kitchen requests and room calls.


* **G2 -> G3, G4 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The robot must first query the inpatient record to ascertain patient retrieval capabilities before executing the specific meal delivery workflow.


* **G4 -> G5, G6 [AND][FALLBACK(G5, G6)]**
* **Justification**: Tray delivery with cooperation is attempted first; if the patient is unable and no companion or nurse is available, the system falls back to robot table manipulation.


* **G7 -> G8, G9 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The robot must successfully gain entry by opening the room door before it can collect and retrieve dirty dishes from the room.


* **G3 -> AT1 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Querying the inpatient record is directly executed by the query task `AT1`.


* **G5 -> AT2, AT3 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Tray delivery requires both transporting the meal from the kitchen (`AT2`) and cooperative retrieval assistance at the room (`AT3`).


* **G6 -> AT4 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Table delivery is achieved by executing the specialized manipulation task `AT4`.


* **G8 -> AT5 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Room door opening is accomplished through cooperative door opening task `AT5`.


* **G9 -> AT6, AT7 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Dish retrieval requires collecting the dishes from the room (`AT6`) followed by transporting them back to the kitchen (`AT7`).
