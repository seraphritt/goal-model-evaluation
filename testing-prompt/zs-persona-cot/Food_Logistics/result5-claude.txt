# Mission Decomposition: Hospital Meal Delivery & Dish Retrieval Robot

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**1. Identifying the top-level scope.** The description bundles two complementary robot-provided services that are triggered by different events: (a) delivering a kitchen order to an inpatient, triggered by a *kitchen delivery request*, and (b) retrieving dirty dishes, triggered by a *call from someone in the room*. Since both are required capabilities of the system but are independent in time and triggering, I model them as two AND-required branches under one root, related in parallel.

**2. Filtering "zero-robot" content.** The description mentions several purely human actions (an inpatient/companion/nurse physically grabbing food, or a person opening a door themselves). Per the rules, these are *not* modeled as goals/tasks — only the robot-facing counterparts are: querying eligibility, presenting/indicating the meal, cooperating to open the door, etc.

**3. Spotting OR vs AND vs FALLBACK.**
- "delivered to a table **or** fetched from the tray" → explicit alternative → **OR**.
- "cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, **or** another robot" → explicit alternative → **OR**, but I collapsed this into two robot-relevant branches (human-assisted vs. robot-robot), since the three human roles are not robot-distinguishable actions for the robot's task.
- "unassisted, with cooperation of two robots, or with cooperation of robots and a human" → explicit alternative → **OR**.
- "opening the door... with another robot or a human" → explicit alternative → **OR**.
- "The robot should alert **if** a wrong meal is retrieved" → explicit IF-condition describing a normal path (correct retrieval) and an exception path (alert) → **FALLBACK**.
- Determining *who* can retrieve before attempting retrieval, and receiving an order before executing delivery, are not stated as "try A, if it fails do B" — they are explicit narrative/procedural orderings → **sequential (;)**, not fallback.
- Delivery and dish-retrieval, or tracking and verifying, have no stated order and are logically independent → **parallel (#)**.

**4. Classifying goal types.** Goals that purely gather information (inpatient-record check, door-signal check, meal-match check, retrieval logging) are **Query**. Goals that are single, concrete, skill-bound actions (placing food, presenting a tray, cooperating to open a door, alerting, collecting dishes) are **Perform**. Goals that require planning/combining multiple steps to reach a state (deliver the meal, retrieve the dishes, open the door, verify-and-alert) are **Achieve**.

**5. Avoiding redundancy.** "Facilitate retrieval" (the physical cooperation act) is kept distinct from "Deliver meal via tray" (the overall achievement, which also includes determining eligibility) so no two nodes represent the same intention.

## Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G15 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Meal delivery and dish retrieval are both required services of the system, triggered independently (kitchen requests vs. in-room calls), with no stated order between them.

- **G2 -> AT1, G3, G10 [AND] (AT1; then (G3 # G10))**
  - **Justification**: An order must first be received (AT1), after which delivery execution (G3) and retrieval tracking/verification (G10) proceed together, since logging/verifying naturally accompanies the act of delivery.

- **G3 -> G4, G5 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "The food can be delivered into a room table by the robot... or the food can be fetched from the robot's tray" — explicit alternative, either suffices, no stated priority.

- **G5 -> G6, G7 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Eligibility must logically be determined (G6) before the cooperative retrieval can be facilitated (G7); this is a procedural ordering stated by the narrative, not a conditioned failure/retry, hence sequential rather than fallback.

- **G7 -> G8, G9 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot" — human-cooperator cases collapse into G8, robot-cooperator case is G9; either suffices, no stated order.

### A few modeling notes worth flagging
- I treated the three human-cooperator roles (inpatient, companion, nurse) as a *single* robot-facing branch (G8) rather than three separate goals, because from the robot's perspective the action performed (present tray + indicate meal) is identical regardless of which human performs the retrieval — separating them would have been redundant per the no-duplication rule.
- The "robots and a human" wording for dish cooperation (AT16) is the one place where the robot count is genuinely ambiguous in the source text; I inferred 1 robot + 1 human by contrast with the explicitly-numbered "two robots" case, but flagged that inference rather than silently asserting it.
- Door-opening (G17) is explicitly scoped to the dish-retrieval branch only, since that's the only place the source text discusses it — I avoided inventing an equivalent requirement for the delivery branch, since it isn't stated there.
