### Step-by-Step Reasoning and Mission Analysis

1. **Analysis of Human vs. Robot Roles (Zero-Robot Constraint Check)**:
* The mission description mentions nurses collecting samples, inserting data into a tracking system, and laboratory personnel picking samples. Per the modeling rules, *"Goals and tasks that are not performed by any robot (zero robot) must not be on the goal model."* Therefore, human-exclusive activities (nurse sample collection and data entry) are excluded from the multi-robot goal model.
* The active robotic agents identified are:
* **Mobile Delivery Robots**: Responsible for transporting secure locked drawers from patient floors to the laboratory and opening locked drawers at specific authorized locations.
* **Robotic Arm**: Responsible for picking up samples in the laboratory, scanning barcodes, sorting samples, and loading them into the analysis machines.




2. **Hierarchical Decomposition & Goal Types**:
* **Root Goal (G1)**: Overall objective to manage secure transport and laboratory processing of samples. This is an **Achieve Goal** because it targets a final state where samples are successfully delivered and loaded into analysis machines.
* **Sub-goal G2 (Transport)**: Mobile robots must transport samples from patient floors to the laboratory and open the locked drawers. This is decomposed into transporting the drawers (Achieve Goal) and opening the drawers (Perform Goal) sequentially (`G2.1;G2.2`).
* **Sub-goal G3 (Processing)**: The robotic arm must pick up, scan, sort, and load samples. These four steps are executed sequentially (`G3.1;G3.2;G3.3;G3.4`) as Perform Goals.
