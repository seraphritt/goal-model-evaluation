### Step-by-Step Reasoning and Mission Analysis

1. **Filtering Zero-Robot Actions (Human-Only Operations):**
* The mission description mentions actions performed by humans: *"A nurse responsible for collecting a sample can request a delivery"* and *"The nurse inserts data about the sample in a track system"*, as well as *"laboratory personnel"*.
* According to the system rule—*"Goals and tasks that are not performed by any robot (zero robot) must not be on the goal model"*—these pure human-executed actions are excluded from the model.
* We focus exclusively on the capabilities executed by mobile transport robots, robotic arms, secure locking mechanisms managed by specific robots, and automated logging/tracking systems.


2. **Top-Level Mission Decomposition:**
* The primary objective is to automate the transportation and laboratory processing of lab samples (`G1`).
* This breaks down into three main operational pillars: transporting samples from patient floors to the laboratory (`G2`), processing samples within the laboratory via a robotic arm (`G3`), and monitoring/logging transit metrics (`G4`).
* **Runtime Execution:** Transportation (`G2`) must occur before laboratory processing (`G3`), while logging (`G4`) runs in parallel with the overall mission: `(G2;G3)#G4`.


3. **Transport & Access Decomposition (`G2`):**
* Transporting samples requires executing mobile robot transit (`G2.1` / `AT1`) and managing secure access to locked drawers (`G2.2` / `AT2`). These are combined via an **AND** decomposition and executed sequentially (`G2.1;G2.2`).


4. **Laboratory Processing Decomposition (`G3`):**
* Laboratory processing requires picking and scanning samples (`G3.1`) followed by sorting and loading them into analysis machines (`G3.2`) using a robotic arm.
* `G3.1` further decomposes into picking samples (`G3.1.1` / `AT3`) and scanning barcodes (`G3.1.2` / `AT4`).
* `G3.2` decomposes into sorting samples (`G3.2.1` / `AT5`) and loading them into the entry module (`G3.2.2` / `AT6`).


5. **Monitoring & Logging Decomposition (`G4`):**
* Securely logging pick location/timestamps (`G4.1` / `AT7`) and tracking transit duration against protocols (`G4.2` / `AT8`) are required to ensure compliance with laboratory protocols. They operate in parallel (`G4.1#G4.2`).
