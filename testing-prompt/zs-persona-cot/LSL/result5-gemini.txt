As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems (MRS), I have analyzed the mission description and filtered out human-centric operations (such as nurse data entry, nurse requests, and manual laboratory personnel actions) in strict compliance with the rule that **goals and tasks performed by zero robots must not be included in the goal model**.

Below is the step-by-step reasoning, hierarchical goal model, detailed goal and task tables, summary table, and logical relationships required for the robotic deployment.

---

### Step-by-Step Mission Analysis & Reasoning

1. **System Boundaries & Robot Roles**:
* **Mobile Delivery Robots**: Responsible for secure transport of locked drawers containing lab samples from patient floors to the laboratory. They require authorized opening mechanisms at origin and navigation capabilities across hospital routes.
* **Laboratory Robotic Arm**: Responsible for extracting samples from the delivery drawers in the laboratory, scanning barcodes, sorting, and loading them into the entry-modules of analysis machines.


2. **Decomposition Strategy (Depth-First Order)**:
* **Root Mission Objective (`G1`)**: Manage the end-to-end automated transportation and laboratory processing of lab samples using a multi-robot system. This is an **Achieve** goal broken down via an **AND** decomposition into transport (`G2`) and lab processing (`G3`) sequentially (`G2;G3`).
* **Transportation Phase (`G2`)**: Involves unlocking/opening secure drawers at patient floors (`G2.1`) followed by mobile transit to the laboratory (`G2.2`) in sequential order (`G2.1;G2.2`).
* **Laboratory Processing Phase (`G3`)**: Involves retrieving and identifying samples (`G3.1`) followed by sorting and loading (`G3.2`) in sequential order (`G3.1;G3.2`).
* **Leaf Goals & Tasks**: Each leaf goal directly maps to operational robot tasks (`AT1` through `AT6`) located at either `patient floors`, `patient floors to laboratory`, or `laboratory`.

### Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Transporting samples from patient floors must physically occur before laboratory processing can take place.


* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The secure drawer must be accessed and opened before the mobile robot can transport it to the laboratory destination.


* **G2.1 -> AT1 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G2.1 is achieved directly by executing the drawer unlocking and opening task at the patient floor.


* **G2.2 -> AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G2.2 is achieved directly by executing the mobile transit task across hospital locations.


* **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Samples must be retrieved and identified through barcode scanning before they can be sorted and loaded into analysis machines.


* **G3.1 -> G3.1.1, G3.1.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The robotic arm must physically pick up the sample before scanning its barcode.


* **G3.1.1 -> AT3 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G3.1.1 is achieved by executing the sample pickup task in the laboratory.


* **G3.1.2 -> AT4 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G3.1.2 is achieved by executing the barcode scanning task in the laboratory.


* **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Samples must be sorted prior to being loaded into the entry-module of analysis machines.


* **G3.2.1 -> AT5 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G3.2.1 is achieved by executing the sample sorting task.


* **G3.2.2 -> AT6 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Goal G3.2.2 is achieved by executing the sample loading task into the analysis machine entry-module.
