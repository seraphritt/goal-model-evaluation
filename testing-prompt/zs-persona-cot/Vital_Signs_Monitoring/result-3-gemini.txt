## 1. Mission Analysis and Step-by-Step Reasoning

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, I have analyzed the natural language mission description for the autonomous hospital vital-signs-checking mission. Here is the step-by-step reasoning and decomposition rationale:

1. **Mission Scope & Periodicity**: The primary objective is recurring (every 2 hours) across all occupied hospital rooms, with a critical high-risk prioritization constraint (first 15 minutes). This requires a top-level split between **Mission Management & Scheduling (`G2`)** and **Room Execution & Patient Monitoring (`G3`)**.
2. **Pre-Entry Validation (`G3.1`)**: Before entering any room, robots must verify two independent preconditions: patient availability and robot battery sufficiency. Both feature exception/fallback workflows (patient unavailable triggers a 5-minute retry; low battery triggers recharging and task reassignment).
3. **Room Entry & Identification (`G3.2`)**: Once inside, the robot must approach, announce the procedure, and identify the patient in multi-bed rooms using alternative methods (asking **OR** scanning barcode).
4. **Condition-Specific Vital Signs & Failure Handling (`G3.3`)**: Vital signs collection depends on patient medical conditions (infectious diseases, post-surgical conditions, or diabetes history). If collection fails, a fallback protocol triggers assessment questions and, in case of non-response, a nurse/doctor alert.
5. **Sanitation & Transition (`G3.4`)**: Post-check procedures mandate actuator disinfection after patient contact and robot sterilization when exiting infectious disease rooms, followed by marking the task complete and proceeding to the next room.
6. **Fleet-Level Escalation (`G2.2`)**: If the mission cannot be completed in time, additional robots are assigned; if no combination of robots can finish the mission, the site manager must be alerted.

* **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND][(G2; G3)]**
* **Justification**: The mission requires both overarching scheduling/fleet management (`G2`) and detailed room execution/patient monitoring (`G3`) executed in a structured sequence to fulfill the global mission.


* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][(G2.1#G2.2)]**
* **Justification**: Periodic scheduling (`G2.1`) and fleet resource/escalation management (`G2.2`) operate concurrently as complementary aspects of mission control.


* **G2.2 -> G2.2.1, G2.2.2 [AND][FALLBACK(G2.2.1, G2.2.2)]**
* **Justification**: First, additional robots are assigned if time constraints cannot be met; as a fallback, if no robot combination can complete the mission, the site manager is alerted.
