As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, I have analyzed the natural language mission description for the hospital patient monitoring scenario. Below is the comprehensive decomposition into a structured goal model, detailing the step-by-step reasoning, hierarchical goal and task definitions, and logical relationships with justifications.

---

## Reasoning and Step-by-Step Thinking

1. **Mission Scope and Periodicity**:
* The mission is periodic ("Every two hours"), requiring all occupied rooms to be visited and vital signs checked. This sets the overarching goal (**G1**) and necessitates high-level scheduling and resource management.
* High-risk patients have a strict temporal constraint ("checked in the first 15 minutes"), requiring prioritization logic.


2. **Pre-Room Entry & Resource Verification**:
* Before entering a room, the robot must check patient availability and battery levels (`G2.1`). If the patient is unavailable, a 5-minute delay fallback applies. If battery is insufficient, recharging and task reassignment are triggered. These exception and fallback mechanisms are critical for multi-robot robustness.
* Rooms may contain multiple beds, requiring patient identification either by asking or scanning a barcode (OR decomposition, `G2.2`).


3. **Clinical Interaction and Condition-Specific Checks**:
* Inside the room, the robot must approach, announce the procedure, and provide instructions (`G3.1`).
* Vital sign checks depend strictly on the patient's medical category (Infectious: HR, BP, Temp, SpO2; Post-surgical: HR, SpO2; Diabetes: Glucose) (`G3.2.1`).
* If collection fails, a fallback questionnaire is triggered. Non-response triggers an alert to medical staff (`G3.2.2`).


4. **Post-Room Hygiene and Multi-Robot Coordination**:
* After checking, the robot marks the patient done and proceeds. If the room involved infectious diseases, sterilization is mandatory. Patient contact requires actuator disinfection (`G3.3`).
* If time constraints cannot be met, additional robots are assigned. If no robot combination can complete the mission, the site manager must be alerted (`G4`).

## Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G4 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The root mission goal requires performing pre-entry protocols (`G2`) and clinical assessments (`G3`) sequentially, while concurrently managing system exceptions and multi-robot resources (`G4`).


* **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Pre-entry assessments (`G2.1`) must be completed before the robot enters the room to identify the patient (`G2.2`).


* **G2.1 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][(parallel)]**
* **Justification**: Checking patient availability (`AT1`) and checking battery levels (`AT2`) are complementary pre-checks that can execute in parallel.


* **G2.2 -> AT3, AT4 [OR][-**]
* **Justification**: The robot identifies the patient either by asking (`AT3`) or scanning a barcode (`AT4`), representing an alternative choice.


* **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The robot approaches and instructs (`G3.1`), then collects vital signs (`G3.2`), and finally completes room tasks and hygiene (`G3.3`) in logical sequence.


* **G3.1 -> AT5, AT6 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The robot must approach the patient (`AT5`) before announcing the procedure and giving instructions (`AT6`).


* **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Vital signs collection (`G3.2.1`) is attempted first, followed sequentially by failure handling and escalation (`G3.2.2`) if collection fails.


* **G3.2.1 -> AT7, AT8, AT9 [OR][(parallel)]**
* **Justification**: Condition-specific vital sign checks are performed depending on patient diagnosis categories.


* **G3.2.2 -> AT10, AT11 [AND][FALLBACK(AT10, AT11)]**
* **Justification**: The assessment questionnaire (`AT10`) is tried first; if patient non-response occurs, an alert to medical staff (`AT11`) is triggered as a fallback mechanism.


* **G3.3 -> AT12, AT13, AT14 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Marking the patient check as done (`AT12`) precedes post-room sterilization (`AT13`) and actuator disinfection (`AT14`).
