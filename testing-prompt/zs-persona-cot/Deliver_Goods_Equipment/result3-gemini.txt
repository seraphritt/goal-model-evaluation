## Mission Analysis & Reasoning

As a mission designer specialized in Multi-Robot Systems, analyzing natural language mission descriptions requires breaking down operational workflows, environmental constraints, fault-tolerance mechanisms, and multi-robot coordination protocols into a formal goal model.

### Key Observations from the Description:

1. **Normal Operational Flow**: The mission is fundamentally divided into two primary phases: **Collection** and **Delivery**. Collection involves navigating to storage facilities (ordered by estimated waiting time plus path length), sending precise resource requests, and waiting for retrieval. Delivery involves executing multiple runs to transport resources to the specified location (such as sterile use destinations or laundry storage rooms).
2. **Multi-Robot Parallelism**: When multiple items are required from different storages, multiple robots can be assigned in parallel to reduce mission completion time.
3. **Fault Tolerance & Battery Management**:
* During the **Collection Phase**, if battery drops to **10%**, the robot must return to the recharging station and reassign the mission.
* During the **Delivery Phase**, if battery drops to **30%**, the robot must return the resource to a checkpoint and reassign the remaining task.
* If returning the resource to a checkpoint fails, an alert must be triggered and a report sent to the sector manager.
