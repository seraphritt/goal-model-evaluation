### 1. Step-by-Step Reasoning & Reflections

1. **Mission Scope Identification**:
The mission describes two core operational workflows in a hospital setting: **Food Delivery** from the kitchen to inpatient rooms and **Dirty Dish Retrieval** from inpatient rooms back to processing areas. Both workflows involve complex interactions between mobile robots, manipulation robots, patients, companions, nurses, and room doors.
2. **Goal Decomposition & Hierarchy**:
* **Top-Level Mission**: The overarching goal is managing the complete hospital meal and dish logistics cycle (`G1`).
* **Delivery Branch (`G1.1`)**: Requires fetching requests, navigating (`G1.1.2`), and executing delivery (`G1.1.3`). Delivery presents a critical branching decision: placing food directly on a table using robotic manipulation (`G1.1.3.1`) versus tray retrieval (`G1.1.3.2`). Because table delivery requires a specialized skill, tray retrieval serves as the primary or fallback path depending on robot capabilities and room conditions.
* **Uncertainty & Collaboration (`G1.1.3.2.1` to `G1.1.3.2.3`)**: Tray retrieval is not trivial. It depends on whether the patient, companion, or nurse is present and capable. This requires querying inpatient records under uncertainty, indicating specific meals when carrying multiple orders, tracking retrieval metrics, and issuing alerts if wrong meals are taken.
* **Dish Retrieval Branch (`G1.2`)**: Initiated by an in-room call (`G1.2.1`), followed by navigation (`G1.2.2`), door opening (`G1.2.3`), and dish collection (`G1.2.4`). Door opening and dish collection explicitly involve multi-agent cooperation (robots cooperating with other robots or humans).


3. **Runtime Operators & Goal Types**:
* **Achieve**: Used when the system transitions the environment to a desired target state (e.g., navigating, delivering, opening doors).
* **Query**: Used for information-gathering actions (e.g., fetching inpatient records, checking assistant availability, receiving pick-up calls).
* **Fallback (`FALLBACK(...)`)**: Used where an alternative execution path is attempted if the primary method fails or is unavailable (e.g., table delivery vs. tray fetching).


4. **Task Mapping (`AT`)**:
* Each leaf goal is backed by specific atomic or composite tasks (`AT1` through `AT11`) assigned to specific locations (hospital) and robot counts (1 to 2 robots, factoring in human-robot collaboration).

### 5. Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G1.1, G1.2 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: The hospital service must first deliver food to inpatients before it can subsequently retrieve dirty dishes from their rooms.


* **G1.1 -> G1.1.1, G1.1.2, G1.1.3 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Food delivery requires querying orders and records first, navigating to the room second, and executing the delivery action last.


* **G1.1.1 -> AT1, AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Querying delivery requests and querying inpatient/companion records are both necessary data-gathering steps.


* **G1.1.3 -> G1.1.3.1, G1.1.3.2 [FALLBACK(G1.1.3.1, G1.1.3.2)][(AND)]**
* **Justification**: The system attempts direct table delivery via manipulation (`G1.1.3.1`); if unsupported or unavailable, it falls back to tray retrieval (`G1.1.3.2`).


* **G1.1.3.2 -> G1.1.3.2.1, G1.1.3.2.2, G1.1.3.2.3 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Tray fetching requires querying assistant availability first, coordinating the retrieval second, and tracking/verifying the outcome concurrently.


* **G1.1.3.2.1 -> AT2 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Determining retrieval agent availability relies directly on querying inpatient records and companion presence.


* **G1.1.3.2.2 -> AT5 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Meal coordination relies on the robot indicating the correct meal to the recipient.


* **G1.1.3.2.3 -> AT6, AT7 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Verification and tracking entail logging retrieval timestamps/locations (`AT6`) and issuing alerts on wrong meal retrieval (`AT7`) in parallel.


* **G1.2 -> G1.2.1, G1.2.2, G1.2.3, G1.2.4 [AND][(sequential)]**
* **Justification**: Dish retrieval proceeds sequentially from receiving the pick-up call, navigating to the room, cooperating to open the door, and collecting the dishes.


* **G1.2.1 -> AT8 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Querying room pick-up calls and door signals directly maps to task `AT8`.


* **G1.2.2 -> AT9 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Navigating to the room for dish pick-up is directly executed by task `AT9`.


* **G1.2.3 -> AT10 [AND]**
* **Justification**: Opening the room door relies on cooperative interaction between robots or humans (`AT10`).


* **G1.2.4 -> AT11 [OR]**
* **Justification**: Dish collection can be performed through alternative execution modalities (unassisted, multi-robot, or human-robot cooperation) as represented by `AT11`.
