### Step-by-Step Reasoning & Thinking Process

1. **Mission Decomposition & Stakeholders Identification**:
* **Actors**: Nurse, Mobile Robot (transportation), Robotic Arm (laboratory manipulation), Laboratory Personnel, Tracking System / Backend Logger.
* **Core Objective**: Securely and timely transport lab samples from patient floors to the laboratory, track them, and load them into analysis machines.


2. **Goal Modeling (Hierarchical Breakdown)**:
* **G1 (High-Level Transport Goal)**: The primary objective is to move samples from patient floors to the lab. This is broken down sequentially (`[;]`):
* *G1.1 (Request & Registration)*: Nurse identifies sample via barcode and registers data.
* *G1.2 (Secure Transit)*: Transporting samples in locked drawers.
* *G1.3 (Lab Unloading & Processing)*: Unloading via robotic arm or personnel, followed by sorting and loading into analysis machines.


* **G2 (Data Logging & Traceability Goal)**: Information about picking location, timestamp, and end-to-end timing must be logged and monitored against protocols.
* **G3 (Security & Access Control Goal)**: Restricting access to locked drawers so only authorized personnel or specific robots at specific locations can unlock them.


3. **Task Allocation & Mapping**:

* **AT1 (Sample Registration)**: Performed by the nurse at patient floors (human task).
* **AT2 (Mobile Transport)**: Performed by a mobile robot moving across hospital corridors.
* **AT3 (Unloading)**: Performed alternatively by a robotic arm or lab personnel.
* **AT4 (Sorting & Loading)**: Performed by the laboratory robotic arm (scanning barcodes, sorting, loading into analysis machines).
* **AT5 (Secure Logging & Monitoring)**: Automated backend tracking of timestamps, locations, and protocol timelines.
