Food logistics

The system should ***1.deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room***.  The delivery is made on an ***2.order-by-order basis in response to kitchen delivery requests***. The food can be delivered into a ***3.room table by the robot (requires a special manipulation robot skill)*** or ***4.the food can be fetched from the robot's tray***. ***5.Fetching from the robot tray requires cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot.*** Some inpatients could be unable to fetch the food from the tray, and a companion visitor or nurse could not be available at the moment of the delivery. ***6.The information about if the inpatient, a companion visitor or nurse can be able to retrieve the food from the tray can be obtained based on the inpatient record according to information about the patient and the presence of a companion into the room***. 
This information is subject to uncertainty. 
***7.The robot can carry multiple meals***, ***8.in the case of a person retrieving the meal the robot should indicate which meal should be retrieved by a given inpatient***. ***9.It should track when and where each meal was retrieved***. ***The robot should alert if a wrong meal is retrieved***.  ***10.Dirty dishes should be retrieved from the room***. ***11.As with delivery, the dish retrieve can occur unassisted, with the cooperation of two robots or with the cooperation of robots and a human***. ***12.Opening the room door can involve cooperation with another robot or a human. Someone in the room can call a robot to pick up the dishes. In that case, that person can signal if they can open the door when the robot comes to it***.


Deliver Goods

When required, ***1.a robot must collect the required resources in the storage and deliver them to the requesting agent in a specified location.***
In the collection phase, ***2.the robot must go to the storage places where the resources can be found. 3.The orde  r to be followed is defined by the estimation of waiting time summed up with the path to be run by the robot.*** Once the robot reaches one storage and it is time to ***4.request the resource, the robot sends a message to the storage with the precise specification of the requested resources*** and ***5.waits until the resources have been retrieved.*** Once retrieved, the delivery phase begins. In this phase, the robot will ***6.make as many runs as necessary(Req. Smell = loophole) to take all the resources to the specified location. 7.If the battery of the assigned robot runs low (10%) in the collection phase, the robot goes back to the recharging station*** and ***8.assigns the mission to another robot***. ***9.If the battery of the assigned robot runs low (30%) in the delivery phase, the robot must return the resource to a checkpoint*** and ***10.assign the remaining task to another robot,*** which will know where the resource is positioned.

***11.In case of failure to return the resource to a checkpoint, an alert must be triggered and the report sent to the sector manager***. ***12.When multiple items are required from different storages, multiple robots can be assigned to parallel collect-deliver*** tasks to reduce the time to finish the mission. Examples of goods/ equipments are: (i) sterile medical equipment Logistics, which should be transported from sterile facilities to use destinations, and (ii) clean linens, which should periodically be moved from the laundry to storage rooms close to where it should be used.
