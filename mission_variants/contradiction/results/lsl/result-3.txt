## Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The lab personnel must unload the sample from the drawer before the robotic arm can touch it ("the robotic arm must never receive a sample directly from the transport robot"), so processing cannot begin until transport is complete. Both sub-missions are mandatory (AND), in that strict order (;).

- **G2 -> AT1, AT2 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Securing the sample in the locked drawer and tracking elapsed transit time are both required, but neither blocks the other — tracking happens continuously throughout the trip, so they run in parallel (#).

- **G3 -> G4, AT7 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: The processing sequence and the logging of the pickup event are both required, but logging the pickup doesn't need to block the arm from continuing to scan/sort/load, so these proceed in parallel (#).

- **G4 -> AT3, AT4, AT5, AT6 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The mission text gives an explicit, single order for the arm's actions — "picks up samples, scans the barcode... sorts, and loads" — all four steps are required (AND) and must occur left-to-right (;).

**Note on exclusions**: I left out the drawer-opening restriction ("only authorized personnel or specific robots in specific locations... open the locked drawers") as a standalone node — the text names no specific robot or additional location for this, so inventing one would violate the "don't make up a location" rule. I folded it into AT1's security description instead. Similarly, barcode identification, the nurse's delivery request, and track-system data entry are human-only (zero-robot) and excluded entirely.
