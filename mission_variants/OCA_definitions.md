- Omission:  World feature not state by any requirement document item. For eg. mission objective, requirement or assumption, unstated software response to some input.
- Contradiction: Requirement document items defining a world feature in an incompatible way.
- Ambiguity: Requirement document item allowing a world feature to be interpreted in different ways. For eg. ambiguous term or statement

Ambiguous and unmeasurable statements may result in a software implementation built from interpretations of requirements, assumptions or domain properties that are different from the intended ones.

EXAMPLES: 
- Omission: no requirement about the expected state of the train doors in case of emergency stop.
- Contradiction: "Train doors must always be kept closed between stations." AND SOMEWHERE ELSE -> "Train doors must be opened once a train is stopped after an emergency signal"
- Ambiguity: Train doors shall be opened as soon as the train is stopped at a platform. (Possible interpretations) "The front of the train is (stopped) at a platform or The whole train is (stopped) at a platform?"
