# Goal Model Design: Hospital Food Delivery & Dish Retrieval Multi-Robot System

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**Step 1 — Identify the macro-processes.**
The description names two independently-triggered responsibilities of the system:
1. **Meal delivery** — triggered "on an order-by-order basis in response to kitchen delivery requests."
2. **Dirty dish retrieval** — triggered when "someone in the room can call a robot to pick up the dishes."

Nothing in the text forces one to happen before/after the other at the system level (they are triggered by different events), so I model them as two AND-required children of a root mission goal, related by **parallel (`#`)** execution — "AND" because both capabilities are mandatory requirements of "the system," not alternatives.

**Step 2 — Exclude zero-robot elements.**
Per the "zero-robot" rule, I excluded as standalone nodes: "a person opens the door themselves" and "a person retrieves the meal unaided by the robot's presence" — these involve no robot action. I kept the robot-tray-presentation + human-retrieval tasks because the robot is always an active participant (holding/presenting the tray or door), even though a human performs part of the physical act.

**Step 3 — Distinguish sequencing types.**
- I used **sequential (`;`)** wherever the text implies a necessary, logical before/after order *without* a conditional failure trigger (e.g., you must receive an order before transporting the meal; you must query who can retrieve before indicating a meal).
- I used **FALLBACK** only where the text explicitly describes a normal behavior and a condition-triggered alternative: *"The robot should generate an alert if the meal retrieval is not performed correctly"* → `FALLBACK(Execute Retrieval, Generate Alert)`. This is the only place in the description with an explicit failure-triggered alternative, so it is the only FALLBACK in the model.
- I used **parallel (`#`)** for true OR-alternatives where the text gives no stated priority/order (e.g., table vs. tray; inpatient vs. companion vs. nurse vs. robot; the three door-opening options; the three dish-retrieval options).

**Step 4 — Collapse single-action nodes into Tasks.**
Since AND/OR decomposition requires ≥2 children, any step that is a single, atomic, operational action (e.g., "indicate meal," "track retrieval," "generate alert," "navigate kitchen→room") is modeled directly as a **Task**, not wrapped in an intermediate Goal with only one child.

**Step 5 — Scope "indicate which meal" correctly.**
The text says indication is needed *"in the case of a person retrieving the meal"* — this explicitly excludes the robot-robot cooperation path. I therefore nested "Indicate Meal" only under the person-assisted retrieval branch, not under the robot-cooperation branch, to avoid misrepresenting the mission.

**Step 6 — Avoid duplicating "Open Room Door."**
Door-opening cooperation is explicitly and only textually anchored to the dish-pickup call scenario ("...that person can signal if they can open the door when the robot comes to it"). To respect the no-redundancy rule (one intention → one goal) and the tree constraint (no shared parents), I modeled "Open Room Door" once, under the dish-retrieval branch, where the text explicitly describes it.

**Step 7 — Classify Goal Types.**
I used **Query** for pure information-gathering ("receive kitchen request," "determine retrieval capability," "receive door-access signal") since these just acquire knowledge the system later acts on and are explicitly described as subject to uncertainty/obtained from records. I used **Achieve** for goals that describe a target state reached via coordinated action (deliver meal, retrieve meal, access room, retrieve dishes), since these require planning across multiple possible paths. No goal in the tree fit **Perform** at the Goal level (simple "execute an action regardless of state") well enough once single actions were demoted to Tasks — all remaining Goals have a clear target condition or queried property, so none were forced into Perform merely for variety.

## Goal Model Tree (overview)

```
G1 Provide Inpatient Meal Delivery & Dish Collection Service
├── G2 Deliver Meal to Inpatient
│   ├── G3 Receive Kitchen Delivery Request
│   ├── AT1 Navigate from Kitchen to Inpatient Room
│   └── G4 Deliver Meal to Recipient
│       ├── AT2 Place Meal on Room Table
│       └── G5 Deliver Meal via Tray Retrieval
│           ├── G6 Determine Meal Retrieval Capability
│           │   ├── AT3 Query Inpatient Record
│           │   └── AT4 Query Companion Presence in Room
│           ├── G7 Execute Tray Retrieval
│           │   ├── G8 Retrieve Meal with Person Assistance
│           │   │   ├── AT5 Indicate Meal to Retrieve
│           │   │   └── G9 Person Retrieves Meal from Tray
│           │   │       ├── AT6 Retrieve with Inpatient Cooperation
│           │   │       ├── AT7 Retrieve with Companion Cooperation
│           │   │       └── AT8 Retrieve with Nurse Cooperation
│           │   └── AT9 Retrieve with Robot Cooperation
│           ├── AT10 Generate Meal Retrieval Alert  (FALLBACK partner of G7)
│           └── AT11 Track Meal Retrieval
└── G10 Retrieve Dirty Dishes from Room
    ├── AT12 Receive Dish Pickup Call
    ├── G11 Access the Room
    │   ├── AT13 Query Door-Access Signal from Caller
    │   └── G12 Open Room Door
    │       ├── AT14 Open Door Unassisted
    │       ├── AT15 Open Door with Robot Cooperation
    │       └── AT16 Open Door with Human Cooperation
    └── G13 Retrieve Dishes from Room
        ├── AT17 Retrieve Dishes Unassisted
        ├── AT18 Retrieve Dishes with Two-Robot Cooperation
        └── AT19 Retrieve Dishes with Robot-and-Human Cooperation
```

## Goal Tables

| Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type | Target Condition / QueriedProperty | Relation | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1**: Provide Inpatient Meal Service | The system must deliver ordered meals to inpatients and retrieve dirty dishes from rooms. | `G2#G10` | Achieve | All kitchen requests result in delivered meals, and all pickup calls result in dishes retrieved. | AND | Both delivery and retrieval are explicitly stated as required system capabilities ("should deliver food…", "Dirty dishes should be retrieved…"); neither is optional/alternative to the other. |
| **G2**: Deliver Meal to Inpatient | The system delivers the ordered meal to the designated inpatient's room. | `G3;AT1;G4` | Achieve | The meal corresponding to a kitchen request reaches the intended inpatient. | AND | "Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room… on an order-by-order basis" requires knowing the order, moving the food, then handing it off — three jointly necessary steps. |
| **G3**: Receive Kitchen Delivery Request | The robot obtains the order details (meal, inpatient, room) for a pending kitchen request. | `-` | Query | QueriedProperty: order content — meal identity, destination room/inpatient. | — (leaf) | "The delivery is made on an order-by-order basis in response to kitchen delivery requests" explicitly frames this as information the robot must obtain before acting, not an action with a target state. |
| **G4**: Deliver Meal to Recipient | The meal is handed to the inpatient either via table or via tray retrieval. | `AT2#G5` | Achieve | The meal is in the inpatient's possession or placed on the room table. | OR | "The food can be delivered into a room table… **or** the food can be fetched from the robot's tray" — explicit "or," no stated priority between the two, hence OR + parallel. |
| **G5**: Deliver Meal via Tray Retrieval | The meal is retrieved from the robot's tray by a cooperating party. | `G6;FALLBACK(G7,AT10);AT11` | Achieve | The meal is retrieved from the tray by an available party. | AND | Capability must be determined, then retrieval attempted (with alert as explicit failure fallback: "generate an alert if the meal retrieval is not performed correctly"), then the event is logged ("track when and where"). All three are jointly required. |
| **G6**: Determine Meal Retrieval Capability | Determine whether the inpatient, companion, or nurse can retrieve the meal. | `AT3#AT4` | Query | QueriedProperty: capability/availability of inpatient, companion, or nurse to retrieve food, subject to uncertainty. | AND | "Can be obtained based on the inpatient record **according to** information about the patient **and** the presence of a companion" — two conjunctive information sources, explicitly flagged as uncertain, matching the Query definition. |
| **G7**: Execute Tray Retrieval | The meal is physically retrieved from the tray, by a person or by another robot. | `G8#AT9` | Achieve | The meal is removed from the tray by the cooperating party. | OR | "Requires cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, **or another robot**" — explicit alternation among cooperating-agent types, no stated order. |
| **G8**: Retrieve Meal with Person Assistance | A human occupant of the room is informed of and retrieves their meal. | `AT5;G9` | Achieve | The assigned meal is retrieved by an available person after being identified to them. | AND | "In the case of a person retrieving the meal the robot should indicate which meal should be retrieved" — the indication must precede the actual retrieval; this qualifier applies only to the person-based paths, not the robot-cooperation path. |
| **G9**: Person Retrieves Meal from Tray | The specific person (inpatient, companion, or nurse) who is available performs the retrieval. | `AT6#AT7#AT8` | Achieve | The meal is retrieved by whichever available person (inpatient/companion/nurse) carries it out. | OR | "Some inpatients could be unable… a companion visitor or nurse could not be available" — availability varies, so these are mutually substitutable alternatives with no stated order. |
| **G10**: Retrieve Dirty Dishes from Room | Dirty dishes are collected from the inpatient's room. | `AT12;G11;G13` | Achieve | Dirty dishes are removed from the room following a pickup call. | AND | The process requires first being called, then gaining room access, then performing retrieval — three jointly necessary, logically ordered steps described across the text. |
| **G11**: Access the Room | The robot gains physical access to the room. | `AT13;G12` | Achieve | The room door is open and the robot can enter/engage. | AND | The signal about door-opening ability is obtained "when the robot comes to it," i.e., right before the door must actually be opened — necessitating the query before the door-opening action. |
| **G12**: Open Room Door | The door is opened via one of the possible cooperation modes. | `AT14#AT15#AT16` | Achieve | The room door is in an open state. | OR | "Opening the room door can involve cooperation with another robot or a human" — "can involve" implies unassisted opening is also possible; three substitutable alternatives, no stated order. |
| **G13**: Retrieve Dishes from Room | Dirty dishes are physically collected. | `AT17#AT18#AT19` | Achieve | Dishes are collected by the robot(s)/human team. | OR | "The dish retrieve can occur unassisted, with the cooperation of two robots, or with the cooperation of robots and a human" — explicit three-way alternation, no order specified. |

| **AT4**: Query Companion Presence in Room | Check whether a companion is present in the room. | AND (child of G6) | Inpatient Room | 1 | "The presence of a companion into the room" is explicitly room-located; a single robot can sense/check this on arrival. |
| **AT5**: Indicate Meal to Retrieve | Robot signals which meal (of multiple carried) belongs to the given inpatient. | AND (child of G8) | Inpatient Room | 1 | "The robot should indicate which meal should be retrieved by a given inpatient" is a single robot's communicative action toward the room occupant. |

| **AT10**: Generate Meal Retrieval Alert | The robot raises an alert if retrieval failed. | AND (FALLBACK partner of G7, child of G5) | Inpatient Room | 1 | "The robot should generate an alert if the meal retrieval is not performed correctly" is a single robot's reactive action at the point of failure. |
| **AT11**: Track Meal Retrieval | Robot records when and where the meal was retrieved. | AND (child of G5) | Inpatient Room | 1 | "It should track when and where each meal was retrieved" — a single logging action performed by the delivering robot. |

| **AT16**: Open Door with Human Cooperation | Robot opens the door together with a human. | OR (child of G12) | Inpatient Room (room door) | 1 | "...or a human" — one robot cooperating with one human occupant. |

| **AT18**: Retrieve Dishes with Two-Robot Cooperation | Two robots jointly collect the dishes. | OR (child of G13) | Inpatient Room | 2 | "...with the cooperation of two robots" is an explicit, exact count. |
| **AT19**: Retrieve Dishes with Robot-and-Human Cooperation | Robot(s) and a human jointly collect the dishes. | OR (child of G13) | Inpatient Room | [1,2] | "...with the cooperation of robots and a human" uses the plural "robots," unlike the "two robots" phrase used for the pure-robot case; a range captures this textual ambiguity rather than assuming an exact count not stated. |

## Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G10 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Meal delivery and dish retrieval are both mandatory system responsibilities, triggered independently (kitchen request vs. room call); nothing ties their timing together.

- **G2 -> G3, AT1, G4 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The robot must know the order, then physically move the meal, then deliver it — a necessary logical order with no stated conditional branching, hence sequential rather than fallback.

- **G4 -> AT2, G5 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Delivered into a room table… or fetched from the robot's tray" is an explicit alternative with no stated precedence.

- **G5 -> G6, G7, AT10, AT11 [AND] (G6 ; FALLBACK(G7, AT10) ; AT11)**
  - **Justification**: Capability must first be determined; retrieval is then attempted, with the alert as the explicit failure-triggered alternative ("if the meal retrieval is not performed correctly"); the outcome is finally logged. Per the schema, FALLBACK pairs are structurally AND-connected.

- **G6 -> AT3, AT4 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Capability is derived jointly "according to information about the patient and the presence of a companion" — both pieces of information are required and can be gathered independently.

- **G7 -> G8, AT9 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot" groups into person-assisted vs. robot-assisted retrieval, which are alternative, non-ordered paths.

- **G8 -> AT5, G9 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The meal must be indicated to the person before that person can retrieve the correct one — a necessary order specific to the person-assisted path, as scoped by "in the case of a person retrieving the meal."

- **G9 -> AT6, AT7, AT8 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Inpatient, companion, and nurse are interchangeable, availability-dependent alternatives ("some inpatients could be unable… a companion… or nurse could not be available"), with no priority stated.

- **G10 -> AT12, G11, G13 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The robot must be called, then gain access to the room, then retrieve the dishes — a logically necessary order.

- **G11 -> AT13, G12 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The caller's door-access signal is obtained "when the robot comes to it," immediately before the door-opening action is carried out.

- **G12 -> AT14, AT15, AT16 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Can involve cooperation with another robot or a human" implies unassisted opening is also an option; all three are substitutable alternatives with no stated order.

- **G13 -> AT17, AT18, AT19 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Can occur unassisted, with the cooperation of two robots, or with the cooperation of robots and a human" is an explicit three-way, unordered alternation.
