# Multi-Robot Goal Model: Inpatient Meal Delivery & Dish Pickup System

## Step-by-step reasoning (how I decomposed the mission)

**1. I identified the two independent service flows** the description names: *food delivery* (triggered "in response to kitchen delivery requests") and *dish pickup* (triggered when "someone in the room can call a robot to pick up the dishes"). Since each is triggered by a different event and can run concurrently for different rooms in a multi-robot fleet, I placed them as **parallel** children of a single root goal (AND — both are mandatory system responsibilities).

**2. I distinguished true OR-alternatives from FALLBACK chains.** The description uses "or" in three places:
- *table delivery* **or** *tray fetch* → no condition/order stated between the two → **OR**, parallel.
- *inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot* → immediately qualified by "some inpatients could be *unable*… companion/nurse could *not be available*" → this is a condition/exception explicitly triggering the next alternative → **FALLBACK**, not OR, even though the word "or" is used.
- *door opened by a robot or a human* → immediately qualified by "that person can **signal if they can** open the door" → again a condition-triggered alternative → **FALLBACK**.
- *unassisted, two robots, or robots+human* dish retrieval → no condition/order given → **OR**, parallel.

**3. I excluded zero-robot actions as standalone nodes.** "The patient places the dirty dishes on a cart" and "someone calls a robot" are purely human actions — they are *not* modeled as independent goals/tasks, but they justify the **Query goals** that capture the robot/system *receiving* that information (which does involve the robot).

**4. I classified goal types** using the given semantics: information-gathering requirements (order details, capability checks, tracking data) → **Query**; concrete single actions (place meal, alert, present tray) → modeled as **Tasks** directly; composite states the robots must bring about through planning → **Achieve**.

**5. I kept every decomposition to ≥2 children**, since a 1-child node isn't a valid AND/OR decomposition — this is why query goals like "Receive Kitchen Delivery Request" remain leaves rather than being artificially split.

## Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G11 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Delivery and pickup are both mandatory system services, triggered by different independent events, and can be executed concurrently across the robot fleet.

- **G2 -> G3, G4 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The order must be received before it can be fulfilled ("delivery is made... in response to kitchen delivery requests").

- **G4 -> AT1, G5 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Transport to the room must occur before the delivery method (table or tray) can be executed.

- **G5 -> AT2, G6 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Delivered into a room table... or fetched from the robot's tray" are two alternatives, either being sufficient; no order between them is stated.

- **G6 -> G7, AT3, G8, G9 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Capability must be known before indicating the meal to the right party, before retrieval can occur, before the retrieval event can be monitored.

- **G8 -> AT4, AT5, AT6, AT7 [AND] FALLBACK(FALLBACK(FALLBACK(AT4,AT5),AT6),AT7)**
  - **Justification**: The inpatient is tried first; "some inpatients could be unable" triggers falling back to the companion; "a companion visitor or nurse could not be available" triggers falling back further to the nurse, then finally to cooperation with another robot. This is a condition-triggered alternation, not a free choice, so FALLBACK applies (AND-connected per the FALLBACK rule) despite the literal "or" in the source text.

- **G9 -> G10, AT8 [AND] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Tracking retrieval data and alerting on a wrong retrieval are both required, with no stated order or dependency between them.

- **G11 -> G12, G13 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The pickup call (and door-capability signal) must be received before the pickup can be fulfilled.

- **G13 -> G14, G15 [AND] (sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The robot must gain access to the room (door open) before it can retrieve the cart that sits at the door.

- **G14 -> AT9, AT10 [AND] FALLBACK(AT9, AT10)**
  - **Justification**: "That person can signal if they can open the door" is an explicit condition: if they can, they open it (default path); if not, the robot falls back to cooperating with another robot to open it.

- **G15 -> AT11, AT12, AT13 [OR] (parallel)**
  - **Justification**: "Unassisted, with the cooperation of two robots, or with the cooperation of robots and a human" are three stated alternatives with no order or condition distinguishing them.
