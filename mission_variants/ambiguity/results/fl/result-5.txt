# Multi-Robot Goal Model: Hospital Meal Delivery & Dish Pickup System

## Step-by-Step Reasoning (Methodology)

Before building the tables, here is my analytical walkthrough of the description:

1. **Two top-level services are described**: food delivery (triggered by kitchen requests) and dish pickup (triggered by a call from the room). Since both are explicitly described as responsibilities of the same robotic system, I unified them under one root goal, connected by **AND** (both are mandatory system responsibilities) with **parallel** runtime — the text gives no explicit statement that one must wait for the other at the system level (different orders/rooms can be in different phases at once).

2. **"Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room"** explicitly names an origin and a destination — this grounds a dedicated *transport* task (using the literal "kitchen" location) that must happen **before** the hand-off method, hence **sequential**.

3. **"...delivered into a room table... OR fetched from the tray"** — explicit "or" → **OR decomposition**. No stated priority between the two → **parallel** (possibility, not obligation) runtime, not fallback (no IF/exception language links them).

4. **Tray-fetch cooperation list** ("inpatient, a companion, a nurse, or another robot") — explicit "or" → **OR**. Again no explicit priority order stated → **parallel**.

5. **Eligibility information** ("based on inpatient record... and the presence of a companion") is explicitly framed as *queried* information (pre-determined, not discovered by trial-and-error), so I modeled it as a **Query Goal**, not a Fallback — the text never says "try the inpatient, and if that fails, try the companion"; it says the system *knows in advance* via records/sensing.

6. **"Indicate which meal... should be retrieved"** is explicitly scoped to "the case of a person retrieving the meal" — so I nested it only under the person-cooperation branch, not the robot-robot branch, to avoid misrepresenting the text.

7. **"Track when and where... / alert if a wrong meal is retrieved"** is a textbook normal-behavior-then-exception pair → **FALLBACK**, per the explicit rule in the instructions.

8. **Dish pickup**: cart is "beside the room door" and door-opening is discussed in the same breath as pickup ("that person can signal if they can open the door **when the robot comes to it**") → door access is a necessary, **sequential** precondition to retrieving the dishes.

9. **Door-opening cooperation** ("another robot or a human") and **dish-retrieval cooperation** ("unassisted, two robots, or robots and a human") are both explicit "or" lists with no stated priority → **OR / parallel**.

10. **Zero-robot elements excluded** (per the rule): the kitchen issuing a request, the patient placing dishes on the cart, and someone calling a robot are all *human-side triggers*, not robot-performed goals/tasks, so they appear only as contextual triggers, never as model nodes.

11. **Uncertainty** ("this information is subject to uncertainty") was treated as an epistemic property of the Query Goal (G5), not a separate node.

## Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G11 [AND](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Both delivery and pickup are core, mandatory responsibilities of the system; no explicit ordering constraint links the two processes at the system/fleet level (they are triggered independently — kitchen requests vs. room calls).

- **G2 -> AT1, G3 [AND](sequential)**
  - **Justification**: "Deliver food **from** the kitchen **to** an inpatient room" explicitly requires transport before hand-off; both are necessary, and transport must occur first.

- **G3 -> AT2, G4 [OR](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Explicit "or" between table delivery and tray fetch; no stated precedence between the two methods.

- **G4 -> G5, G6, G9 [AND](sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Determining eligibility, performing the cooperative retrieval, and tracking/verifying are all necessary, and the text presents them in this operational order.

- **G5 -> AT3, AT4 [AND](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Eligibility is explicitly based on "the inpatient record **according to**... the patient **and** the presence of a companion" — both data points are jointly required, with no stated order between the two lookups.

- **G6 -> G7, AT9 [OR](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Explicit "or" between person-based and robot-based cooperation; presented as a flat alternative list.

- **G7 -> AT5, G8 [AND](sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Indicating the correct meal is a prerequisite to successful cooperation with a specific person in a multi-meal scenario; both required, indication first.

- **G8 -> AT6, AT7, AT8 [OR](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Explicit "or" list of inpatient/companion/nurse cooperators, no priority stated.

- **G9 -> G10, AT11 [AND] FALLBACK(G10, AT11)**
  - **Justification**: Classic normal-behavior/exception pattern: tracking is the default behavior; alerting is explicitly conditioned ("**if** a wrong meal is retrieved"), matching the fallback definition exactly. Per the rules, fallback children are AND-connected.

- **G10 -> AT10 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: The sole way to realize tracking is logging retrieval time/location; trivially AND with one mandatory task.

- **G11 -> G12, G15 [AND](sequential)**
  - **Justification**: The robot must gain access to/through the door before it can reach the cart beside it; both access and retrieval are required, access first.

- **G12 -> G13, G14 [AND](sequential)**
  - **Justification**: Determining whether a person will assist logically precedes/accompanies the act of opening ("...signal if they can open the door **when** the robot comes to it"); both are necessary.

- **G13 -> AT12 [AND]**
  - **Justification**: Single operational realization (receiving/checking the person's signal); trivially AND.

- **G14 -> AT13, AT14 [OR](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Explicit "or" between robot cooperation and human cooperation for opening the door; no priority stated.

- **G15 -> AT15, AT16, AT17 [OR](parallel)**
  - **Justification**: Explicit list "unassisted... two robots... or... robots and a human" — alternative modes with no stated priority.
