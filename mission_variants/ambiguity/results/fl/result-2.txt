# Mission Decomposition: Hospital Meal Delivery & Dish Retrieval

## Step-by-Step Reasoning

**1. Identify the top-level intentions.** The description contains two clearly separable service lines: (a) delivering food from the kitchen to a room, and (b) retrieving dirty dishes from a room. Nothing in the text forces one to wait for the other (they are triggered by different events — a kitchen order vs. an occupant's call), so I model them as two AND-required, parallel children of a single root goal.

**2. Identify explicit alternatives (OR).** The text explicitly offers alternatives in four places, each becomes an OR-decomposition:
- "delivered into a room table... **or** fetched from the robot's tray" → delivery mode.
- "cooperation with the inpatient, a companion, a nurse, **or** another robot" → I collapsed the three human roles (inpatient/companion/nurse) into one *Human Cooperation* branch and kept *Robot Cooperation* separate, because the underlying robot-side intention ("facilitate a human taking the meal") is identical regardless of which human it is — duplicating it per role would violate the no-redundancy rule. The specific role is already resolved earlier by the capability query.
- "opening the room door can involve cooperation with another robot **or** a human" → door-opening mode.
- "dish retrieve can occur unassisted, with cooperation of two robots **or** with cooperation of robots and a human" → here the three modes are genuinely distinct operational intentions (no cooperation / 2 robots / robot+human), so I kept all three separate.

**3. Identify the one true Fallback.** Fallback requires an explicit normal-behavior/alternative-triggered-by-condition pattern. Only one place matches this exactly: *"It should track when and where each meal was retrieved. The robot should generate an alert if the meal retrieval is not performed correctly."* Normal behavior = track; condition ("if not performed correctly") = alert. I did **not** use Fallback anywhere else (e.g., table vs. tray is a situational choice, not a retry-after-failure pattern — correctly modeled as OR).

**4. Identify Query goals.** Any goal whose purpose is only to obtain information (not to physically act) is a Query goal: determining who can retrieve food (explicitly "subject to uncertainty"), receiving the kitchen order, receiving the dish-pickup call, and reading the door-opening signal.

**5. Identify Perform vs. Achieve.** I used a consistent rule: a node that is a concrete, atomic, directly-executable action is **Perform** (transport, place, indicate/present, record, alert, physically collect, physically open with a partner). A node that aggregates several sub-goals (sequential steps, or alternative manners of reaching one state) represents a **state to be brought about**, so it is **Achieve**, with a target condition describing that state.

**6. Zero-robot rule.** I excluded: the inpatient/companion/nurse physically eating or taking food as a standalone goal, and the occupant opening the door unassisted — these involve no robot action and were folded instead into the *information* that the robot queries (capability / door-signal), never modeled as goals themselves.

**7. Locations and robot counts** were drawn strictly from the text: "Kitchen" and "Inpatient Room" are the only locations mentioned. Robot counts default to 1 except where cooperation with "another robot" or "two robots" is explicit (→ 2).

  - *Justification*: Meal delivery and dish retrieval are two independently-triggered service lines (kitchen order vs. occupant call); nothing requires one to wait on the other.

- **G2 -> G3, G4 [AND](sequential)**
  - *Justification*: The order must be known ("order-by-order basis in response to kitchen delivery requests") before any transport/delivery action can start.

- **G4 -> G5, G6 [AND](sequential)**
  - *Justification*: The robot must physically reach the room (transport) before it can deliver via table or tray.

- **G6 -> G7, G8 [OR](parallel)**
  - *Justification*: "delivered into a room table... or fetched from the robot's tray" is an explicit alternative with no stated order of preference.

- **G8 -> G9, G10 [AND](sequential)**

- **G10 -> G11, G14 [AND](sequential)**

- **G11 -> G12, G13 [OR](parallel)**
