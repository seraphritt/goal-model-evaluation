To design the goal model for the multi-robot system, we decompose the mission into hierarchical goals and tasks, considering the provided definitions and requirements.

### Goal Model Decomposition

1. **Main Goal (G1): Deliver Food and Retrieve Dishes**
   - **Type**: Achieve
   - **Target Condition**: Food delivered and dishes retrieved successfully.
   - **Decomposition**: AND (G2 and G3 must both succeed)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution

2. **Sub-Goal G2: Deliver Food**
   - **Type**: Achieve
   - **Target Condition**: Food delivered to the inpatient room.
   - **Decomposition**: OR (either G2.1 or G2.2)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution

3. **Sub-Goal G2.1: Deliver Food Directly**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Robot delivers food directly to the room table.
   - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

4. **Sub-Goal G2.2: Arrange Food Retrieval from Tray**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Arrange for someone to retrieve food from the robot's tray.
   - **Decomposition**: AND (G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution

5. **Sub-Goal G2.2.1: Check Retrieval Capability**
   - **Type**: Query
   - **Enquired Information**: Whether the inpatient or companion can retrieve the food.
   - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

6. **Sub-Goal G2.2.2: Indicate Correct Meal**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Robot indicates the correct meal for the inpatient.
   - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

7. **Sub-Goal G2.2.3: Arrange Assistance**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Arrange assistance for meal retrieval.
   - **Decomposition**: OR (G2.2.3.1 or G2.2.3.2)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution

8. **Sub-Goal G2.2.3.1: Involve Another Robot**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Another robot assists with meal retrieval.
   - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

9. **Sub-Goal G2.2.3.2: Involve Human**
   - **Type**: Perform
   - **Description**: Nurse or companion assists with meal retrieval.
   - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

10. **Sub-Goal G3: Retrieve Dirty Dishes**
    - **Type**: Achieve
    - **Target Condition**: Dirty dishes retrieved from the room.
    - **Decomposition**: OR (G3.1 or G3.2)
    - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution

11. **Sub-Goal G3.1: Retrieve Dishes Directly**
    - **Type**: Perform
    - **Description**: Robot retrieves dishes directly.
    - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

12. **Sub-Goal G3.2: Arrange Dish Retrieval with Assistance**
    - **Type**: Perform
    - **Description**: Arrange for assistance in retrieving dishes.
    - **Decomposition**: OR (G3.2.1 or G3.2.2)
    - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution

13. **Sub-Goal G3.2.1: Involve Another Robot**
    - **Type**: Perform
    - **Description**: Another robot assists with dish retrieval.
    - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"

14. **Sub-Goal G3.2.2: Involve Human**
    - **Type**: Perform
    - **Description**: Nurse or companion assists with dish retrieval.
    - **Runtime Annotation**: "-"
