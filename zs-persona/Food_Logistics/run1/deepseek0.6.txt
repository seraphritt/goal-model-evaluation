### Goal Model Design

The goal model is structured to represent the hierarchical decomposition of the mission into goals and tasks, considering the AND/OR relationships and runtime annotations.

---

### Goals Table

| Name         | Text                                                                                     | Runtime                     | Goal Type   | Relation  |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| **G1**       | Deliver food and retrieve dirty dishes in a hospital setting.                          | "-"                         | Achieve    | -         |
| **G2**       | Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room.                                   | ";"                         | Achieve    | AND       |
| **G2.1**     | Determine if the inpatient can retrieve food from the robot's tray.                     | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G2.2**     | Arrange assistance for food retrieval if necessary.                                    | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G2.3**     | Carry food to the inpatient room.                                                       | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G2.4**     | Deliver food to the inpatient room.                                                     | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G3**       | Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room.                                         | "-"                         | Achieve    | AND       |
| **G3.1**     | Determine if someone can retrieve the dishes from the room.                             | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G3.2**     | Arrange assistance for dish retrieval if necessary.                                    | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G3.3**     | Carry dirty dishes from the inpatient room.                                            | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |
| **G3.4**     | Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room.                                         | "-"                         | Perform    | AND       |

| **AT3.3**    | Carry the dirty dishes from the inpatient room.                                        | AND      | Inpatient room  | 1                |

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND]**
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4 [AND]**
- **G2.1 -> AT2.1 [AND]**
- **G2.2 -> AT2.2 [AND]**
- **G2.3 -> AT2.3 [AND]**
- **G2.4 -> AT2.4 [AND]**
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3, G3.4 [AND]**
- **G3.1 -> AT3.1 [AND]**
- **G3.2 -> AT3.2 [AND]**
- **G3.3 -> AT3.3 [AND]**
- **G3.4 -> AT3.4 [AND]**

This model ensures that the mission is decomposed into manageable tasks, each executed by a robot or with human assistance, following the specified AND/OR relationships.
