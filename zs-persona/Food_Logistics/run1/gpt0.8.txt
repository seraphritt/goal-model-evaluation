**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Food Delivery System**

| Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type | Target Condition / Enquired Information | Relation |
|------|------|---------|-----------|----------------------------------------|----------|
| **G1: Deliver Food** | Deliver food from kitchen to inpatient room. | `;` (sequential) | Achieve | `Food is delivered to inpatient room.` | AND |
| **G1.1: Process Kitchen Order** | Process kitchen order and transport food to room. | `-` | Perform | `Order processed and food transported.` | AND |
| **G1.2: Decide Delivery Method** | Decide whether to deliver food to table or tray. | `FALLBACK(G1.3.1, G1.3.2)` | Perform | – | OR |

| **G1.3.1: Deliver to Table** | Deliver food directly onto patient’s table. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.3.2: Deliver to Tray** | Deliver food into patient’s tray for later retrieval. | `#` (parallel) | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.3.2.1: Query Patient Retrieval Capability** | Check if patient can retrieve food from tray. | `-` | Query | `Can patient retrieve from tray?` | AND |
| **G1.3.2.2: Query Companion Availability** | Check if companion visitor is present. | `-` | Query | `Is companion visitor available?` | AND |
| **G1.3.2.3: Query Nurse Availability** | Check if nurse is available to assist. | `-` | Query | `Is nurse available?` | AND |
| **G1.3.2.4: Handle Retrieval** | Manage patient or companion retrieval of food from tray. | `#` (parallel) | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.4: Handle Dish Cleaning** | Retrieve dirty dishes from patient room. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.5: Open Door** | Open the patient room door, possibly with cooperation. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
