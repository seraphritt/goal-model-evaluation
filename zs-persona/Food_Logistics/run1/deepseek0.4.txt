- **G1** is the root goal, decomposed into **G2** and **G3** in parallel.

- Each task under the goals is sequential, with the necessary number of robots specified.

This model ensures that the system can handle both food delivery and dish retrieval efficiently, considering various cooperation scenarios and fallback options.
