Here is the structured goal model for the multi-robot system, designed to deliver food and retrieve dishes in a hospital setting:

| Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type | Relation |
|------|------|---------|-----------|----------|

| **G2.3** | Track meal retrieval | - | Perform | G2 -> G2.3 |
