To design the goal model for the multi-robot system, we will follow a structured approach, breaking down the mission description into a hierarchical structure of goals and tasks. The goal model will be represented using AND and OR decompositions, along with runtime annotations for execution structure.

### Goal Model

We will start by identifying the main objectives and decomposing them into sub-goals and tasks.

#### Root Goal: Manage Delivery and Retrieval Process
- **G1: Manage Food Delivery and Dish Retrieval**
  - **Goal Type**: Achieve
  - **Target Condition**: Ensure food is delivered to the inpatient room and dirty dishes are retrieved from the room.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - G2: Deliver Food to Inpatient Room
    - G3: Retrieve Dirty Dishes from Inpatient Room

#### Sub-Goal: Deliver Food to Inpatient Room
- **G2: Deliver Food to Inpatient Room**
  - **Goal Type**: Achieve
  - **Target Condition**: Food is delivered to the correct inpatient room and placed on the room table or retrieved by the inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - G2.1: Check if Assistance is Required for Delivery
    - G2.2: Deliver Food Directly to Room Table
    - G2.3: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance

#### Sub-Goal: Retrieve Dirty Dishes from Inpatient Room
- **G3: Retrieve Dirty Dishes from Inpatient Room**
  - **Goal Type**: Achieve
  - **Target Condition**: Dirty dishes are retrieved from the inpatient room and delivered to the kitchen.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - G3.1: Check if Assistance is Required for Retrieval
    - G3.2: Retrieve Dishes Directly
    - G3.3: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance

#### Sub-Goal: Check if Assistance is Required
- **G2.1: Check if Assistance is Required for Delivery**
  - **Goal Type**: Query
  - **Enquired Information**: Whether the inpatient can retrieve the food from the tray, and whether a companion visitor or nurse is available in the room.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**: None (Leaf Goal)

- **G3.1: Check if Assistance is Required for Retrieval**
  - **Goal Type**: Query
  - **Enquired Information**: Whether the inpatient can retrieve the dishes, and whether a companion visitor or nurse is available in the room.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**: None (Leaf Goal)

#### Sub-Goal: Deliver Food Directly to Room Table
- **G2.2: Deliver Food Directly to Room Table**
  - **Goal Type**: Perform
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - AT1: Deliver Food to Room Table
  - **Runtime Annotation**: None (Leaf Goal)

#### Sub-Goal: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance
- **G2.3: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance**
  - **Goal Type**: Perform
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - AT2: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance
  - **Runtime Annotation**: None (Leaf Goal)

#### Sub-Goal: Retrieve Dishes Directly
- **G3.2: Retrieve Dishes Directly**
  - **Goal Type**: Perform
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - AT3: Retrieve Dishes Directly
  - **Runtime Annotation**: None (Leaf Goal)

#### Sub-Goal: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance
- **G3.3: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance**
  - **Goal Type**: Perform
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Decomposition**:
    - AT4: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance
  - **Runtime Annotation**: None (Leaf Goal)

### Task Model

Each task is a leaf node in the goal model and represents a concrete operation that a robot or group of robots can execute.

#### Task: Deliver Food to Room Table
- **AT1: Deliver Food to Room Table**
  - **Task Name**: Deliver Food to Room Table
  - **Task Description**: Deliver the food directly to the inpatient room table using a special manipulation robot skill.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Inpatient Room Table
  - **Number of Robots**: 1

#### Task: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance
- **AT2: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance**
  - **Task Name**: Fetch Food from Robot's Tray with Assistance
  - **Task Description**: Fetch the food from the robot's tray with the cooperation of the inpatient, a companion visitor, a nurse, or another robot.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Inpatient Room
  - **Number of Robots**: 1 or 2

#### Task: Retrieve Dishes Directly
- **AT3: Retrieve Dishes Directly**
  - **Task Name**: Retrieve Dishes Directly
  - **Task Description**: Retrieve dirty dishes directly from the inpatient room without assistance.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Inpatient Room
  - **Number of Robots**: 1

#### Task: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance
- **AT4: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance**
  - **Task Name**: Retrieve Dishes with Assistance
  - **Task Description**: Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room with the cooperation of another robot or a human.
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Inpatient Room
  - **Number of Robots**: 1 or 2
