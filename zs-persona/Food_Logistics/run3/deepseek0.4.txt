To design the goal model for the mission of delivering food and retrieving dirty dishes in a hospital setting, we structure the problem into a hierarchical tree using AND and OR decompositions. The model ensures both tasks are completed successfully, considering uncertainties and cooperative efforts.

### Goal Model Structure

1. **Top-Level Goal (G1)**
   - **Name:** G1
   - **Title:** Deliver food and retrieve dirty dishes from inpatient rooms
   - **Type:** Achieve
   - **Target Condition:** All food deliveries and dish retrievals are completed correctly.
   - **Relation:** AND
   - **Children:** G2 (Food Delivery), G3 (Dish Retrieval)

2. **Sub-Goal G2 (Food Delivery)**
   - **Name:** G2
   - **Title:** Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room

   - **Target Condition:** All food deliveries are completed correctly.

   - **Children:** G2.1 (Delivery Method), G2.2 (Correct Meal Retrieval), G2.3 (Track and Alert)

3. **Sub-Goal G2.1 (Delivery Method)**

   - **Name:** G2.1.1

   - **Name:** G2.2.2

9. **Sub-Goal G2.3 (Track and Alert)**
   - **Name:** G2.3

   - **Relation:** AND
   - **Children:** AT2.7 (Track Meals), AT2.8 (Alert Wrong Meal)

10. **Sub-Goal G3 (Dish Retrieval)**
    - **Name:** G3
    - **Title:** Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room
    - **Type:** Achieve
    - **Target Condition:** All dirty dishes are retrieved correctly.
    - **Relation:** AND
    - **Children:** G3.1 (Retrieval Method), G3.2 (Door Opening)

11. **Sub-Goal G3.1 (Retrieval Method)**
    - **Name:** G3.1
    - **Title:** Determine dish retrieval method
    - **Type:** Achieve
    - **Target Condition:** Correct retrieval method is selected.
    - **Relation:** OR
    - **Children:** G3.1.1 (Robot Retrieval), G3.1.2 (Cooperative Retrieval)

12. **Sub-Goal G3.1.1 (Robot Retrieval)**
    - **Name:** G3.1.1
    - **Title:** Robot retrieves dishes alone
    - **Type:** Achieve
    - **Target Condition:** Dishes are retrieved by the robot.
    - **Relation:** AND
    - **Children:** AT3.1 (Retrieve Dishes)

13. **Sub-Goal G3.1.2 (Cooperative Retrieval)**
    - **Name:** G3.1.2
    - **Title:** Cooperative dish retrieval
    - **Type:** Achieve
    - **Target Condition:** Dishes are retrieved with another robot or human.
    - **Relation:** AND
    - **Children:** AT3.2 (Cooperative Dish Retrieval)

14. **Sub-Goal G3.2 (Door Opening)**
    - **Name:** G3.2
    - **Title:** Open the room door

    - **Target Condition:** Door is opened successfully.

16. **Sub-Goal G3.2.2 (Human Opens Door)**
