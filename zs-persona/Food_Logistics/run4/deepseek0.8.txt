### Goal Model Decomposition

The mission is decomposed into a hierarchical goal model with AND/OR relationships, parallel or sequential execution, and appropriate task assignments. Below is the structured breakdown:

#### Goals

| **Name** | **Relation** | **Type** | **Target Condition/Enquired Information** | **Runtime Annotation** |
|----------|--------------|----------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| **G1**   | -            | Achieve  | Food delivered and dishes retrieved.      | Sequential (G2; G3)    |
| **G2**   | AND with G3  | Perform  | Execute food delivery tasks.              | Sequential (G2.1; G2.2; G2.3; G2.4) |
| **G3**   | AND with G2  | Perform  | Execute dish retrieval tasks.             | Sequential (G3.1; G3.2) |
| **G2.1** | OR with G2.2 | Achieve  | Direct delivery completed.                | Parallel (AT1)         |
| **G2.2** | AND with G2.3| Perform  | Fetch from tray.                          | Sequential (G2.2.1; G2.2.2; G2.2.3; G2.2.4) |

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1** is the root goal, decomposed into **G2** and **G3** in a sequential manner.
- **G2** is further decomposed into **G2.1** and **G2.2** in parallel, with **G2.2** requiring sequential execution of its sub-goals.
- **G2.2** includes a fallback to **G2.4** if necessary.
- **G3** is decomposed into **G3.1** and **G3.2**, with **G3.2** requiring sequential execution of its tasks.
- **G3.2.1** and **G3.2.2** are parallel tasks under **G3.2**.

This model ensures efficient and flexible execution, handling uncertainties and fallback scenarios effectively.
