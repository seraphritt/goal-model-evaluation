To model the mission as a goal model for a multi-robot system, we decompose the mission into a hierarchical structure of goals and tasks. The goal model captures the logical relationships and execution structures required to achieve the mission objectives.

### Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G1.1, G1.2, G1.3 [AND]**
- **G1.1 -> G1.1.1, G1.1.2 [OR]**
- **G1.1.2 -> G1.1.2.1, G1.1.2.2 [OR]**
- **G1.2 -> G1.2.1, G1.2.2 [OR]**
- **G1.2.2 -> G1.2.2.1, G1.2.2.2 [OR]**
- **G1.3 -> G1.3.1, G1.3.2 [OR]**

This goal model ensures that all aspects of the mission are covered, with clear hierarchical decomposition and appropriate execution structures.
