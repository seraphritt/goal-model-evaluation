**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Food Delivery**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Target Condition / Enquired Info** | **Relation** |
|----------|----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------------------------|--------------|
| G1: Deliver Food to Inpatient Room | Deliver a meal from the kitchen to the patient’s room, responding to individual kitchen requests. | `;` (sequential) | Perform | – | AND |
| G2: Obtain Delivery Order | Acquire all information needed to decide how the meal will be delivered. | `#` (parallel) | Perform | – | AND |
| G2.1: Query Inpatient Record | Retrieve patient details and presence of a companion from the hospital information system. | `-` | Query | *patient details, companion presence* | AND |
| G2.2: Determine Retrieval Capability | Find out whether the patient can retrieve the meal from the robot’s tray. | `-` | Query | *can patient retrieve from tray?* | AND |
| G3: Deliver Food | Choose the appropriate delivery method and execute it. | `FALLBACK(G3.1, G3.2)` | Perform | – | OR |
| G3.1: Deliver to Table | Place the meal directly on the patient’s table. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G3.2: Deliver to Tray | Place the meal on the robot’s tray for the patient to pick up. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G4: Retrieve Dishes | Collect dirty dishes from the patient’s room. | `FALLBACK(G4.1, FALLBACK(G4.2, G4.3))` | Perform | – | OR |
| G4.1: Unassisted Retrieval | Pick up dishes without human help. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G4.2: Two‑Robot Retrieval | Two robots cooperate to pick up dishes. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G4.3: Robot‑Human Retrieval | Robot waits for a human to help pick up dishes. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G5: Open Door | Open the patient’s room door, possibly with human assistance. | `FALLBACK(G5.1, G5.2)` | Perform | – | OR |
| G5.1: Robot Alone | Robot opens the door by itself. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G5.2: Robot‑Human | Robot waits for a human to open the door. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |

### Logical Relationships (Summary)

```
G1 -> G2, G3 [AND][;]
G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [AND][#]
G3 -> G3.1, G3.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G3.1, G3.2)]
G3.1 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][;]
G3.2 -> AT3, AT4, AT5, AT6, AT7 [AND][;]
G4 -> G4.1, G4.2, G4.3 [OR][FALLBACK(G4.1, FALLBACK(G4.2, G4.3))]
G4.1 -> AT8, AT9 [AND][;]
G4.2 -> AT10, AT11 [AND][;]
G4.3 -> AT12, AT13 [AND][;]
G5 -> G5.1, G5.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G5.1, G5.2)]
G5.1 -> AT14, AT15 [AND][;]
G5.2 -> AT16, AT17 [AND][;]
```

This model captures the hierarchical decomposition of the multi‑robot food‑delivery mission, distinguishing between **perform**, **query**, and **achieve** goals, and linking each goal to concrete, executable tasks with explicit location and robot‑count specifications.
