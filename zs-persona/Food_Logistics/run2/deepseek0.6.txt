Here is the structured goal model for the food delivery and dish retrieval mission in a hospital setting:

### Goals Table

| Name         | Text                                                                 | Runtime       | Goal Type    | Relation |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------|--------------|----------|
| G1           | Deliver food and retrieve dishes                                       | -             | Achieve     | AND       |
| G2           | Deliver food to the inpatient room                                    | ; G2.1, G2.2  | Perform     | OR        |
| G2.1         | Deliver food directly to the room table                               | -             | Perform     | AND       |
| G2.2         | Deliver food via the robot's tray                                     | FALLBACK(G2.2.1, G2.2.2) | Perform | OR        |
| G2.2.1       | Patient can retrieve food from tray                                    | -             | Query       | AND       |
| G2.2.2       | Patient cannot retrieve food from tray                                 | -             | Perform     | AND       |
| G2.3         | Handle multiple meals                                                 | -             | Perform     | AND       |
| G3           | Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room                         | -             | Perform     | AND       |
| G3.1         | Retrieve dishes unassisted                                            | -             | Perform     | OR        |
| G3.2         | Retrieve dishes with another robot                                    | -             | Perform     | OR        |
| G3.3         | Retrieve dishes with a human                                         | -             | Perform     | OR        |

### Tasks Table

| Name         | Text                                                                 | Relation | Location       | Number of Robots |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|----------------|------------------|
| AT1          | Deliver food directly to the room table                               | AND      | Hospital       | 1                |
| AT2          | Check patient's record for ability to retrieve food from tray        | AND      | Hospital       | 1                |
| AT3          | Alert if wrong meal is retrieved                                      | AND      | Hospital       | 1                |
| AT4          | Retrieve dishes unassisted                                            | AND      | Hospital       | 1                |
| AT5          | Retrieve dishes with another robot                                    | AND      | Hospital       | 2                |
| AT6          | Retrieve dishes with a human                                         | AND      | Hospital       | 1                |

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1** is an **Achieve Goal** that depends on both **G2** (delivering food) and **G3** (retrieving dishes), both of which are **AND** goals.
- **G2** is an **OR Goal** with subgoals **G2.1** (direct delivery) and **G2.2** (tray delivery). **G2.2** further decomposes into **G2.2.1** (patient can retrieve) and **G2.2.2** (patient cannot retrieve), using a **FALLBACK** structure.
- **G2.3** ensures multiple meals are handled correctly, a **Perform Goal**.
- **G3** is an **OR Goal** with subgoals for unassisted, robot-assisted, and human-assisted dish retrieval.
- Each task is an actionable step, with specific locations and robot numbers, ensuring clear operational steps for each goal.
