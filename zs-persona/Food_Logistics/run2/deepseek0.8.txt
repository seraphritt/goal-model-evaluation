Here is the goal model for the multi-robot system, structured into a hierarchical decomposition based on the mission description:

### Goal Model Table

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Target Condition/Enquired Information | Runtime Annotation | Relation |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|------------------------------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| G1      | Manage Food Delivery and Retrieval | Manage the delivery of food and retrieval of dirty dishes in the hospital. | Achieve | Food delivery and dish retrieval completed successfully. | ; | - |
| G2      | Deliver Food | Deliver food from the kitchen to the inpatient room. | Achieve | Food is successfully delivered to the inpatient room. | ; | AND |
| G3      | Retrieve Dirty Dishes | Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room. | Achieve | Dirty dishes are collected and returned. | ; | AND |
| G2.1    | Place Food on Table | Place the food directly on the inpatient room table. | Perform | Food is placed on the table. | ; | OR |
| G2.2    | Fetch Food from Tray | Fetch food from the robot's tray with cooperation if needed. | Achieve | Food is correctly retrieved from the tray. | FALLBACK(FetchFoodFromTrayWithRobot, FetchFoodFromTrayWithHuman) | OR |
| G2.2.1  | Check Inpatient Ability | Determine if the inpatient can retrieve food from the tray. | Query | Information on inpatient's ability to retrieve food. | - | AND |
| G2.2.2  | Indicate Correct Meal | Indicate which meal belongs to the inpatient. | Perform | Correct meal is indicated. | - | AND |
| G2.2.3  | Track Retrieval | Track when and where each meal is retrieved and alert if wrong. | Achieve | Correct meal is retrieved. | - | AND |
| G3.1    | Unassisted Retrieval | Retrieve dishes without assistance. | Perform | Dishes are collected. | ; | OR |
| G3.2    | Assisted by Another Robot | Retrieve dishes with another robot's help. | Perform | Dishes are collected with assistance. | ; | OR |
| G3.3    | Human Assisted Retrieval | Retrieve dishes with human assistance. | Perform | Dishes are collected with human help. | ; | OR |

### Task Model Table

| Task ID | Task Title | Task Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| AT1     | Navigate to Room | Navigate to the inpatient room. | Sequential | Inpatient Room | 1 |
| AT2     | Open Door | Open the room door. | Sequential | Room Entrance | 1 or 2 |
| AT3     | Place Food | Place food on the table. | Sequential | Inpatient Room | 1 |
| AT4     | Close Door | Close the room door. | Sequential | Room Entrance | 1 |
| AT5     | Collect Dishes | Collect dirty dishes. | Sequential | Inpatient Room | 1 |
| AT6     | Signal Cooperation | Signal if can open the door. | Sequential | Room Entrance | 1 or 2 |

### Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND]**
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [OR]**
- **G2.2 -> G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3 [AND]**
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3 [OR]**
- **G2.1 -> AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4 [Sequential]**
- **G3.1 -> AT1, AT2, AT5, AT4 [Sequential]**

This model ensures that the system can handle both delivery and retrieval tasks efficiently, considering various cooperation scenarios and uncertainties.
