### Task Model

| Name | Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|------|------|----------|----------|------------------|
| **AT1** | Retrieve patient’s ability to fetch food from the robot tray (query database) | AND (to G2.1) | hospital (information system) | 1 |
| **AT2** | Deliver food onto the patient’s table (requires manipulation skill) | AND (to G2.2.1) | inpatient room | 1 |
| **AT3** | Monitor patient’s retrieval of the meal (wait state) | AND (to G2.2.2) | inpatient room | 1 |
| **AT4** | Assign a unique meal ID to the patient and log it | AND (to G2.3) | robot’s internal memory | 1 |
| **AT5** | Monitor which meal is actually retrieved by the patient | AND (to G2.3) | inpatient room | 1 |
| **AT6** | Alert staff if the retrieved meal does not match the assigned ID | AND (to G2.4) | inpatient room | 1 |
| **AT7** | Retrieve dirty dishes from the room (unassisted) | AND (to G3.2.1) | inpatient room | 1 |
| **AT8** | Retrieve dirty dishes from the room (cooperative) | AND (to G3.2.2) | inpatient room | [2,2] |
| **AT9** | Open the room door cooperatively (robot‑human or robot‑robot) | AND (to G2.5, G3.3) | room door | [1,2] |
| **AT10** | Clean up the room after dish retrieval (e.g., dispose of trash) | AND (to G3.4) | inpatient room | 1 |
