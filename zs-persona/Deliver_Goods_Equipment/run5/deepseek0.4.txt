To design the goal model for the mission, we decompose the mission into hierarchical goals and tasks, considering the specified conditions and requirements. The goal model is structured as follows:

### Goal Model Summary

**Top-Level Goal:**
- **G1: Execute Resource Collection and Delivery Mission**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** Resources are collected from storage and delivered to the specified location.  
  - **Decomposition:** AND (G2, G3)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

**Sub-Goals:**
- **G2: Collection Phase**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** Resources are collected from all required storages.  
  - **Decomposition:** AND (G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G3: Delivery Phase**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** All collected resources are delivered to the specified location.  
  - **Decomposition:** AND (G3.1, G3.2, G3.3)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

**Sub-Goals of G2:**
- **G2.1: Plan Collection Route**  
  - **Type:** Perform  
  - **Target Condition:** A valid route is planned for collection.  
  - **Decomposition:** Leaf node.  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G2.2: Visit Each Storage**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** All storages are visited in the planned order.  
  - **Decomposition:** AND (G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G2.3: Request Resources**  
  - **Type:** Perform  
  - **Target Condition:** Resources are requested from each storage.  
  - **Decomposition:** Leaf node.  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G2.4: Handle Low Battery in Collection**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** Battery is recharged or mission is handed over.  
  - **Decomposition:** OR (G2.4.1, G2.4.2)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

**Sub-Goals of G3:**
- **G3.1: Plan Delivery Route**  
  - **Type:** Perform  
  - **Target Condition:** A valid route is planned for delivery.  
  - **Decomposition:** Leaf node.  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G3.2: Make Delivery Runs**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** All resources are delivered to the specified location.  
  - **Decomposition:** AND (G3.2.1, G3.2.2)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

- **G3.3: Handle Low Battery in Delivery**  
  - **Type:** Achieve  
  - **Target Condition:** Resources are returned to a checkpoint or mission is handed over.  
  - **Decomposition:** OR (G3.3.1, G3.3.2)  
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution.

### Tasks

**Tasks for G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3, G3.2.1, G3.2.2:**
- **AT1: Visit Storage [n]**  
  - **Type:** Task  
  - **Description:** Visit the nth storage to collect resources.  
  - **Location:** Storage [n]  
  - **Number of Robots:** 1  
  - **Relation:** AND.

**Tasks for G2.4.1, G3.3.1:**
- **AT2: Recharge Battery**  
  - **Type:** Task  
  - **Description:** Return to the recharging station and recharge the battery.  
  - **Location:** Recharging Station  
  - **Number of Robots:** 1  
  - **Relation:** AND.

**Tasks for G2.4.2, G3.3.2:**
- **AT3: Handover Mission**  
  - **Type:** Task  
  - **Description:** Assign the remaining task to another robot.  
  - **Location:** Current location  
  - **Number of Robots:** 1  
  - **Relation:** AND.

**Task for G3.3.3:**
- **AT4: Trigger Alert**  
  - **Type:** Task  
  - **Description:** Send an alert to the sector manager.  
  - **Location:** Sector Manager's Office  
  - **Number of Robots:** 1  
  - **Relation:** AND.

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND][;]**
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4 [AND][;]**
- **G2.2 -> G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G2.2.3 [AND][;]**
- **G2.4 -> G2.4.1, G2.4.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G2.4.1, G2.4.2)]**
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3 [AND][;]**
- **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [AND][;]**
- **G3.3 -> G3.3.1, G3.3.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G3.3.1, G3.3.2)]**

This goal model ensures that the mission is executed efficiently, with robots handling resource collection and delivery while managing battery levels and potential failures.
