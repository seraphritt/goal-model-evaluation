### Goal Model Design

The goal model is structured to capture the mission's requirements, decomposing the main objective into sub-goals and tasks, considering parallel and sequential execution, and handling fallback scenarios for battery issues.

---

### Goal Model Summary

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Target Condition | Runtime Annotation | Relation |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|--------------------|----------|
| G1      | Deliver Required Resources | Deliver resources to the requesting agent. | Achieve | Resources delivered to the specified location. |ParallelGroup1,ParallelGroup2 | AND |
| G2      | Collect Resources from Storage | Collect required resources from storage locations. | Perform | Resources collected and ready for delivery. |ParallelGroup1 | AND |
| G3      | Deliver Resources to Requesting Agent | Transport resources to the specified location. | Perform | Resources delivered successfully. |ParallelGroup2 | AND |

---

### Sub-Goals and Tasks

#### G2: Collect Resources from Storage
- **G2.1**: Identify Storage Locations
  - **Task**: Identify where resources are stored.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Storage facilities
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G2.2**: Estimate Waiting Time and Path
  - **Task**: Calculate waiting time and path length.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Planning module
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G2.3**: Move to Storage
  - **Task**: Navigate to identified storage location.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Storage facility
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G2.4**: Request Resources
  - **Task**: Send message to storage for resources.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Storage facility
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G2.5**: Handle Low Battery (10%)
  - **Task**: Return to recharge and assign mission to another robot.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Recharging station
  - **Robots**: 1

#### G3: Deliver Resources to Requesting Agent
- **G3.1**: Transport Resources
  - **Task**: Move resources to the specified location.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Delivery location
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G3.2**: Handle Low Battery (30%)
  - **Task**: Return resources to a checkpoint and assign remaining task.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Checkpoint
  - **Robots**: 1

- **G3.3**: Trigger Alert on Failure
  - **Task**: Send alert and report to sector manager if unable to return.
  - **Runtime**: Sequential
  - **Relation**: AND
  - **Location**: Sector manager's system
  - **Robots**: 1

---

### Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3**: Parallel execution for collection and delivery.
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4, G2.5**: Sequential execution for collection steps.
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3**: Sequential execution for delivery steps.

---

### Summary

The goal model captures the mission's phases, ensuring efficient resource management with fallback mechanisms for battery issues. It balances parallel and sequential tasks to optimize mission completion.
