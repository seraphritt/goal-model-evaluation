To design the goal model for the mission, we'll structure it into a hierarchical tree with goals and tasks, considering the phases, parallel execution, and fallback mechanisms. Here's the organized structure:

### Goal Model Structure

1. **G1: Execute Resource Collection and Delivery Mission**
   - **Goal Type**: Achieve
   - **Target Condition**: All resources collected and delivered successfully.
   - **Decomposition**: AND (both collection and delivery must be completed)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution (";")
     - Sub-goals: G2 (Resource Collection), G5 (Resource Delivery)

2. **G2: Resource Collection**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (all resources must be collected)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution ("#")
     - Sub-goals: G3, G4, ... (each for a specific resource)

3. **G3: Collect Resource A from Storage 1**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (collection and waiting for retrieval)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution (";")
     - Tasks: AT1 (Travel to Storage 1), AT2 (Request Resource A), AT3 (Wait for Retrieval)

4. **G4: Collect Resource B from Storage 2**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (collection and waiting for retrieval)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution (";")
     - Tasks: AT4 (Travel to Storage 2), AT5 (Request Resource B), AT6 (Wait for Retrieval)

5. **G5: Resource Delivery**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (all resources must be delivered)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Parallel execution ("#")
     - Sub-goals: G6, G7, ... (each for a specific resource)

6. **G6: Deliver Resource A to Destination**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (delivery runs)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution (";")
     - Tasks: AT7 (First Delivery Run), AT8 (Second Delivery Run), ... (as needed)

7. **G7: Deliver Resource B to Destination**
   - **Goal Type**: Perform
   - **Decomposition**: AND (delivery runs)
   - **Runtime Annotation**: Sequential execution (";")
     - Tasks: AT9 (First Delivery Run), AT10 (Second Delivery Run), ... (as needed)

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G5 [AND; Sequential]**
- **G2 -> G3, G4, ... [AND; Parallel]**
- **G3 -> AT1, AT2, AT3 [AND; Sequential]**
- **G4 -> AT4, AT5, AT6 [AND; Sequential]**
- **G5 -> G6, G7, ... [AND; Parallel]**

This structure ensures that all resources are collected and delivered efficiently, with parallel execution where possible, and fallback mechanisms for handling battery issues and failures.
