To model the mission description into a goal model, we decompose the mission into a hierarchical structure of goals and tasks, considering the specified conditions and relationships.

### Goal Model

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Relation | Target Condition | Runtime Annotation |
|--------|------------|-----------|-----------|----------|------------------|--------------------|
| G1     | Fulfill Resource Request | Ensure the requested resources are collected from storage and delivered to the specified location. | Achieve | - | Resources are collected and delivered | - |
| G2     | Collection | Collect the required resources from the storage locations. | Perform | AND (with G3) | Resources collected | FALLBACK(G2.1, G2.2) |
| G3     | Delivery | Deliver the collected resources to the specified location. | Perform | AND (with G2) | Resources delivered | - |
| G2.1   | Collect Alone | Collect resources from storage without battery issues. | Perform | OR (with G2.2) | Resources collected | - |
| G2.2   | Handoff Collection | Handoff collection to another robot due to low battery. | Achieve | OR (with G2.1) | Mission continues | - |
| G3.1   | Deliver Alone | Deliver resources without battery issues. | Perform | OR (with G3.2) | Resources delivered | - |
| G3.2   | Handoff Delivery | Handoff delivery to another robot due to low battery. | Achieve | OR (with G3.1) | Mission continues | - |
| G4     | Handle Multiple Resources | Collect multiple resources from different storages. | Perform | AND | All resources collected | # (parallel) |
| G4.1   | Collect Resource 1 | Collect resource 1 from storage 1. | Perform | AND (with G4.2) | Resource 1 collected | - |
| G4.2   | Collect Resource 2 | Collect resource 2 from storage 2. | Perform | AND (with G4.1) | Resource 2 collected | - |

### Task Model

| Task ID | Task Title | Task Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|---------|------------|-----------|----------|----------|-------------------|
| AT1     | Collect Resources | Collect resources from storage. | AND (with G2.1) | Storage | 1 |
| AT2     | Handoff Collection | Handoff collection to another robot. | AND (with G2.2) | Recharging Station | 1 |
| AT3     | Deliver Resources | Deliver resources to destination. | AND (with G3.1) | Destination | 1 |
| AT4     | Handoff Delivery | Handoff delivery to another robot. | AND (with G3.2) | Checkpoint | 1 |
| AT5     | Collect Resource 1 | Collect resource 1 from storage 1. | AND (with G4.1) | Storage 1 | 1 |
| AT6     | Collect Resource 2 | Collect resource 2 from storage 2. | AND (with G4.2) | Storage 2 | 1 |

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND]**
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G2.1, G2.2)]**
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2 [OR][FALLBACK(G3.1, G3.2)]**
- **G4 -> G4.1, G4.2 [AND][# (parallel)]**
- **G4.1 -> AT5 [AND]**
- **G4.2 -> AT6 [AND]**
- **G2.1 -> AT1 [AND]**
- **G2.2 -> AT2 [AND]**
- **G3.1 -> AT3 [AND]**
- **G3.2 -> AT4 [AND]**

This structure ensures that the mission is decomposed into manageable sub-tasks and goals, considering parallel execution and fallback mechanisms for robust mission completion.
