3. Summary of Logical Relationships

- **G1 → G2, G3**  
  – The overall mission (G1) requires first completing the collection phase (G2) and then the delivery phase (G3).  
  – This relationship is sequential (AND).

- **G2 → AT1, AT2, AT3**  
  – Within the collection phase (G2), the primary tasks (AT1 and AT2) must be executed sequentially.  
  – If the battery falls below 10%, the fallback alternative (AT3) is executed.  
  – This is modeled as a fallback structure: FALLBACK((AT1; AT2), AT3).

- **G3 → AT4, AT5, AT6, AT7**  
  – Within the delivery phase (G3), the primary tasks (AT4 and AT5) are executed sequentially.  
  – If the battery falls below 30%, the fallback alternative (AT6 and AT7) is executed.  
  – This is modeled as a fallback structure: FALLBACK((AT4; AT5), (AT6; AT7)).
