**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Delivery Mission**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Target Condition / Enquired Info** | **Relation** |
|----------|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| **G1: DeliverResources** | Deliver the requested resources to the specified destination. | `;` (sequential) | Achieve | `All requested resources are delivered to the destination.` | – |
| **G2: CollectionPhase** | Perform the collection of resources from the storage. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3: DeliveryPhase** | Perform the delivery of the collected resources to the destination. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.1: CalculateOrder** | Compute the optimal visiting order of storages based on waiting‑time + path cost. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.2: TravelToStorage** | Travel from current location to the selected storage. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.3: RequestResource** | Send a request to the storage for the specified resources. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.4: WaitForRetrieval** | Wait until the storage has retrieved the requested resources. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.5: BatteryHandling** | Handle low‑battery situations during collection. | `FALLBACK(G2.5.1, G2.5.2)` | Perform | – | OR |
| **G2.5.1: BatteryOk** | Battery level is above 10 % – continue collection. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G2.5.2: BatteryLow** | Battery level has fallen below 10 % – recharge and re‑assign. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3.1: TransportResource** | Carry the resources from the storage to the destination. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3.2: MultipleRuns** | Make as many trips as needed to deliver all resources. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3.3: DeliveryBatteryHandling** | Handle low‑battery situations during delivery. | `FALLBACK(G3.3.1, G3.3.2)` | Perform | – | OR |
| **G3.3.1: DeliveryBatteryOk** | Battery level is above 30 % – continue delivery. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3.3.2: DeliveryBatteryLow** | Battery level has fallen below 30 % – return to checkpoint and re‑assign. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G3.4: AlertOnFailure** | Trigger an alert and report to the sector manager if return to checkpoint fails. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |

---

**Task Model**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Relation** | **Location** | **Number of Robots** |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **AT1: TravelToStorage** | Move the robot from its current location to the target storage. | AND | `Storage` | 1 |
| **AT2: RequestResource** | Send a message to the storage with the exact resource specification. | AND | `Storage` | 1 |
| **AT3: WaitForRetrieval** | Wait until the storage confirms that the resources have been retrieved. | AND | `Storage` | 1 |
| **AT4: MonitorBattery** | Continuously monitor the robot’s battery level. | AND | `RobotCurrentLocation` | 1 |
| **AT5: RechargeBattery** | Move to the recharging station and recharge until battery ≥ 10 %. | AND | `RechargingStation` | 1 |
| **AT6: ReassignMission** | Notify another available robot and hand over the remaining mission. | AND | `RobotCurrentLocation` | 1 |
| **AT7: TransportResource** | Carry the resources from the storage to the destination. | AND | `Destination` | 1 |
| **AT8: MultipleRuns** | Perform successive trips until all resources are delivered. | AND | `Destination` | 1 |
| **AT9: MonitorDeliveryBattery** | Continuously monitor the robot’s battery during delivery. | AND | `RobotCurrentLocation` | 1 |
| **AT10: ReturnToCheckpoint** | Return the partially delivered resources to a checkpoint. | AND | `Checkpoint` | 1 |
| **AT11: TriggerAlert** | Send an alert and report to the sector manager. | AND | `SectorManager` | 1 |

### Logical Relationships

```
G1 -> G2, G3 [AND] (sequential)
G2 -> G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4, G2.5 [AND]
G2.5 -> G2.5.1, G2.5.2 [FALLBACK]
G2.5.1 -> AT4 [AND]
G2.5.2 -> AT5, AT6 [AND]
G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3, G3.4 [AND]
G3.3 -> G3.3.1, G3.3.2 [FALLBACK]
G3.3.1 -> AT9 [AND]
G3.3.2 -> AT10, AT6 [AND]
```

*Parallelism:*  
If multiple items are requested from different storages, the system can instantiate several **G2**/ **G3** pairs concurrently (each pair handling one item). Each pair runs independently, allowing parallel collection‑delivery cycles and reducing overall mission time.
