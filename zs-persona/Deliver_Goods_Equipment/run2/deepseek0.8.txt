### Goal Model Design

The goal model is structured to represent the mission's requirements, decomposing the main objective into sub-goals and tasks, considering parallel and sequential execution where appropriate.

### Summary of Logical Relationships

- **G1** (Collect and Deliver Resources) is an AND goal with **G2** (Collection Phase) and **G3** (Delivery Phase), executed in parallel.
- **G2** decomposes into **G2.1**, **G2.2**, **G2.3**, and **G2.4** in sequence.
  - **G2.4** decomposes into **G2.4.1** and **G2.4.2** in sequence.
- **G3** decomposes into **G3.1**, **G3.2**, and **G3.3** in sequence.
  - **G3.3** decomposes into **G3.3.1** and **G3.3.2** in sequence.
- **G2.3** and **G3.1** can be executed in parallel when multiple robots are assigned to collect multiple resources.

This model ensures efficient resource collection and delivery, leveraging parallel execution for multiple tasks and handling battery constraints gracefully.
