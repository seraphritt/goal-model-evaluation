### Notes on the Model  

* **Parallelism** – The top‑level mission is sequential (collection → delivery).  
  Within each phase, sub‑tasks are executed in parallel where possible (e.g., planning and moving can be overlapped).  
* **Fallbacks** – Battery‑low situations are handled via fallback decompositions, ensuring that a lower‑battery robot hands over the mission to a healthy robot.  
* **Failure Handling** – If a robot cannot return resources to a checkpoint, an alert and report are generated, satisfying the safety requirement.  
* **Scalability** – The model supports parallel collect‑deliver cycles for multiple items by instantiating multiple copies of the `Collect Resources` → `Deliver Resources` chain, each handled by a different robot.  

This goal model provides a clear, hierarchical decomposition of the mission into actionable tasks, ready for implementation in a multi‑robot coordination framework.
