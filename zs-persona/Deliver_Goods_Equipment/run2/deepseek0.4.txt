### Goal Model Design

The goal model is structured to represent the hierarchical decomposition of the mission into goals and tasks, considering the specified conditions and requirements.

---

### Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3 [AND]**
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2, G2.3 [ParallelGroup]**
- **G2.1 -> AT2.1.1, AT2.1.2, AT2.1.3 [SequentialGroup]**
- **G2 -> G2.4 [AND]**
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2, G3.3 [ParallelGroup]**
- **G3.1 -> AT3.1.1, AT3.1.2, AT3.1.3 [SequentialGroup]**
- **G3 -> G3.4 [AND]**

This model ensures that the mission is decomposed into manageable goals and tasks, with appropriate handling of battery issues and resource management.
