### Goal Model Design

The mission involves robots collecting resources and delivering them, with specific handling for battery levels and parallel tasks. The goal model is structured to reflect this process, using AND/OR decompositions and runtime annotations for execution.

### Goal and Task Tables

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Target Condition | Runtime Annotation | Relation |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| G1      | Execute Mission | Collect and deliver resources efficiently. | Achieve | Mission completion | # (Parallel) | - |
| G2      | Collection Phase | Gather resources from storage. | Perform | Resources collected | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G3      | Delivery Phase | Deliver resources to the destination. | Perform | Resources delivered | ; (Sequential) | AND |

#### Sub-Goals and Tasks for G2

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Target Condition | Runtime Annotation | Relation |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| G2.1    | Move to Storage | Navigate to storage location. | Perform | At storage | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G2.2    | Request Resources | Send request for required items. | Perform | Resources retrieved | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G2.3    | Wait for Resources | Wait until resources are ready. | Perform | Resources ready | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G2.4    | Battery Check | Monitor battery level. | Perform | Battery level >10% | FALLBACK(G2.5) | AND |
| G2.5    | Recharge and Assign | Recharge and assign to another robot. | Perform | Mission continues | - | - |

#### Sub-Goals and Tasks for G3

| Goal ID | Goal Title | Goal Text | Goal Type | Target Condition | Runtime Annotation | Relation |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| G3.1    | Move to Delivery | Navigate to delivery location. | Perform | At delivery point | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G3.2    | Deliver Resources | Transfer resources to the agent. | Perform | Resources delivered | ; (Sequential) | AND |
| G3.3    | Return to Checkpoint | Return resources if battery low. | Perform | At checkpoint | FALLBACK(G3.4) | AND |
| G3.4    | Recharge and Assign | Recharge and assign remaining task. | Perform | Mission continues | - | - |

#### Tasks for G2

| Task ID | Task Title | Task Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| AT1     | Navigate to Storage | Move to storage. | AND | Storage location | 1 |
| AT2     | Request Resources | Send resource request. | AND | Storage location | 1 |
| AT3     | Wait for Resources | Wait at storage. | AND | Storage location | 1 |
| AT4     | Recharge | Return to station. | AND | Recharge station | 1 |
| AT5     | Assign Mission | Handover to another robot. | AND | Recharge station | 1 |

#### Tasks for G3

| Task ID | Task Title | Task Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| AT6     | Navigate to Delivery | Move to delivery point. | AND | Delivery location | 1 |
| AT7     | Deliver | Transfer resources. | AND | Delivery location | 1 |
| AT8     | Return to Checkpoint | Go to checkpoint. | AND | Checkpoint | 1 |
| AT9     | Recharge | Return to station. | AND | Recharge station | 1 |
| AT10    | Assign Remaining | Handover to another robot. | AND | Checkpoint | 1 |

### Summary of Relationships

- **G1** is the root goal, decomposed into **G2** and **G3** in parallel.
- **G2** is decomposed into sequential sub-goals and tasks.
- **G3** is similarly decomposed into sequential sub-goals and tasks.
- Fallbacks are used for battery issues, ensuring mission continuity.

This model ensures efficient resource management with parallel execution and fallback mechanisms for robust mission handling.
