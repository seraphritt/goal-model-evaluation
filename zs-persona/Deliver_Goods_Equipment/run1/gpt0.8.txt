**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Resource Delivery Mission**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Relation** |
|----------|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| **G1: Execute Resource Delivery Mission** | Perform delivery of the requested resources to the designated agent. | `;` | Perform | AND (G2, G3, G5, G6) |
| **G2: Collection Phase** | Perform the collection of resources from the storages. | `;` | Perform | AND (G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4, G2.5) |
| **G2.1: Determine Storage Order** | Compute the optimal visit order of storages based on waiting‑time + path cost. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT1) |
| **G2.2: Navigate to Storage** | Drive to the selected storage location. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT2) |
| **G2.3: Request Resources** | Send a request message to the storage with the exact resource specification. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT3) |
| **G2.4: Wait for Retrieval** | Await acknowledgement that the resources have been taken from the storage. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT4) |
| **G2.5: Battery Check Low (Collection)** | Ensure the battery is above 10 %; otherwise recharge and re‑assign. | `FALLBACK(G2.5.1,G2.5.2)` | Perform | AND (G2.5.1, G2.5.2) |
| **G2.5.1: Battery ≥ 10 %** | Battery level is sufficient to continue the collection. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT5) |
| **G2.5.2: Battery < 10 %: Recharge & Re‑assign** | Recharge the battery and hand over the mission to another robot. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT6, AT7) |
| **G3: Delivery Phase** | Perform the delivery of the collected resources to the destination. | `;` | Perform | AND (G3.1, G3.2, G3.3) |
| **G3.1: Navigate to Delivery Location** | Drive to the specified delivery point. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT8) |
| **G3.2: Deliver Items** | Transfer the resources to the destination. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT9) |
| **G3.3: Battery Check Low (Delivery)** | Ensure the battery is above 30 %; otherwise return to a checkpoint and re‑assign. | `FALLBACK(G3.3.1,G3.3.2)` | Perform | AND (G3.3.1, G3.3.2) |
| **G3.3.1: Battery ≥ 30 %** | Battery level is sufficient to continue the delivery. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT16) |
| **G3.3.2: Battery < 30 %: Return & Re‑assign** | Return the resource to a checkpoint and hand over the remaining task to another robot. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT10, AT17) |
| **G5: Failure Handling** | Manage failures that occur during the mission. | `;` | Perform | AND (G5.1, G5.2, G5.3) |
| **G5.1: Failure to Return Resource to Checkpoint** | Detect that the robot failed to return the resource to a checkpoint. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT18) |
| **G5.2: Trigger Alert** | Send an alert to the sector manager. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT11) |
| **G5.3: Send Report** | Transmit the failure report to the sector manager. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT12) |
| **G6: Parallel Assignment** | Assign multiple robots to parallel collect‑deliver tasks to reduce mission time. | `#` | Perform | AND (G6.1, G6.2) |
| **G6.1: Allocate Robots to Parallel Tasks** | Allocate robots to the different collect‑deliver subtasks. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT13) |
| **G6.2: Determine Task Distribution** | Decide which robot handles which storage and delivery location. | `-` | Perform | AND (AT14) |
