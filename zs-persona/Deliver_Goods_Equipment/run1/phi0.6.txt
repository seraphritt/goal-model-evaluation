– In each phase, if the normal process cannot be completed because of low battery, a fallback branch is activated (using FALLBACK(...)).  
– Leaf goals use “-” as their runtime annotation.

Summary of Logical Relationships
──────────────────────────────

- G1 → G2, G3 [AND] (Sequential execution: first collect then deliver)
  - G2 → G2.1, G2.2 [Fallback]  
    • G2.1 is the normal collection process; if low battery occurs (10%), fallback to G2.2.
  - G3 → G3.1, G3.2 [Fallback]
    • G3.1 is the normal delivery process; if low battery occurs (30%), fallback to G3.2.
      – G3.2 → G3.2.1, G3.2.2 [Fallback]  
        ‣ G3.2.1: Return resource to checkpoint.  
        ‣ G3.2.2: Trigger alert and report failure if returning fails.

Task assignments (all tasks are leaves in their parent goals):
- G2.1 → AT1, AT2 [AND] (the collection phase is performed by navigating and then requesting retrieval)
- G2.2 → AT3, AT4 [AND] (handle low battery during collection)
- G3.1 → AT5 [Single] (normal delivery execution)
- G3.2.1 → AT6 [Single] (return resource to checkpoint)
- G3.2.2 → AT7 [Single] (trigger alert if checkpoint return fails)

This model captures the mission’s main phases, including contingency handling for low battery conditions and the possibility of parallel execution when multiple items are required from different storages.
