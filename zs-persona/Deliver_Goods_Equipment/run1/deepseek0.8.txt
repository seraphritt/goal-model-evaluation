### Goal Model Decomposition

Below is the hierarchical decomposition of the mission description into a goal model. The goal model is structured using **AND** and **OR** decompositions, along with runtime annotations for parallel and sequential execution.

| G1            | Deliver requested resources to the specified location.                                   | "-"                      | Achieve     | -        |
| G2            | Collect resources from storage locations.                                                 | "#" (parallel)           | Perform     | AND      |
| G3            | Deliver collected resources to the specified location.                                    | "-"                      | Perform     | AND      |

### Explanation of the Goal Model

1. **Root Goal (G1): Deliver Requested Resources**
   - The primary goal is to deliver resources from storage to a specified location.
   - This involves two sub-goals: collection (G2) and delivery (G3).

2. **Collection Phase (G2)**
   - Robots collect resources from multiple storage locations in parallel.
   - Each storage location is represented as a task (AT1).

3. **Delivery Phase (G3)**
   - After collecting resources, robots deliver them to the specified location (AT2).
   - Delivery is handled sequentially after collection.

4. **Battery Management**
   - If the battery drops to 10% during collection, the robot recharges (G5).
   - If the battery drops to 30% during delivery, the robot returns resources to a checkpoint and hands over the task to another robot (G6).

---

### Summary

The goal model is structured to ensure that robots collect and deliver resources efficiently while managing their battery levels. The use of parallel execution for collection ensures faster mission completion when multiple robots are involved. Sequential execution is used for delivery to ensure resources are delivered correctly after collection. Battery management goals ensure robots can recharge or hand over tasks safely if needed.
