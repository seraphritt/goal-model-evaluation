### Summary of Decomposition

1. **G1** is the top-level goal to deliver resources. It is decomposed into **G2** (collection phase) and **G3** (delivery phase) in a **sequential (AND)** manner.
2. **G2** is further decomposed into:
   - **G2.1**: Visiting storages in the optimal order.
   - **G2.2**: Requesting resources from the storage.
   - **G2.3**: Handling low battery in the collection phase.
3. **G3** is further decomposed into:
   - **G3.1**: Delivering all resources to the specified location.
   - **G3.2**: Handling low battery in the delivery phase.
4. **G2.3** and **G3.2** use a **FALLBACK** operator to handle battery-related failures.
5. Tasks are assigned to individual robots to perform specific actions (e.g., visiting storages, requesting resources, delivering resources).

This decomposition ensures that the mission is executed efficiently while handling potential failures and resource constraints.
