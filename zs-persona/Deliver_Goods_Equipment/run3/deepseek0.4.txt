To design the goal model for the mission, we break it down into hierarchical goals and tasks, considering the sequential and parallel execution as well as fallback scenarios. Here's the structured goal model:

### Goal Model

**Top-Level Goal:**
- **G1: Deliver Required Resources**
  - **Goal Type:** Achieve
  - **Target Condition:** All resources are delivered to the specified location.
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Sequential execution of G2 and G3.

**Sub-Goals:**
- **G2: Collect Resources from Storages**
  - **Goal Type:** Perform
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Parallel execution of G2.1, G2.2, ..., G2.n (OR decomposition).
  - **Decomposition:** Each G2.i is an AND goal with tasks to go to storage, request resources, and wait.

- **G3: Deliver Collected Resources**
  - **Goal Type:** Perform
  - **Runtime Annotation:** Parallel execution of AT3.1, AT3.2, ..., AT3.m (OR decomposition).
  - **Decomposition:** Each AT3.i is a task to deliver resources to a specific location.

### Detailed Breakdown

1. **G1: Deliver Required Resources**
   - **Type:** Achieve
   - **Relation:** AND (sequential execution of G2 and G3)
   - **Runtime:** ;

2. **G2: Collect Resources from Storages**
   - **Type:** Perform
   - **Relation:** OR (parallel execution of sub-goals)
   - **Runtime:** #
   - **Sub-Goals:** G2.1, G2.2, ..., G2.n (each an AND goal)

3. **G3: Deliver Collected Resources**
   - **Type:** Perform
   - **Relation:** OR (parallel execution of tasks)
   - **Runtime:** #
   - **Tasks:** AT3.1, AT3.2, ..., AT3.m

4. **G2.1: Collect Resources from Storage 1**
   - **Type:** Perform
   - **Relation:** AND (sequential tasks)
   - **Runtime:** ;
   - **Tasks:** AT2.1.1 (Go to Storage 1), AT2.1.2 (Request Resources), AT2.1.3 (Wait for Retrieval)

5. **AT2.1.1: Go to Storage 1**
   - **Type:** Task
   - **Location:** Storage 1
   - **Number of Robots:** 1

6. **AT2.1.2: Request Resources**
   - **Type:** Task
   - **Location:** Storage 1
   - **Number of Robots:** 1

7. **AT2.1.3: Wait for Retrieval**
   - **Type:** Task
   - **Location:** Storage 1
   - **Number of Robots:** 1

8. **AT3.1: Deliver Resources to Location A**
   - **Type:** Task
   - **Location:** Location A
   - **Number of Robots:** 1 or more

### Summary

- **G1** ensures that resources are both collected and delivered sequentially.
- **G2** handles collection from multiple storages in parallel, each requiring a series of tasks.
- **G3** manages delivery in parallel, ensuring efficient distribution.

This model efficiently handles the mission requirements, including parallel execution for time reduction and fallback mechanisms for battery issues.
