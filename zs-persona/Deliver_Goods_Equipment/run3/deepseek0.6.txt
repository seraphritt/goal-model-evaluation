### Summary of Logical Relationships

- **G1 -> G2, G3**: AND (sequential execution)
- **G2 -> G2.1, G2.2**: AND (sequential execution)
- **G2.1 -> G2.1.1, G2.1.2, ...**: OR (parallel execution)
- **G2.2 -> G2.2.1, G2.2.2**: FALLBACK(G2.2.1, G2.2.2)
- **G3 -> G3.1, G3.2**: AND (sequential execution)
- **G3.1 -> G3.1.1, G3.1.2, ...**: OR (parallel execution)
- **G3.2 -> G3.2.1, G3.2.2**: FALLBACK(G3.2.1, G3.2.2)

Each goal and task is related through the specified decompositions, ensuring the mission is executed efficiently and robustly.
