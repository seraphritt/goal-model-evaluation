**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Resource Delivery Mission**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Target Condition / Enquired Info** | **Relation** |
|----------|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| G1 – Complete Mission | Complete the resource delivery mission by collecting and delivering required resources. | `;` (sequential) | Perform | – | AND |
| G2 – Collect Resources | Collect required resources from storage locations. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G2.1 – Plan Order | Plan the order of visiting storage locations based on waiting time and path. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| G2.2 – Request Resources | Send request to storage for specified resources. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| G2.3 – Wait Retrieval | Wait until resources are retrieved from storage. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| G2.4 – Low‑Battery Handling (Collection) | Handle low‑battery situation during the collection phase. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G3 – Deliver Resources | Deliver collected resources to the specified location. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |
| G3.1 – Make Delivery Runs | Transport resources to destination. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| G3.2 – Low‑Battery Handling (Delivery) | Handle low‑battery situation during the delivery phase. | `;` | Perform | – | AND |

| **Name** | **Text** | **Relation** | **Location** | **Number of Robots** |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|
| AT1 – Plan Order | Compute optimal visiting order for storage locations. | AND | Planning Module | 1 |
| AT2 – Request Resources | Send request message to storage with resource specification. | AND | Storage | 1 |
| AT3 – Wait Retrieval | Wait until storage confirms resource retrieval. | AND | Storage | 1 |
| AT4 – Return to Recharge | Return robot to recharge station due to low battery. | AND | Recharge Station | 1 |
| AT5 – Assign Mission to Another Robot | Assign remaining collection task to another robot. | AND | Control Center | 1 |
| AT6 – Transport Resources to Destination | Transport resources from storage to destination location. | AND | Destination | 1 |
| AT7 – Return Resource to Checkpoint | Return resource to checkpoint due to low battery during delivery. | AND | Checkpoint | 1 |
| AT8 – Assign Remaining Task to Another Robot | Assign remaining delivery task to another robot. | AND | Control Center | 1 |
| AT9 – Trigger Alert and Report | Trigger alert and send report to sector manager if resource cannot be returned to checkpoint. | AND | Control Center | 1 |

### Logical Relationships

- **G1 → G2, G3** [AND][;] (Complete Mission requires both Collection and Delivery in sequence)  
- **G2 → G2.1, G2.2, G2.3, G2.4** [AND][;] (Collect Resources requires planning, requesting, waiting, and low‑battery handling in sequence)  
- **G2.1 → AT1** [AND] (Goal Plan Order is achieved by task AT1)  
- **G2.2 → AT2** [AND] (Goal Request Resources is achieved by task AT2)  
- **G2.3 → AT3** [AND] (Goal Wait Retrieval is achieved by task AT3)  
- **G2.4 → AT4, AT5** [AND][;] (Low‑battery handling during collection requires returning to recharge and re‑assigning the mission in sequence)  
- **G3 → G3.1, G3.2** [AND][;] (Deliver Resources requires making delivery runs and handling low battery during delivery in sequence)  
- **G3.1 → AT6** [AND] (Goal Make Delivery Runs is achieved by task AT6)  
- **G3.2 → AT7, AT8, AT9** [AND][;] (Low‑battery handling during delivery requires returning to checkpoint, re‑assigning the remaining task, and triggering an alert in sequence)  

This goal‑task decomposition captures the full multi‑robot mission, including sequencing, parallelism, fallback handling, and battery‑management contingencies.
