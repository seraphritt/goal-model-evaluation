## 1. Modeling rationale and assumptions

I decomposed the mission into two main service capabilities:

1. **Food delivery from kitchen to inpatient room**, triggered by kitchen delivery requests.
2. **Dirty-dish retrieval from inpatient room**, triggered either as part of service or by someone in the room calling a robot.

The mission has several **uncertainty and cooperation points**:

* The patient may or may not be able to fetch food.
* A companion or nurse may or may not be available.
* Another robot may be needed for cooperation.
* The room door may require help from a human or another robot.
* Wrong meal retrieval must be detected and alerted.
* Dirty-dish collection may be done by one robot, two robots, or robot-human cooperation.

Because of these alternatives, many decompositions are modeled as **OR with FALLBACK runtime**, meaning the system tries one feasible strategy and falls back to another when the first one is unavailable or fails.

# 5. Logical relationships and justifications

## Top-level mission

* **G1 -> G2, G14 [AND][parallel `#`]**

  * **Justification**: The system must support both food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval. They are both part of the inpatient food-service mission. Parallel runtime is appropriate because the hospital fleet may receive delivery and pickup requests independently.

---

## Food delivery branch

* **G2 -> G3, G4, G5, G7 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: A food order must first be received, then the meals must be loaded, then the robot must reach the room, and only then can the meal be handed off.

* **G3 -> AT1 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Obtaining the kitchen delivery request is mandatory because the delivery is order-based.

* **G4 -> AT2, AT3 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot must first go to the kitchen pickup point and then load the requested meals.

* **G5 -> AT4, G6 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot must navigate to the room before obtaining room access.

* **G6 -> AT5, AT6 [OR][FALLBACK(AT5, AT6)]**

  * **Justification**: Door opening can be supported by a human or another robot. Either one is sufficient, so this is an OR decomposition. Fallback handles unavailability of one cooperation partner.

---

## Meal handoff branch

* **G7 -> G8, G9, G13 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot first assesses the context, then performs the meal handoff, and finally monitors retrieval and exceptions.

* **G8 -> AT7, AT8 [AND][parallel `#`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot needs both patient/context information and live helper availability. These checks can be performed in parallel because they query different information sources.

* **G9 -> G10, G11, G12 [OR][FALLBACK(G10, FALLBACK(G11, G12))]**

  * **Justification**: The food can be made available through table placement, human tray retrieval, or robot-assisted tray retrieval. Only one successful handoff mode is required.

* **G10 -> AT9 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Table placement is achieved by executing the manipulation task that moves the correct meal onto the table.

* **G11 -> AT10, AT11 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: When a human retrieves from the tray, the robot must first indicate the correct meal and then wait for the person to retrieve it.

* **G12 -> AT12, AT13 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Robot-assisted tray fetch requires first requesting a helper robot and then executing the cooperative transfer.

* **G13 -> AT14, AT15, AT16 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot must record retrieval, check correctness, and alert if a wrong meal is retrieved. These actions are all necessary to satisfy the monitoring requirement.

---

## Dirty-dish retrieval branch

* **G14 -> G15, G16, G18, G22 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Dirty-dish retrieval begins with a pickup request, then the robot reaches the room, collects dishes, and transports them to the designated destination.

* **G15 -> AT17, AT18 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot must receive the dish pickup call and record whether the caller can open the door.

* **G16 -> AT19, G17 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: The robot must navigate to the room before door-opening cooperation can occur.

* **G17 -> AT20, AT21 [OR][FALLBACK(AT20, AT21)]**

  * **Justification**: Door opening can be performed with human cooperation or robot cooperation. Either is sufficient.

* **G18 -> G19, G20, G21 [OR][FALLBACK(G19, FALLBACK(G20, G21))]**

  * **Justification**: Dirty-dish retrieval may occur unassisted, with two robots, or with robot-human cooperation. Only one successful collection mode is needed.

* **G19 -> AT22, AT23 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: In unassisted collection, the robot must locate the dishes and then load them.

* **G20 -> AT24, AT25 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Two-robot collection requires requesting the second robot and then executing the cooperative loading.

* **G21 -> AT26, AT27 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: Human-assisted collection requires coordination with the human and then loading the dishes.

* **G22 -> AT28, AT29 [AND][sequential `;`]**

  * **Justification**: After collection, dishes must be transported and unloaded at the cleaning or collection area.
