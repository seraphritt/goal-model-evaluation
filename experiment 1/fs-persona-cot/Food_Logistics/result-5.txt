**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Food & Dish Delivery**

Below is a fully‑annotated goal model that follows the specification.  
All goals are numbered depth‑first (G1, G2 …).  
All tasks are numbered depth‑first (AT1, AT2 …).  
Each goal/table entry contains a *Runtime* annotation, *Goal Type*, a *Target Condition* or *Enquired Information*, the *Relation* between its children, and a justification.

### Remarks on Justifications

* **Parallel vs Sequential** – Most sub‑tasks that are independent (e.g., opening the door and waiting for human) are marked parallel `#`; steps that must happen in order (placing meal, announcing, monitoring, alert) are marked sequential `;`.
* **Fallback** – The decision between table delivery and tray delivery is a classic fallback: try table first, if it fails (e.g., patient cannot fetch), then try tray.
* **OR vs AND** – When a goal has multiple possible ways to be satisfied (e.g., picking up dishes), OR is used; when all child tasks must be done (e.g., deliver meal to tray involves several sequential steps), AND is used.
* **Runtime `-`** – Leaf goals or tasks that do not control execution flow simply use `-`.

This model provides a clear, hierarchical view of the multi‑robot mission, indicating how higher‑level goals decompose into actionable tasks while respecting the mission’s constraints and uncertainties.
