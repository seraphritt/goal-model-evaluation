Below is a **goal model decomposition** for the hospital food-delivery multi-robot mission. I treat the system as a service that must support two major capabilities: **food delivery** and **dirty-dish retrieval**. The uncertainty about patient/helper availability is modeled with **Query goals** and **FALLBACK/OR decompositions**.

---

# 1. Design Rationale

The mission contains three important kinds of requirements:

1. **Food must be delivered order-by-order**

   * This implies a sequential workflow: receive request, prepare meals, navigate, access room, and perform delivery.

2. **Delivery has alternative handoff strategies**

   * The robot may place food directly on the table, but only if it has a special manipulation skill.
   * Otherwise, the meal may be fetched from the tray by a patient, companion, nurse, or another robot.
   * Because availability/capability is uncertain, this naturally becomes an **OR/FALLBACK structure**.

3. **Traceability and safety are required**

   * The robot may carry multiple meals.
   * It must indicate which meal belongs to which inpatient.
   * It must track when and where each meal was retrieved.
   * It must alert if the wrong meal is retrieved.

4. **Dirty dishes must also be retrieved**

   * Dish retrieval may happen unassisted, with two robots, or with robot-human cooperation.
   * Door opening may require a human or another robot.
   * A person in the room may call the robot and indicate whether they can open the door.
