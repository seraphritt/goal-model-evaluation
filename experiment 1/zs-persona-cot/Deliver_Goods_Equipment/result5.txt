**Logical Relationships**

- **G1 → G2, G3, G4, G5**  [**AND**][**parallel**]  
  *The mission is achieved only when all four sub‑goals are fulfilled; battery‑management and failure‑handling run concurrently with collection and delivery.*

- **G2 → AT1, AT2, AT3, AT4**  [**AND**][**sequential**]  
  *The collection phase follows the strict sequence: navigate, request, wait, and pick.*

- **G3 → AT5, AT6, AT7**  [**AND**][**sequential**]  
  *The delivery phase loads, transports, then unloads the resources.*

- **G4 → AT8, AT9**  [**AND**][**sequential**]  
  *Battery monitoring precedes mission reassignment.*

- **G5 → AT10, AT11**  [**AND**][**sequential**]  
  *Alerting is followed by reporting.*

---

**Justification of Goal Types**

| Goal | Chosen Type | Reason |
|------|-------------|--------|
| G1 (Mission) | **Achieve** | The goal specifies a future world state: all requested resources are at the destination. |
| G2, G4, G5 | **Perform** | These goals describe actions that must be carried out; they do not define a target state beyond task completion. |
| G3 (Delivery) | **Achieve** | Like G1, delivering resources changes the world state by placing the resources at the destination. |

**Target Conditions**

- **G1:** *Resources delivered to destination and mission acknowledged.*
- **G3:** *Resources present at destination and delivery confirmed.*

**Enquired Information**

- *None of the defined goals are query goals; the mission only requires actions, not informational queries.*
