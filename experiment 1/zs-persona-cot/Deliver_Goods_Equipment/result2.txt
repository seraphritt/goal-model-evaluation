## Goal & Task Model – Multi‑Robot Resource Delivery

Below is a complete, depth‑first numbered goal model that captures the mission description.  
All goals are linked to the appropriate tasks and the logical relationships between them are explicit.

---

### 1. Goal Table

| Name                               | Text                                                                                                                  | Runtime      | Goal Type   | Correct Goal Type | Target / Enquired                                                                                 | Relation |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **G1 – DeliverRequestedResources** | Ensure that all requested resources are delivered to the specified destination and the request is marked “Fulfilled”. | `(G2;G3)#G4` | **Achieve** | **Achieve**       | **Target Condition**: *All requested resources are at destination and request status = Fulfilled* | AND      |
| **G2 – CollectionPhase**           | Perform the collection of the required resources from the designated storage locations.                               | `;`          | **Perform** | **Perform**       | –                                                                                                 | AND      |
| **G3 – DeliveryPhase**             | Perform the delivery of the collected resources to the destination location.                                          | `;`          | **Perform** | **Perform**       | –                                                                                                 | AND      |
| **G4 – FailureHandling**           | Handle failures that occur during resource return to a checkpoint and report them to the sector manager.              | `#`          | **Perform** | **Perform**       | –                                                                                                 | AND      |


**Justification of Goal Types**

* **G1** – *Achieve*: The mission’s objective is to bring the world into a state where the requested resources are at the destination and the request is considered completed.
* **G2, G3, G4** – *Perform*: These sub‑goals are strictly action‑oriented; they are satisfied when the corresponding activities (collecting, delivering, or failure handling) are carried out.

### 5. Remarks

* **Depth‑first numbering**: Goals and tasks follow a depth‑first traversal (G1 → G2 → AT1 … G3 → AT4 … G4 → AT9 …).
* **Runtime annotations**:  
  * `;` – sequential execution of child nodes.  
  * `#` – parallel execution of child nodes.  
  * `(G2;G3)#G4` – collection and delivery are sequential, while failure handling runs in parallel.  
* **Relations**: All parent–child links use **AND**, because each sub‑goal/task must be completed for the parent to be satisfied.
* **Goal types**: Only the top‑level goal is an *Achieve* goal; all other goals are *Perform* goals, reflecting the purely operational nature of the mission.

This model can now be instantiated in a multi‑robot planning framework, with each robot executing the appropriate tasks and handling battery and failure contingencies as specified.
