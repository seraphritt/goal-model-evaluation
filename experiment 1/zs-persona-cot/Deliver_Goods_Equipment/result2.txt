## Goal Model — Resource Collection and Delivery Mission

### Goal Table

| Name                                                 | Text                                                                                                                           | Runtime             | Goal Type   | Target Condition / Enquired Information                                                                                    | Relation |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **G1: Fulfill Resource Delivery Request**            | Ensure that the required resources are collected from storage and delivered to the requesting agent at the specified location. | `G2;G5;G9;G18`      | **Achieve** | All requested resources are delivered to the requesting agent, or the mission is safely reassigned/escalated when needed.  | AND      |
| **G2: Obtain Mission Request Information**           | Obtain the information needed to execute the resource delivery request.                                                        | `AT1;G3;G4`         | **Query**   | Requested resources, quantities, requesting agent, and target delivery location.                                           | AND      |
| **G3: Identify Required Resources**                  | Determine which resources and quantities are required.                                                                         | `-`                 | **Query**   | Resource type, quantity, and specifications required by the requesting agent.                                              | AND      |
| **G4: Identify Requesting Agent and Destination**    | Determine who requested the resources and where they must be delivered.                                                        | `-`                 | **Query**   | Requesting agent identity and specified delivery location.                                                                 | AND      |
| **G5: Plan Collection and Allocation**               | Plan where resources must be collected and how robots should be assigned.                                                      | `G6;G7;G8`          | **Achieve** | A valid collection plan exists, including storage locations, visit order, and robot allocation.                            | AND      |
| **G6: Locate Candidate Storages**                    | Identify the storage places where the requested resources can be found.                                                        | `-`                 | **Query**   | Storage locations containing each required resource.                                                                       | AND      |
| **G7: Define Storage Visit Order**                   | Define the order of storage visits based on estimated waiting time plus robot path cost.                                       | `-`                 | **Query**   | Ordered list of storages ranked by waiting time plus travel path.                                                          | AND      |
| **G8: Allocate Robots to Collection-Delivery Tasks** | Decide whether one robot or multiple robots should perform the mission.                                                        | `FALLBACK(AT2,AT3)` | **Achieve** | Robots are assigned to the mission, prioritizing parallel assignment when multiple storages/items make it beneficial.      | OR       |
| **G9: Execute Resource Transport Mission**           | Execute the assigned collection and delivery work.                                                                             | `G10;G13`           | **Achieve** | Required resources are retrieved from storage and transported to the target location.                                      | AND      |
| **G10: Complete Collection Phase**                   | Collect the required resources from the selected storage locations.                                                            | `FALLBACK(G11,G12)` | **Achieve** | Resources are retrieved from storage, or the mission is reassigned if the robot battery becomes too low during collection. | OR       |
| **G11: Retrieve Resources from Storage**             | Go to each storage, request the required resources, and wait until they are retrieved.                                         | `AT4;AT5;AT6;AT7`   | **Achieve** | Requested resources are physically obtained by the assigned robot.                                                         | AND      |
| **G12: Handle Low Battery During Collection**        | Handle the case where the assigned robot reaches low battery during the collection phase.                                      | `AT8;AT9;AT10`      | **Achieve** | Robot returns to recharging station and the remaining mission is assigned to another robot.                                | AND      |
| **G13: Complete Delivery Phase**                     | Deliver all collected resources to the specified location, making as many runs as necessary.                                   | `FALLBACK(G14,G15)` | **Achieve** | All resources are delivered, or the remaining delivery task is safely transferred to another robot.                        | OR       |
| **G14: Perform Delivery Runs**                       | Transport the resources to the specified location through one or more delivery runs.                                           | `AT11;AT12;AT13`    | **Achieve** | Every required resource batch reaches the requesting agent’s specified location.                                           | AND      |
| **G15: Handle Low Battery During Delivery**          | Handle the case where the assigned robot reaches low battery during the delivery phase.                                        | `FALLBACK(G16,G17)` | **Achieve** | Resource is safely placed at a checkpoint and the remaining task is reassigned; otherwise, failure is escalated.           | OR       |
| **G16: Transfer Remaining Delivery via Checkpoint**  | Return the resource to a checkpoint and assign the remaining task to another robot.                                            | `AT14;AT15;AT16`    | **Achieve** | Resource is positioned at a known checkpoint and another robot receives the remaining delivery task.                       | AND      |
| **G17: Escalate Checkpoint Return Failure**          | Trigger an alert and send a report to the sector manager if the resource cannot be returned to a checkpoint.                   | `AT17;AT18`         | **Perform** | The required alert and sector manager report are performed after checkpoint return failure.                                | AND      |
| **G18: Close Mission**                               | Confirm that the mission result has been recorded and communicated.                                                            | `AT19;AT20`         | **Perform** | Delivery completion or escalation status is communicated and logged.                                                       | AND      |

## Task Table

| Name                                                 | Text                                                                                                                                   | Relation | Location                                         | Number of Robots |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| **AT1: Receive Resource Request**                    | Receive the request for resources from the requesting agent or system.                                                                 | AND      | Requesting system / hospital unit                | 1                |
| **AT2: Assign Multiple Robots in Parallel**          | Assign multiple robots to collect and deliver resources from different storages in parallel when this reduces mission completion time. | OR       | Dispatch/control center                          | `[2,n]`          |
| **AT3: Assign Single Robot**                         | Assign one robot to perform the full collection and delivery mission when parallelization is not needed or not available.              | OR       | Dispatch/control center                          | 1                |
| **AT4: Navigate to Storage**                         | Move the assigned robot to the next storage according to the planned visit order.                                                      | AND      | Storage locations                                | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT5: Send Resource Request to Storage**            | Send a message to the storage with the precise specification of the requested resources.                                               | AND      | Storage location                                 | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT6: Wait for Resource Retrieval**                 | Wait until the storage retrieves and provides the requested resources.                                                                 | AND      | Storage location                                 | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT7: Load Retrieved Resources**                    | Receive and load the retrieved resources onto the robot for transport.                                                                 | AND      | Storage location                                 | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT8: Return to Recharging Station**                | If battery reaches 10% during collection, return the robot to the recharging station.                                                  | AND      | Recharging station                               | 1                |
| **AT9: Reassign Collection Mission**                 | Assign the remaining collection mission to another available robot.                                                                    | AND      | Dispatch/control center                          | 1                |
| **AT10: Transfer Collection Context**                | Transfer pending resource specifications, storage order, and mission status to the replacement robot.                                  | AND      | Dispatch/control center                          | 1                |
| **AT11: Navigate to Delivery Location**              | Move the robot carrying resources to the specified delivery location.                                                                  | AND      | Specified delivery location                      | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT12: Deliver Resource Batch**                     | Deliver the current batch of resources to the requesting agent or target location.                                                     | AND      | Specified delivery location                      | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT13: Repeat Delivery Runs**                       | Repeat delivery trips as many times as necessary until all resources are transported.                                                  | AND      | Between storage/checkpoint and delivery location | `[1,n]`          |
| **AT14: Return Resource to Checkpoint**              | If battery reaches 30% during delivery, move the resource to a checkpoint.                                                             | AND      | Checkpoint                                       | 1                |
| **AT15: Register Resource Checkpoint Position**      | Record the exact checkpoint where the resource was placed so the replacement robot can find it.                                        | AND      | Checkpoint / control system                      | 1                |
| **AT16: Assign Remaining Delivery to Another Robot** | Assign the remaining delivery task to another robot and provide the resource checkpoint location.                                      | AND      | Dispatch/control center                          | 1                |
| **AT17: Trigger Alert**                              | Trigger an alert if the robot fails to return the resource to a checkpoint.                                                            | AND      | Control system                                   | 1                |
| **AT18: Send Report to Sector Manager**              | Send a failure report to the sector manager after checkpoint return failure.                                                           | AND      | Control system / sector manager office           | 1                |
| **AT19: Notify Mission Result**                      | Notify the requesting agent or system that the mission was completed, reassigned, or escalated.                                        | AND      | Requesting system / delivery location            | 1                |
| **AT20: Log Mission Execution**                      | Record mission execution data, including resources delivered, robot assignment, battery events, checkpoint use, and failures.          | AND      | Control system                                   | 1                |

## Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G5, G9, G18 [AND][sequential: `G2;G5;G9;G18`]**

* **G2 -> AT1, G3, G4 [AND][sequential: `AT1;G3;G4`]**

* **G5 -> G6, G7, G8 [AND][sequential: `G6;G7;G8`]**

* **G8 -> AT2, AT3 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(AT2,AT3)`]**
  Multiple-robot assignment is preferred when different resources/storages allow parallel execution; otherwise, a single robot is assigned.

* **G9 -> G10, G13 [AND][sequential: `G10;G13`]**

* **G10 -> G11, G12 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(G11,G12)`]**
  Normal collection is attempted first; if the robot battery reaches 10%, the collection contingency is executed.

* **G11 -> AT4, AT5, AT6, AT7 [AND][sequential: `AT4;AT5;AT6;AT7`]**

* **G12 -> AT8, AT9, AT10 [AND][sequential: `AT8;AT9;AT10`]**

* **G13 -> G14, G15 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(G14,G15)`]**
  Normal delivery is attempted first; if battery reaches 30%, the delivery contingency is executed.

* **G14 -> AT11, AT12, AT13 [AND][sequential: `AT11;AT12;AT13`]**

* **G15 -> G16, G17 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(G16,G17)`]**
  The robot first attempts checkpoint transfer; if it fails, alert and reporting are performed.

* **G16 -> AT14, AT15, AT16 [AND][sequential: `AT14;AT15;AT16`]**

* **G17 -> AT17, AT18 [AND][sequential: `AT17;AT18`]**

* **G18 -> AT19, AT20 [AND][sequential: `AT19;AT20`]**
