## Goal Model — Resource Collection and Delivery Mission

### Goals

| Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type | Correct Goal Type | Target Condition / Enquired Information | Relation |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **G1: Fulfill Resource Delivery Request** | Ensure that the required resources are collected from storage and delivered to the requesting agent at the specified location. | `G2;G3;G7;G13` | **Achieve** — the mission wants to bring about a final world state. | **Achieve**  | **Target condition:** required resources are delivered to the requesting agent at the specified location. | Root / AND |
| **G2: Characterize Resource Request** | Obtain the mission information needed to perform the collection and delivery. | `-` | **Query** — the goal retrieves information before action. | **Query** | **Enquired information:** required resources, quantities, requesting agent, delivery location, storage candidates, mission trigger, and current robot availability. | AND child of G1 |
| **G3: Establish Collection Plan** | Define how resources will be collected, including storage order and robot assignment mode. | `G4;G5;G6` | **Achieve** — the desired state is a usable collection plan. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** collection plan exists with ordered storages and assigned robot strategy. | AND child of G1 |
| **G4: Locate Storage Sources** | Identify the storage places where each required resource can be found. | `-` | **Query** — the system enquires where resources are available. | **Query** | **Enquired information:** storage locations containing each required resource. | AND child of G3 |
| **G5: Estimate Storage Priority** | Estimate the priority of each storage visit using waiting time plus robot path cost. | `-` | **Query** — the system calculates information used for planning. | **Query** | **Enquired information:** estimated waiting time, path cost, and resulting visit order for each storage. | AND child of G3 |
| **G6: Assign Robot Execution Mode** | Decide whether the mission will use one robot or multiple robots in parallel. | `FALLBACK(AT1,AT2)` | **Achieve** — the goal is satisfied when an execution mode is assigned. | **Perform** | **Target condition:** one or more robots are assigned to the mission according to the collection plan. | AND child of G3 |
| **G7: Execute Collection Phase** | Perform the collection of resources from the selected storages while handling low-battery conditions. | `G8;G9` | **Achieve** — the target is to have the resources retrieved from storage. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** required resources are collected or collection is safely reassigned. | AND child of G1 |
| **G8: Monitor Collection Battery Safety** | Check whether the assigned robot battery reaches the 10% low-battery threshold during collection. | `-` | **Query** — the system enquires the current battery condition. | **Query** | **Enquired information:** robot battery level during collection and whether it is ≤ 10%. | AND child of G7 |
| **G9: Collect Resources From Storages** | Collect resources normally, or reassign the mission if the collection battery threshold is reached. | `FALLBACK(G10,G12)` | **Achieve** — the target is resource collection or safe continuation by another robot. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** resources are retrieved, or remaining collection is assigned to another robot. | AND child of G7 |
| **G10: Complete Ordered Storage Visits** | Visit storages according to the calculated order and request the required resources. | `AT3;AT4;AT5` | **Achieve** — the target is that all required resources are retrieved from storage. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** all requested resources are retrieved and loaded by the robot. | OR child of G9 |
| **G12: Reassign Collection on Low Battery** | If the robot reaches 10% battery during collection, return it to recharge and assign the mission to another robot. | `AT6;AT7` | **Achieve** — the goal brings about a safe reassignment state. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** low-battery robot is sent to recharge and another robot receives the remaining mission. | OR child of G9 |
| **G13: Execute Delivery Phase** | Deliver all collected resources to the specified location while handling delivery low-battery conditions. | `G14;G15` | **Perform** — the target is successful delivery or safe handoff. | **Perform** | **Target condition:** resources are delivered, or remaining delivery is safely reassigned. | AND child of G1 |
| **G14: Monitor Delivery Battery Safety** | Check whether the assigned robot battery reaches the 30% low-battery threshold during delivery. | `-` | **Query** — the system enquires the current delivery battery condition. | **Query** | **Enquired information:** robot battery level during delivery and whether it is ≤ 30%. | AND child of G13 |
| **G15: Deliver Resources to Requesting Agent** | Deliver resources normally, or handle delivery interruption if battery becomes low. | `FALLBACK(G16,G18)` | **Achieve** — the desired state is delivery completion or safe continuation. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** resources are delivered to the requester, or safely checkpointed and reassigned. | AND child of G13 |
| **G16: Complete Required Delivery Runs** | Make as many trips as necessary to deliver all resources to the specified location. | `AT8;AT9` | **Achieve** — the target is complete delivery of all resources. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** all resources are transported and handed over at the specified location. | OR child of G15 |
| **G18: Handle Delivery Low Battery** | If the robot reaches 30% battery during delivery, secure the resource at a checkpoint and assign the remaining task to another robot. | `G19;G22` | **Achieve** — the goal ensures delivery can continue safely after interruption. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** resource is checkpointed, its position is known, and another robot receives the remaining task. | OR child of G15 |
| **G19: Secure Resource at Checkpoint** | Return the resource to a checkpoint, or trigger failure handling if checkpoint return fails. | `FALLBACK(AT10,G20)` | **Achieve** — the target is safe checkpoint placement or alerting if that cannot be achieved. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** resource is placed at checkpoint, or alert/report is issued after failure. | AND child of G18 |
| **G20: Alert Checkpoint Return Failure** | Trigger an alert and send a report to the sector manager if the robot cannot return the resource to a checkpoint. | `AT11;AT12` | **Achieve** — the goal brings about the failure notification state. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** alert is triggered and failure report is sent to the sector manager. | OR child of G19 |
| **G22: Transfer Remaining Delivery to Replacement Robot** | Inform another robot where the resource is positioned and assign the remaining delivery task. | `AT13;AT14` | **Achieve** — the goal is satisfied when the replacement robot can continue the delivery. | **Achieve** | **Target condition:** replacement robot knows the checkpoint position and has the remaining delivery assignment. | AND child of G18 |

## Tasks

| Name                                                     | Text                                                                                                                                           | Relation         | Location                      | Number of Robots |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------: |
| **AT1: Assign Parallel Robots**                          | Assign multiple robots to parallel collect-deliver tasks when items are required from different storages and parallelism reduces mission time. | OR child of G6   | `mission_control`             |          `[2,n]` |
| **AT2: Assign Single Robot**                             | Assign one robot to execute the collection and delivery mission when parallel execution is not required or not available.                      | OR child of G6   | `mission_control`             |              `1` |
| **AT3: Navigate to Ordered Storage**                     | Move the assigned robot to the next storage according to the order defined by waiting time plus path cost.                                     | AND child of G10 | `storage_places`              |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT4: Send Resource Request Message**                   | Send a message to the storage with the precise specification of the requested resources.                                                       | AND child of G10 | `storage_places`              |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT5: Wait and Load Retrieved Resources**               | Wait until the storage retrieves the resources, then load or receive them for delivery.                                                        | AND child of G10 | `storage_places`              |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT6: Return to Recharging Station**                    | Send the low-battery robot back to the recharging station during collection.                                                                   | AND child of G12 | `recharging_station`          |              `1` |
| **AT7: Assign Collection to Replacement Robot**          | Assign the remaining collection mission to another available robot.                                                                            | AND child of G12 | `mission_control`             |              `1` |
| **AT8: Transport Resources to Delivery Location**        | Move the collected resources to the specified location, making as many runs as necessary.                                                      | AND child of G16 | `specified_delivery_location` |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT9: Handover Resources to Requesting Agent**          | Deliver the resources to the requesting agent at the specified location.                                                                       | AND child of G16 | `specified_delivery_location` |          `[1,n]` |
| **AT10: Return Resource to Checkpoint**                  | Move the resource to a checkpoint when the robot reaches 30% battery during delivery.                                                          | OR child of G19  | `checkpoint`                  |              `1` |
| **AT11: Trigger Failure Alert**                          | Trigger an alert if the robot cannot return the resource to a checkpoint.                                                                      | AND child of G20 | `mission_control`             |              `1` |
| **AT12: Send Failure Report to Sector Manager**          | Send a failure report to the sector manager after unsuccessful checkpoint return.                                                              | AND child of G20 | `sector_manager_station`      |              `1` |
| **AT13: Record Resource Checkpoint Position**            | Register the checkpoint position where the resource is located so the replacement robot can find it.                                           | AND child of G22 | `checkpoint`                  |              `1` |
| **AT14: Assign Remaining Delivery to Replacement Robot** | Assign the remaining delivery task to another robot and provide the checkpoint location.                                                       | AND child of G22 | `mission_control`             |              `1` |

## Logical Relationships

* **G1 -> G2, G3, G7, G13 [AND][sequential: `G2;G3;G7;G13`]**

* **G3 -> G4, G5, G6 [AND][sequential: `G4;G5;G6`]**

* **G6 -> AT1, AT2 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(AT1,AT2)`]**
  Parallel assignment is preferred when multiple items are required from different storages; single-robot assignment is the fallback.

* **G7 -> G8, G9 [AND][sequential: `G8;G9`]**

* **G9 -> G10, G12 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(G10,G12)`]**
  Normal collection is attempted first; if the robot reaches 10% battery, collection is reassigned.

* **G10 -> AT3, AT4, AT5 [AND][sequential: `AT3;AT4;AT5`]**

* **G12 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][sequential: `AT6;AT7`]**

* **G13 -> G14, G15 [AND][sequential: `G14;G15`]**

* **G15 -> G16, G18 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(G16,G18)`]**
  Normal delivery is attempted first; if the robot reaches 30% battery during delivery, checkpoint-based reassignment is activated.

* **G16 -> AT8, AT9 [AND][sequential: `AT8;AT9`]**

* **G18 -> G19, G22 [AND][sequential: `G19;G22`]**

* **G19 -> AT10, G20 [OR][fallback: `FALLBACK(AT10,G20)`]**
  Returning the resource to a checkpoint is attempted first; if it fails, alert and reporting behavior is triggered.

* **G20 -> AT11, AT12 [AND][sequential: `AT11;AT12`]**

* **G22 -> AT13, AT14 [AND][sequential: `AT13;AT14`]**
