**Goal Model – Hospital Food Delivery & Dish‑Retrieval Mission**

| # | Name | Text | Runtime | Goal Type | Target Cond. / Enquired Info | Relation |
|---|------|------|---------|-----------|------------------------------|----------|
| 1 | **G1**: Hospital Service Mission | Deliver requested food to an inpatient and collect all dirty dishes from the patient’s room, while ensuring the correct meal is retrieved and the room door is left in a safe state. | `;` | **Achieve** | “All requested meals are delivered and retrieved correctly; all dirty dishes are collected and disposed; door remains closed.” | **AND** |
| 2 | **G1.1**: Deliver Food Order | Execute the food‑delivery sequence for the current kitchen request. | `FALLBACK(G1.1.1,G1.1.2)` | **Perform** | – | **OR** (fallback) |
| 3 | **G1.1.1**: Deliver to Table | Place the meal directly onto the patient’s bedside table using the robot’s manipulation skill. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
| 4 | **G1.1.2**: Deliver to Tray | Hand the meal over to the patient’s tray, possibly with human or robot cooperation. | `;` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
| 5 | **G1.1.2.0**: Query Patient Retrieval Capability | Check inpatient record and room presence for patient/companion/nurse availability to retrieve the meal. | `-` | **Query** | “Is a human present and able to retrieve the meal?” | **AND** |
| 6 | **G1.1.2.1**: Attempt Transfer or Request Human | If a human can retrieve the meal, transfer it; otherwise ask a human to help. | `FALLBACK(G1.1.2.1.1,G1.1.2.1.2)` | **Perform** | – | **OR** |
| 7 | **G1.1.2.1.1**: Transfer Meal to Patient | Physically hand the meal to the patient or their assistant. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
| 8 | **G1.1.2.1.2**: Request Human Assistance | Ask a nurse, companion or other human to assist with meal handover. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
| 9 | **G1.1.3**: Track Meal Retrieval | Log the delivered meal and confirm it is retrieved by the patient. | `-` | **Achieve** | “All delivered meals are recorded and correctly retrieved.” | **AND** |
|10 | **G1.1.4**: Alert Wrong Meal Retrieval | Detect if a wrong meal is taken and notify staff. | `-` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|11 | **G1.2**: Retrieve Dirty Dishes | Collect and dispose all dirty dishes from the patient’s room. | `;` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|12 | **G1.2.1**: Check for Dishes | Scan the room for any dirty dishes. | `-` | **Query** | “Are there dirty dishes present?” | **AND** |
|13 | **G1.2.2**: Retrieve Dishes | Execute dish retrieval, possibly alone, with a robot partner or a human. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **OR** |
|14 | **G1.2.2.1**: Unassisted Retrieval | Robot retrieves the dish by itself. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|15 | **G1.2.2.2**: Robot Cooperation Retrieval | Two robots cooperate to lift a heavy or large dish. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|16 | **G1.2.2.3**: Human Assistance Retrieval | Request a human to pick up the dish. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|17 | **G1.2.3**: Open Room Door | Open the patient room door, possibly alone or with assistance. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **OR** |
|18 | **G1.2.3.1**: Open Door Alone | Robot uses its own actuator to open the door. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|19 | **G1.2.3.2**: Request Human to Open Door | Ask a nurse or companion to open the door. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|20 | **G1.2.3**: Use Another Robot to Open Door | Another robot opens the door on behalf of the primary robot. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |
|21 | **G1.2.4**: Dispose Dishes | Transport collected dishes to the designated disposal bin. | `#` | **Perform** | – | **AND** |

### Task Model

| 3 | **AT3**: Navigate to Inpatient Room | Robot drives to the specific patient’s room. | AND | patient room | 1 |

| 5 | **AT5**: Query Retrieval Capability | Robot queries the hospital information system for patient/companion/nurse availability. | AND | hospital database | 1 |

|23 | **AT23**: Navigate to Door | Robot moves to the patient room door. | AND | patient room | 1 |

|25 | **AT25**: Notify Human to Open Door | Robot sends a request to a nurse or companion to open the door. | AND | patient room | 1 |
|26 | **AT26**: Wait for Door Opening | Robot waits until the door is confirmed open. | AND | patient room | 1 |
|27 | **AT27**: Request Another Robot Assistance | Robot signals another robot to help open the door. | AND | patient room | 1 |
|28 | **AT28**: Receive Door Opening | Robot acknowledges the other robot has opened the door. | AND | patient room | 1 |
|29 | **AT29**: Carry Dish to Disposal | Robot transports the collected dish to the disposal area. | AND | disposal bin | 1 |

### Justification of Goal Types

| Goal | Type | Rationale |
|------|------|-----------|
| G1 (Hospital Service Mission) | **Achieve** | The mission seeks a specific future state: meals delivered, dishes collected, correct meal retrieval, and safe door closure. |
| G1.1 (Deliver Food Order) | **Perform** | Success depends on executing the delivery sequence; any applicable plan suffices to claim success. |
| G1.1.1, G1.1.2 (Delivery methods) | **Perform** | These are concrete delivery actions that the robot must perform. |
| G1.1.2.0 (Query Patient Retrieval Capability) | **Query** | It simply retrieves information from the patient record; no action is required beyond reading data. |
| G1.1.2.1 (Attempt Transfer or Request Human) | **Perform** | Involves physical actions (handover or request). |
| G1.1.3 (Track Meal Retrieval) | **Achieve** | It ensures a state condition (meal correctly retrieved) is achieved; monitoring and recording are required. |
| G1.1.4 (Alert Wrong Meal Retrieval) | **Perform** | The robot must act by sending an alert when a condition is detected. |
| G1.2 (Retrieve Dirty Dishes) | **Perform** | Execution of dish collection tasks. |
| G1.2.1 (Check for Dishes) | **Query** | Simple presence check; no physical action. |
| G1.2.2 (Retrieve Dishes) | **Perform** | Actual physical retrieval. |
| G1.2.3 (Open Room Door) | **Perform** | Physical action to open door. |
| G1.2.4 (Dispose Dishes) | **Perform** | Physical disposal action. |
