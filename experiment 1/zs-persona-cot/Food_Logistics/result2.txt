## Goal model assumptions

I model the mission as one **food service cycle**: first the robot system fulfills a kitchen delivery request, then it handles dirty-dish retrieval when the room requests or when the service cycle requires it. Fallbacks represent uncertainty about human availability, inpatient ability, companion presence, nurse availability, and robot skill availability.

Fallback preference used here:

`table placement → inpatient tray retrieval → companion tray retrieval → nurse tray retrieval → robot-assisted tray retrieval`

For door opening:

`human-assisted opening → robot-assisted opening`
