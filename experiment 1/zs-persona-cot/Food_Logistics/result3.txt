Below is a **goal model decomposition** for the multi-robot hospital food delivery and dirty-dish retrieval mission.

I model the mission as two event-driven service branches:

1. **Deliver requested meals from kitchen to inpatient room**
2. **Retrieve dirty dishes from inpatient room when requested/needed**
