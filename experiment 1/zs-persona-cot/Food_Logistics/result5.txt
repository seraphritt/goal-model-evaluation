## Assumptions and monitored variables

Main monitored variables: `order_id`, `meal_ids`, `assigned_meal_id`, `patient_id`, `room_id`, `delivery_request`, `patient_can_fetch`, `companion_present`, `companion_can_fetch`, `nurse_available`, `helper_robot_available`, `manipulation_skill_available`, `door_open_support`, `retrieved_meal_id`, `retrieval_time`, `retrieval_location`, `dish_pickup_request`, `human_can_open_door`, `dirty_dishes_location`.

The model uses fallback when alternatives are possible under uncertainty, for example: table placement vs. tray retrieval, inpatient vs. companion vs. nurse vs. robot cooperation, and human door opening vs. robot cooperation.
