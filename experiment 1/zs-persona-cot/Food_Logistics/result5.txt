## Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Food Delivery

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Target Condition / Enquired Info** | **Relation** |
|----------|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| **G1** – *Deliver Food to Inpatient Room* | Achieve that the ordered meal is delivered from the kitchen to the inpatient’s room (either placed on the table or retrieved by the patient). | `G1.1;G1.2;G1.3;G1.4;G1.5` | Achieve | Food is placed on the patient’s table **or** the patient has retrieved the meal and the robot has verified the correct item. | – (root) |
| **G1.1** – *Obtain Delivery Order* | Query the kitchen system for the order details (order‑id, patient‑id, meal items, pickup location). | `-` | Query | Order ID, patient ID, meal list, kitchen location | AND |
| **G1.2** – *Load Meals onto Robot* | Perform the loading of the ordered meals onto the robot’s tray. | `AT1;AT2` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.3** – *Transport Meals to Patient Room* | Transport the loaded meals from the kitchen to the patient’s room. | `#` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.4** – *Deliver Food to Patient* | Deliver the meals to the patient either by placing them on the table or by assisting a retrieval. | `FALLBACK(G1.4.1,G1.4.2)` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.4.1** – *Place Meals on Table* | Use the robot’s manipulation skill to place meals on the patient’s table. | `-` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.4.2** – *Assist Patient Retrieval* | Assist the patient (or companion, nurse, or another robot) to fetch the meal from the tray. | `FALLBACK(G1.4.2.1,G1.4.2.2)` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.4.2.1** – *Query Retrieval Capability* | Check whether the patient can retrieve the meal from the tray (based on patient record and presence of a companion). | `-` | Query | Can the patient retrieve? Companion present? | AND |
| **G1.4.2.2** – *Coordinate Fetch* | Coordinate with the patient/companion to physically retrieve the meal. | `AT8;AT9;AT10;AT11` | Perform | – | AND |
| **G1.5** – *Retrieve Dirty Dishes* | Retrieve dirty dishes from the patient’s room and bring them back to the kitchen. | `AT12;AT13;AT14;AT15` | Perform | – | AND |

## Goal‑Type Justifications

| Goal | Type | Justification |
|------|------|---------------|
| G1 (root) | **Achieve** | The mission’s ultimate objective is to bring the meal from the kitchen to the patient, i.e., a change in the world state. |
| G1.1 | **Query** | The robot must obtain order details before acting; no physical action is performed. |
| G1.2 | **Perform** | Physical loading of meals is executed by the robot. |
| G1.3 | **Perform** | Navigation and door opening are concrete actions. |
| G1.4 | **Perform** | Delivery is a physical act, but it includes a choice (place vs. fetch) handled via fallback. |
| G1.4.1 | **Perform** | Manipulation of the tray to table is a physical task. |
| G1.4.2 | **Perform** | Assisting a human is a physical, cooperative action. |
| G1.4.2.1 | **Query** | Determining if the patient can fetch is an information‑seeking step. |
| G1.4.2.2 | **Perform** | The robot physically helps with retrieval. |
| G1.5 | **Perform** | Dish retrieval is a direct physical operation. |

---

**Note**: All non‑leaf goals have an explicit runtime annotation that captures the execution order or fallback logic, and every goal/task is linked to its parent via an AND/OR relation. This structure supports the required uncertainty handling (e.g., patient retrieval capability) and cooperative behavior (door opening, fetching, dish pickup).
