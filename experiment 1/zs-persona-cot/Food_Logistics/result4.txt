## Goal Model — Food Delivery and Dirty-Dish Retrieval in Hospital MRS

**Notation used:**
`#` = parallel execution; `;` = sequential execution; `FALLBACK(a,b)` = try `a`, otherwise try `b`.
In the tables, **Relation** means the node’s relation to its parent. Parent decompositions are detailed later.

### Goal-type justification

Most goals are **Achieve goals** because the mission requires target world states, such as “meal delivered,” “door opened,” “correct meal retrieved,” and “dirty dishes removed.”
**Query goals** are used where the system must obtain information without necessarily acting, such as checking kitchen orders, inpatient records, companion presence, nurse availability, and pickup signals.
**G12** is a **Perform goal** because the mission explicitly requires tracking and alerting activities; its success depends on performing monitoring/logging/alerting rather than only producing a physical world state.
