**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Food Delivery & Dish Retrieval**

| Name | Text | Runtime | Decomposition | Goal Type | Target Condition / Enquired Info | Relation |
|------|------|---------|---------------|-----------|----------------------------------|----------|
| **G1: Deliver Meals and Clean Up** | Achieve the delivery of all requested meals to the correct inpatient rooms **and** the retrieval of all dirty dishes, while ensuring doors are handled correctly. | `#` | AND | **Achieve** | “All requested meals are delivered to the correct rooms, all requested dirty dishes are retrieved, and all doors are opened/closed appropriately.” | – |
| **G2: Deliver Meal** | Perform the delivery of a meal for a given order. | `;` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G2.1: Query Patient Retrieval Capability** | Query whether the patient can retrieve the meal from the robot’s tray (and whether a companion is present). | `-` | AND | **Query** | “Patient can retrieve from tray? Companion present?” | – |
| **G2.2: Delivery Mode Decision** | Decide between delivering to the table or to the tray based on the patient’s capability. | `FALLBACK(G2.2.1, G2.2.2)` | OR | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G2.2.1: Deliver Meal to Table** | Perform delivery of the meal onto the patient’s table using the special manipulation skill. | `-` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G2.2.2: Deliver Meal to Tray** | Perform delivery of the meal onto the robot’s tray for patient retrieval. | `-` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G3: Retrieve Dirty Dishes** | Perform the retrieval of dirty dishes from the patient room. | `;` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G3.1: Respond to Dish Pickup Request** | Respond when a person in the room signals a request to pick up dishes. | `-` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |
| **G3.2: Retrieve Dishes** | Perform retrieval of dishes, opening doors if necessary, and delivering them to the kitchen. | `-` | AND | **Perform** | – | – |

### Task Model

| Name | Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots |
|------|------|----------|----------|------------------|
| **AT1: Navigate to Kitchen and Load Meals** | Robot navigates to kitchen and loads requested meal(s) onto the tray. | AND (under G2.2) | kitchen | 1 |
| **AT2: Deliver Meal to Table** | Robot places the meal onto the patient’s table using the special manipulation skill. | AND (under G2.2.1) | room | 1 |
| **AT3: Deliver Meal to Tray** | Robot places the meal onto its tray for patient retrieval. | AND (under G2.2.2) | room | 1 |
| **AT4: Await Retrieval** | Robot waits for the patient, companion, nurse, or another robot to retrieve the meal from the tray. | AND (under G2.2.2) | room | 1 |
| **AT5: Validate Meal** | Robot scans the retrieved meal to verify it matches the order. | AND (under G2.2.2) | robot | 1 |
| **AT6: Log Retrieval** | Robot logs the time, location, and patient identifier for each retrieved meal. | AND (under G2.2.2) | robot | 1 |
| **AT7: Alert Wrong Meal** | Robot alerts the system and staff if a wrong meal has been retrieved. | AND (under G2.2.2) | robot | 1 |
| **AT8: Wait for Dish Request** | Robot waits for a person in the room to signal a request to pick up dishes. | AND (under G3.1) | robot | 1 |
| **AT9: Open Door** | Robot cooperates with a human or another robot to open the room door if needed. | AND (under G3.2) | room | [1,2] |
| **AT10: Pick Up Dishes** | Robot (or two robots) picks up dirty dishes from the room. | AND (under G3.2) | room | [1,2] |
| **AT11: Deliver Dishes to Kitchen** | Robot transports the collected dishes back to the kitchen for washing. | AND (under G3.2) | kitchen | 1 |

### Justification of Goal Types  

| Goal | Type | Reasoning |
|------|------|-----------|
| **G1** | Achieve | Specifies a **world state** the system must bring about (all meals delivered, dishes cleaned, doors handled). |
| **G2, G2.2, G2.2.1, G2.2.2, G3, G3.1, G3.2** | Perform | Each goal explicitly **describes an action** (delivery, retrieval, door opening) that must be carried out. |
| **G2.1** | Query | The goal only **retrieves information** (patient’s ability to fetch a tray) and does not directly trigger physical actions. |
