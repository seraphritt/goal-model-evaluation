Below is an explicit **goal-model decomposition** for the mission. I treat the mission as a **hospital food-service cycle** with two major service responsibilities: **deliver meals** and **retrieve dirty dishes**.

## 1. Modeling rationale

The mission contains three main operational concerns:

1. **Food delivery is request-driven**
   The mission starts only when the kitchen issues a delivery request. Therefore, the model begins with querying and capturing the kitchen order.

2. **Delivery has alternative strategies**
   A meal can be transferred either by:

   * placing it on the room table with a manipulation-capable robot, or
   * letting someone retrieve it from the robot tray.

   Since the first option requires a special robot capability, the model uses a **fallback**: try autonomous/table delivery when possible, otherwise use tray retrieval with cooperation.

3. **Uncertainty must be handled at runtime**
   The availability and capability of the inpatient, companion, nurse, or another robot is uncertain. Therefore, the model includes explicit **query goals** and **fallback relationships** to choose an available retriever or door-opening helper.

# 5. Logical relationships

## Main mission

**G1 -> G2, G3, G7, G22 [AND][sequential: `G2;G3;G7;G22`]**

* **Justification**: The mission starts with a kitchen request, then requires contextual assessment, then food delivery, and finally dirty-dish retrieval. These are not alternatives; they are required phases of the full service.

---

## Kitchen request handling

**G2 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential: `AT1;AT2`]**

* **Justification**: The system must first receive the kitchen request and then obtain the details of that request.

---

## Context assessment under uncertainty

**G3 -> G4, G5, G6 [AND][parallel: `G4#G5#G6`]**

* **Justification**: Patient capability, human availability, and robot capability are all needed to select the delivery strategy. They can be queried in parallel.

**G4 -> AT3 [AND]**

* **Justification**: The inpatient record is the source of information about whether the inpatient can retrieve food.

**G5 -> AT4, AT5 [AND][parallel: `AT4#AT5`]**

* **Justification**: Companion presence and nurse availability are separate sources of possible human cooperation.

**G6 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][parallel: `AT6#AT7`]**

* **Justification**: The system needs to know both whether manipulation is available and whether another robot can cooperate.

---

## Meal delivery

**G7 -> G8, G9, G13, G21 [AND][sequential: `G8;G9;G13;G21`]**

* **Justification**: Meals must be prepared, transported, transferred, and then recorded as delivered.

**G8 -> AT8, AT9 [AND][sequential: `AT8;AT9`]**

* **Justification**: The robot must load meals before mapping them to inpatients and tray positions.

* **Justification**: The robot must first navigate to the room and then handle door access if needed.

* **Justification**: Human door opening requires the robot to request and coordinate the action.

* **Justification**: Robot door opening requires coordination between the delivery robot and support robot.

**G13 -> G14, G15 [AND][fallback: `FALLBACK(G14,G15)`]**

**G15 -> G16, AT20, AT21, AT22, AT23 [AND][sequential: `G16;AT20;AT21;AT22;AT23`]**
