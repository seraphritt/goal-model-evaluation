### Key Design Rationale

* **Sequence vs. Alternative** – The mission has a clear start–end flow (door → decision → delivery → dish cleanup), hence the root uses a sequential runtime. Alternative delivery and retrieval strategies are captured with OR decompositions.
* **Fallback** – The “patient retrieval” vs. “table delivery” is a fallback: the robot first tries to hand the meal to the patient; if the patient cannot retrieve, it falls back to table delivery. This is encoded with `FALLBACK(G3,G4)` inside G1.
* **Uncertainty Handling** – Patient capability and companion presence are uncertain; the robot explicitly queries the hospital system and sensors, reflected in the Query goals.
* **Cooperation** – Door opening and dish retrieval may require cooperation; OR nodes capture the possible cooperation modes.
* **Task Granularity** – Each leaf goal is realized by one or more concrete tasks, each with a clear location and robot count, making the model actionable for multi‑robot planners.
