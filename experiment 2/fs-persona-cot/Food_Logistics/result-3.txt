Below is a **goal-model-oriented decomposition** of the mission. I use the mission as two major service flows: **food delivery** and **dirty-dish retrieval**. The model also captures uncertainty, fallback alternatives, cooperation with humans/robots, and verification of correct meal retrieval.

## 1. Modeling interpretation

The mission contains two main operational objectives:

1. **Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room**

   * Triggered by a kitchen delivery request.
   * Done order-by-order.
   * The robot may carry multiple meals.
   * The meal can be transferred by:

     * placing it on the room table, requiring manipulation skill; or
     * having someone/another robot fetch it from the tray.
   * Tray retrieval requires cooperation.
   * The robot must indicate the correct meal, track retrieval, and alert if the wrong meal is retrieved.

2. **Retrieve dirty dishes from the room**

   * Can be requested by someone in the room.
   * Door opening may require cooperation.
   * Dish pickup may be unassisted, robot-robot cooperative, or robot-human cooperative.

Because several alternatives depend on availability and uncertainty, I use **FALLBACK** when the system should try one option first and only try another if the first fails. I use **AND** when all children are required to satisfy the parent, and **OR/FALLBACK-like alternatives** when one of several strategies is sufficient.
