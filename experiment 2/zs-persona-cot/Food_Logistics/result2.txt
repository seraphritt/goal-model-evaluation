# 1. Design Reasoning Summary

The mission has two main service responsibilities:

1. **Deliver food from the kitchen to an inpatient room**

   * Triggered by **kitchen delivery requests**.
   * Done **order by order**.
   * May involve multiple meals.
   * The food can be delivered by:

     * placing it on the room table, requiring a manipulation-capable robot; or
     * letting someone fetch it from the robot tray.

2. **Retrieve dirty dishes from the inpatient room**

   * May be triggered by someone in the room calling a robot.
   * Retrieval may be:

     * unassisted by one robot;
     * cooperative between two robots;
     * cooperative between robot and human.

The mission also includes uncertainty and cooperation:

* Whether the inpatient, companion, or nurse can retrieve food is uncertain.
* The robot must query or infer this information from inpatient records and room context.
* Door opening may require a human or another robot.
* If someone retrieves food from the tray, the robot must indicate the correct meal, track retrieval time/location, and alert if the wrong meal is taken.
