### Task Model

| # | Task ID & TITLE | Text | Relation | Location | Number of Robots | Justification |
|---|-----------------|------|----------|----------|------------------|---------------|
| 1 | **AT1_PickMeal** | Robot moves to kitchen and picks up the ordered meal. | AND | Kitchen | 1 | The robot must physically fetch the meal before any delivery. |
| 2 | **AT2_DoorDelivery** | Robot opens the patient room door for direct or tray delivery. | AND | Room door | [1,2] | Door opening may need human help or another robot; thus a flexible robot count. |
| 3 | **AT3_DeliverTable** | Robot places the meal on the patient’s table. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Direct delivery requires the robot to physically place the meal. |
| 4 | **AT4_DeliverTray** | Robot places the meal on a tray for the patient/assistant to retrieve. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Tray delivery is a distinct physical action from table delivery. |
| 5 | **AT5_QueryRecord** | Robot queries the hospital database for the patient’s fetch capability. | AND | Hospital database | 1 | Needed before deciding fetch agent. |
| 6 | **AT6_WaitPatientFetch** | Robot waits for the patient to pick up the tray. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Directly supports patient fetch option. |
| 7 | **AT7_WaitCompanionFetch** | Robot waits for a companion visitor to pick up the tray. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Supports companion fetch option. |
| 8 | **AT8_WaitNurseFetch** | Robot waits for a nurse to pick up the tray. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Supports nurse fetch option. |
| 9 | **AT9_WaitRobotFetch** | Robot waits for another robot to pick up the tray. | AND | Patient room | [1,2] | Requires at least one other robot; flexible count allows coordination. |
|10 | **AT10_UpdateTracking** | Update internal state with meal retrieved timestamp and location. | AND | Robot internal | 1 | Keeps track of which meal was retrieved where, required for alerting on wrong meal. |
|11 | **AT11_AlertWrongMeal** | Notify patient if retrieved meal is incorrect. | AND | Patient room | 1 | Reactive action triggered after meal retrieval. |
|12 | **AT12_RetrieveDishes** | Robot picks up dirty dishes from the patient room. | AND | Patient room | [1,2] | Dish retrieval may be done unassisted or with cooperation. |
|13 | **AT13_DoorDish** | Robot opens the patient room door for dish retrieval. | AND | Room door | [1,2] | Needed for both unassisted and assisted dish retrieval. |

### Logical Relationships

- **G0 → G1, G5** `[AND][#]`  
  *Justification:* The system must perform both food delivery and dish retrieval concurrently.

- **G1 → G1.0, G1.1** `[AND][;]`  
  *Justification:* Picking up the meal must precede the decision on delivery method.

- **G1.1 → G1.1.1, G1.1.2** `[OR][FALLBACK(G1.1.1, G1.1.2)]`  
  *Justification:* Either direct delivery or tray delivery can satisfy the delivery requirement; fallback represents the OR.

- **G1.1.1 → AT2_DoorDelivery, AT3_DeliverTable** `[AND][;]`  
  *Justification:* The robot must open the door and then place the meal on the table in order.

- **G1.1.2 → AT2_DoorDelivery, AT4_DeliverTray, AT5_QueryRecord, G1.1.2.3, AT10_UpdateTracking, AT11_AlertWrongMeal** `[AND][;]`  
  *Justification:* All these steps must occur sequentially for tray delivery.

- **G1.1.2.3 → AT6_WaitPatientFetch, AT7_WaitCompanionFetch, AT8_WaitNurseFetch, AT9_WaitRobotFetch** `[OR][FALLBACK(...)]`  
  *Justification:* Any of the fetch agents can retrieve the tray; the robot tries them in priority order.

- **G5 → AT12_RetrieveDishes, AT13_DoorDish** `[AND][;]`  
  *Justification:* The robot must open the door before picking up dishes.
