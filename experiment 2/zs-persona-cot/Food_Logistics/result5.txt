## 1. Modeling rationale

I decomposed the mission into **three major sequential phases**:

1. **Prepare the food delivery order**: receive the kitchen request, identify the inpatient/room/meals, assess who can cooperate, and load/map meals.
2. **Deliver the food to the inpatient room**: reach the room, open the door, transfer the meal either by table placement or tray fetching, and record the delivery.
3. **Retrieve dirty dishes**: receive a pickup request, access the room, collect dishes using one of the available cooperation modes, and return the dishes.

Uncertainty is modeled through **Query Goals** and **FALLBACK relations**. For example, if the inpatient cannot retrieve the meal, the system can try a companion/nurse, and if that fails, another robot.

# 2. Goal model hierarchy

```text
G1 Fulfill inpatient meal logistics
├── G2 Prepare delivery order
│   ├── G3 Obtain kitchen delivery request
│   ├── G4 Assess retrieval cooperation context
│   └── G5 Prepare meals for transport
├── G6 Deliver food to inpatient room
│   ├── G7 Reach and enter room
│   │   └── G8 Open room door
│   │       ├── G9 Human-assisted door opening
│   │       └── G10 Robot-assisted door opening
│   ├── G11 Transfer food to recipient
│   │   ├── G12 Place food on room table
│   │   └── G13 Fetch food from robot tray
│   │       ├── G14 Obtain retriever cooperation
│   │       ├── G15 Guide correct meal selection
│   │       └── G16 Control retrieval outcome
│   │           ├── G17 Confirm correct meal retrieval
│   │           └── G18 Alert wrong meal retrieval
│   └── G19 Confirm delivery record
└── G20 Retrieve dirty dishes
    ├── G21 Receive dish pickup context
    ├── G22 Access room for dish pickup
    │   ├── G23 Human-assisted pickup door opening
    │   └── G24 Robot-assisted pickup door opening
    ├── G25 Collect dirty dishes
    │   ├── G26 Unassisted dish collection
    │   ├── G27 Two-robot dish collection
    │   └── G28 Human-robot dish collection
    └── G29 Return dirty dishes
```

# 6. Logical relationships and justifications

* **G1 -> G2, G6, G20 [AND][sequential: `G2 ; G6 ; G20`]**
  **Justification**: The overall mission is not only to deliver food but also to support dish retrieval. Preparation must happen before delivery, and dirty dishes are retrieved after the meal service context exists.

* **G2 -> G3, G4, G5 [AND][`(G3 # G4) ; G5`]**
  **Justification**: The robot must know the order and the cooperation context before loading/mapping meals. Order data and patient/cooperator information can be obtained in parallel.

* **G3 -> AT1, AT2 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: The request must first be read, then its operational data must be extracted.

* **G4 -> AT3, AT4, AT5 [AND][`(AT3 # AT4) ; AT5`]**
  **Justification**: The inpatient record and room presence information are both needed. The uncertainty annotation depends on both.

* **G5 -> AT6, AT7 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: Meals must be physically loaded before the robot can map each meal to a tray position and patient.

* **G6 -> G7, G11, G19 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: The robot must reach/access the room, transfer the meal, and then record the delivery.

* **G7 -> AT8, G8 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: The robot first navigates to the door, then needs the door opened to complete access.

* **G8 -> G9, G10 [OR][`FALLBACK(G9,G10)`]**
  **Justification**: The description allows door opening either with a human or another robot. One successful method is enough.

* **G11 -> G12, G13 [OR][`FALLBACK(G12,G13)`]**
  **Justification**: Food can be delivered onto the table or fetched from the tray. These are alternative transfer strategies.

* **G13 -> G14, G15, G16 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: Tray fetching requires a retriever, correct meal indication, and monitoring of the retrieval result.

* **G14 -> AT15, AT16, AT17 [OR][`FALLBACK(AT15,FALLBACK(AT16,AT17))`]**
  **Justification**: The retriever can be the inpatient, a companion/nurse, or another robot. Because each may be unavailable, fallback is appropriate.

* **G16 -> G17, G18 [OR][`FALLBACK(G17,G18)`]**
  **Justification**: The normal outcome is correct retrieval. If that fails because the wrong meal is retrieved, the robot must alert.

* **G20 -> G21, G22, G25, G29 [AND][sequential]**
  **Justification**: Dirty dish retrieval requires receiving the pickup context, accessing the room, collecting dishes, and returning them.

* **G22 -> G23, G24 [OR][`FALLBACK(G23,G24)`]**
  **Justification**: During pickup, the door may be opened by a human or through robot cooperation.

* **G25 -> G26, G27, G28 [OR][`FALLBACK(G26,FALLBACK(G27,G28))`]**
  **Justification**: The mission explicitly says dish retrieval can occur unassisted, with two robots, or with robots and a human. Only one successful mode is needed.

# 7. Main monitored variables

| `order_id`               | Kitchen delivery request identifier.                                 |
| `patient_id`             | Inpatient receiving the meal.                                        |

| `meal_ids`               | Meals carried by the robot.                                          |
