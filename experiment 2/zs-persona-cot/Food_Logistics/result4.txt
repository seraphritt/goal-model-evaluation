#### Justification Summary
* The top‑level **Achieve** goal (G1) captures the mission’s final state (food delivered & dishes cleaned) and sequences the prerequisite sub‑activities.
* **Perform** goals (G2, G3, G4, G5, G7, G8) represent concrete robot actions that must be carried out, whereas **Query** (G6) is a data‑gathering step that informs the subsequent choice of delivery method.
* OR decompositions (fallbacks) model the inherent uncertainty and optional cooperation: the robot can open a door alone or with help; it can retrieve dishes alone or with another robot; it can deliver food onto the table or hand it to a patient depending on patient capability.
* Each task is a leaf node with clear *Location* (hospital database, patient room, robot internal) and *Number of Robots* (1 or 2), reflecting the actual execution constraints. The justification for each attribute is explicitly linked to the mission description (e.g., “robot must read patient record” → 1 robot, “retrieve dishes with two robots” → 2 robots).
