## 1. Design rationale

The mission has two major service objectives: **deliver the correct meal** and **retrieve dirty dishes**. The delivery is request-driven, so the model starts with processing the kitchen request. Because the delivery mode depends on uncertain information about patient/companion/nurse availability, those checks are modeled as **Query Goals**. The actual delivery alternatives are modeled with **OR/FALLBACK**, because placing the food on the table, human tray retrieval, or robot tray retrieval are alternative ways to satisfy the same handover objective.

Dish retrieval is also modeled with alternatives: the robot may retrieve dishes alone, with another robot, or with human cooperation. Door opening appears twice because it is relevant both to delivery and dish pickup, and it can be achieved through cooperation with either a human or another robot.
