**Goal Model – Multi‑Robot Resource Delivery Mission**

| **Name** | **Text** | **Runtime** | **Goal Type** | **Target Condition / Queried Property** | **Relation** | **Justification** |
|----------|----------|-------------|----------------|----------------------------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **G1: Deliver Mission** | Achieve delivery of all requested resources to the specified destination. | `;` (sequential) | **Achieve** | `Resources are delivered to the destination.` | – (root) | The mission’s ultimate objective is to bring the required goods to the requesting agent; therefore it is an *achieve* goal. |
| **G2: Collection Phase** | Perform collection of resources from storage locations as per the ordering. | `;` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G1) | Collection is a mandatory prerequisite for delivery, so it must be completed before the delivery phase. |
| **G2.0: Query Storage Info** | Obtain storage locations, waiting times, and path lengths to plan the collection order. | `-` | **Query** | `StorageLocations, WaitingTimes, PathLengths` | AND (child of G2) | Ordering depends on waiting‑time and path‑length estimates, so this information must be retrieved first. |
| **G2.4: Battery Handling** | Check battery level and handle low‑battery scenarios during collection. | `FALLBACK(G2.4.2, G2.4.1)` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G2) | Battery status dictates whether the robot continues or must recharge and reassign; a fallback captures the two possible branches. |
| **G2.4.1: Battery Low Handling** | Return to recharging station and reassign mission when battery below threshold. | `;` | **Perform** | – | OR (child of G2.4) | Executed only when the battery check fails, therefore a separate branch in the fallback. |
| **G2.4.1.1: Return to Recharge** | Navigate robot to recharging station to restore battery. | `-` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G2.4.1) | Recharge must happen before any further actions. |
| **G2.4.1.2: Assign Mission** | Assign remaining tasks to another robot. | `-` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G2.4.1) | After recharging, the original robot must hand over the remaining work. |
| **G2.4.2: Battery OK Check** | Confirm battery level is sufficient to continue collection. | `-` | **Perform** | – | OR (child of G2.4) | If the check succeeds, the robot proceeds with the remaining collection steps. |
| **G3: Delivery Phase** | Transport collected resources to the destination location. | `;` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G1) | Delivery is the second mandatory phase following collection. |
| **G3.3: Delivery Battery Handling** | Check battery during delivery and handle low‑battery. | `FALLBACK(G3.3.2, G3.3.1)` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G3) | Similar to collection, battery level during delivery determines whether the robot continues or must drop the resource. |
| **G3.3.1: Delivery Battery Low Handling** | Return resource to checkpoint and reassign mission if battery falls below threshold. | `;` | **Perform** | – | OR (child of G3.3) | Executed when the battery check fails. |
| **G3.3.1.1: Return Resource to Checkpoint** | Place resource at checkpoint to preserve it during battery recovery. | `-` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G3.3.1) | Ensures the resource is safe before reassigning. |
| **G3.3.1.2: Assign Mission** | Assign remaining delivery tasks to another robot. | `-` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G3.3.1) | The original robot must hand over the task after low‑battery handling. |
| **G3.3.1.3: Trigger Alert** | Trigger an alert to the sector manager if failure occurs during checkpoint transfer. | `-` | **Perform** | – | AND (child of G3.3.1) | Required by the failure‑handling protocol. |
| **G3.3.2: Delivery Battery OK Check** | Confirm battery level is sufficient to continue delivery. | `-` | **Perform** | – | OR (child of G3.3) | If the battery is sufficient, the robot continues with the remaining delivery steps. |
| **G12: Parallel Assignment for Multi‑Item Mission** | Assign multiple robots to parallel collect‑deliver tasks for items from different storages. | `-` | **Perform** | – | OR (child of G1) | Optional only when the request contains multiple items; otherwise the single‑robot workflow suffices. |

### Tasks

| **Name** | **Text** | **Relation** | **Location** | **Number of Robots** | **Justification** |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **AT1: Navigate to Storage** | Robot moves to the specified storage location to collect the resource. | AND (child of G2) | `Storage_X` | `1` | A single robot performs the navigation; the action is deterministic and isolated. |
| **AT2: Send Request Message** | Robot sends a request to the storage with exact resource specification. | AND (child of G2) | `Storage_X` | `1` | Communication is performed by the same robot that will retrieve the item. |
| **AT3: Wait for Retrieval** | Robot waits until the storage completes the retrieval of the resource. | AND (child of G2) | `Storage_X` | `1` | The robot must pause until the storage indicates completion. |
| **AT4: Check Battery Level** | Robot queries its battery status. | OR (child of G2.4.2) | Current location | `1` | Battery status determines whether collection can continue. |
| **AT5: Return to Recharge Station** | Robot navigates to the charging station to recharge. | AND (child of G2.4.1) | `ChargingStation` | `1` | Recharging is required when battery falls below 10 %. |
| **AT6: Assign Mission to Another Robot** | Robot hands over the remaining tasks to an available robot. | AND (children of G2.4.1 & G3.3.1) | `ChargingStation` / `Checkpoint_X` (depends on context) | `1` | Reassignment is essential after low‑battery handling. |
| **AT7: Carry Resource to Checkpoint** | Robot moves the resource to a checkpoint during delivery. | AND (child of G3) | `Checkpoint_X` | `1` | Checkpoints provide a temporary hold‑point for the resource. |
| **AT8: Deliver Resource to Destination** | Robot transports the resource from the checkpoint to the final destination. | AND (child of G3) | `Destination_X` | `1` | Final delivery step. |
| **AT9: Check Battery During Delivery** | Robot checks its battery status while en route to destination. | OR (child of G3.3.2) | Current location | `1` | Determines if the robot can continue delivery. |
| **AT10: Return Resource to Checkpoint Due to Low Battery** | Robot places the resource back at the checkpoint when battery dips below 30 %. | AND (child of G3.3.1) | `Checkpoint_X` | `1` | Prevents loss of resource during a low‑battery scenario. |
| **AT11: Trigger Alert to Sector Manager** | Robot sends an alert if it fails to return the resource to the checkpoint. | AND (child of G3.3.1) | `Base` | `1` | Failure handling requires notification to the sector manager. |
| **AT12: Parallel Assignment for Multi‑Item Mission** | Robot controller assigns multiple robots to parallel collect‑deliver tasks. | OR (child of G12) | `MissionControl` | `[1,N]` | Multiple robots are employed only when multiple items are requested; the number is variable. |

### Logical Relationships

- **G1 → G2, G3, G12**  
  **Relation:** OR (parallel)  
  **Justification:** The mission can be executed by a single robot following the standard collection → delivery flow (G2 → G3), or by multiple robots executing parallel collect‑deliver tasks when several items are required (G12). Both satisfy the overall objective, hence an OR relation.

- **G2 → G2.0, AT1, AT2, AT3, G2.4**  
  **Relation:** AND (sequential `;`)  
  **Justification:** The collection phase must first gather necessary information (G2.0), then navigate (AT1), request (AT2), wait (AT3), and finally check battery status (G2.4). Each step depends on the previous one, hence sequential AND.

- **G2.4 → G2.4.1, G2.4.2**  
  **Relation:** OR (fallback `FALLBACK(G2.4.2, G2.4.1)`)  
  **Justification:** Battery status leads either to continuation (`G2.4.2`) or to low‑battery handling (`G2.4.1`); the fallback captures the two exclusive branches.

- **G2.4.1 → AT5, AT6**  
  **Relation:** AND (`;`)  
  **Justification:** Recharging must complete before the mission can be reassigned.

- **G3 → AT7, AT8, G3.3**  
  **Relation:** AND (sequential `;`)  
  **Justification:** The robot must first carry the resource to a checkpoint (AT7), then deliver it (AT8), and finally monitor battery during the remaining delivery (G3.3).

- **G3.3 → G3.3.1, G3.3.2**  
  **Relation:** OR (fallback `FALLBACK(G3.3.2, G3.3.1)`)  
  **Justification:** Depending on the battery check, the robot either continues (`G3.3.2`) or must handle a low‑battery situation (`G3.3.1`).

- **G3.3.1 → AT10, AT6, AT11**  
  **Relation:** AND (`;`)  
  **Justification:** The resource must be returned to a checkpoint, the mission reassigned, and an alert triggered if the return fails.

- **G12 → AT12**  
  **Relation:** AND (`-`)  
  **Justification:** Parallel assignment is a single action performed by the mission controller; no further sub‑decomposition.
